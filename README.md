# Elseworlds-Adam
Story files for Elseworlds Story – Adam: chapters, characters, worldbuilding, and notes.

## Summary
Elseworlds: Adam is a trauma‑intimate, character‑driven contemporary drama that follows Jane McFly and Dr. Adam Carter as they navigate grief, identity, memory, and chosen family. This repository is a living workspace for the manuscript: draft chapters, character profiles, continuity notes, and a story bible that collects timelines, rules, and thematic guidance.

## What you'll find here
- Chapters/ — The draft manuscript, organized as ~80 numbered Markdown chapter files (read in numeric order for the narrative flow).
- Characters/ — Individual character files, relationship maps, and a central main-characters note for quick reference.
- Worldbuilding/ — Story Bible, Master Timeline, continuity rules, tone & style guidance.

## Themes & tone
- Survival vs. living; grief & rebirth
- Identity, memory, and DID portrayed with trauma‑informed realism
- Intimate, character‑focused prose with slow emotional beats and domestic grounding

## How to use this repo
- Read the Chapters folder in numeric order to follow the story arc.
- Consult Worldbuilding/Story Bible.md and Master Timeline.md for continuity, character arcs, and rules about memory/integration.
- Use Characters/ for quick reference on relationships, motivations, and details that should remain consistent across drafts.

## Status
Draft manuscript with ~80 chapters; story bible and timelines are actively maintained and intended to grow as the narrative evolves.

## Notes
This repository is structured as a personal writing workspace; create issues or pull requests if you'd like to suggest edits to chapters, character pages, or the story bible.
