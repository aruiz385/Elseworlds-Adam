# Chapter 63: Questions


Adam looked between Jane and Andrew.


Then back toward Kirby's room.


The hallway felt heavy.


Like everyone was waiting for someone else to make the impossible decision.


Adam finally sighed.


"I'm going back in."


Jane immediately looked up.


"What?"


"I want to finish the conversation."


Another.


"I want to make sure he's okay."


Another.


"And answer whatever questions he has."


Jane tightened her hold on Lizzy.


"Adam."


Another.


"Are you sure that's a good idea?"


Adam's expression softened.


He understood what she was really asking.


What she was worried about.


Not Kirby.


Him.


"Jane."


Another.


"It's fine."


Another.


"I want to help him."


Another.


"I look at him and I see myself."


The words were quiet.


Honest.


"He's confused."


Another.


"Scott just explained that he lost eight years of his life."


Another.


"It has to be terrifying."


Jane looked away.


Because she knew he was right.


Adam turned toward Andrew.


"I know you want to tell him the truth."


Another.


"And honestly?"


Another.


"I appreciate it."


Another.


"I know you're doing it because you care about Jane."


Another.


"And because you care about me."


Another.


"But I still think Scott's right."


Andrew folded his arms.


Listening.


Adam continued.


"I don't think dropping everything on him at once is the answer."


Another.


"I think he needs support."


Another.


"I think he needs time."


Another.


"And I think easing him into reality is still the best approach."


The silence stretched.


Then Adam looked at Jane.


Really looked at her.


Her red eyes.


The exhaustion.


The heartbreak.


The determination.


"Jane."


Another.


"I know you're strong."


Another.


"Please hold on a little longer."


Jane looked down at Lizzy.


Then nodded.


Slowly.


Andrew looked unconvinced.


Completely unconvinced.


And Adam knew it.


The second Jane looked away Andrew stepped toward him.


Pulling him a little farther down the hallway.


Far enough that Jane couldn't hear.


Both of them looked back toward her.


Jane sat in a chair.


Holding Lizzy tightly.


Almost protectively.


As if she was afraid someone might take her away.


Andrew's expression hardened.


"I know Jane."


Another.


"I really know her."


Another.


"She's not okay."


Adam nodded.


"I know."


"No."


Andrew shook his head.


"You don't."


Another.


"We can't keep pushing her like this."


Another.


"Look at her."


Adam did.


And his chest immediately tightened.


Because he saw it too.


The hurt.


The exhaustion.


The impossible position she had been forced into.


But he also knew something Andrew didn't.


Or maybe something Andrew couldn't see right now.


"Andrew."


Another.


"I know Jane too."


Another.


"I know how strong she is."


Another.


"And I know that if something goes wrong with Kirby's recovery..."


Another.


"If she feels like she gave up on him..."


Another.


"She'll never forgive herself."


Andrew looked away.


Because he knew Adam was right.


Even if he hated it.


Adam sighed.


"I trust her."


Another.


"I trust that she can do this."


Andrew stared at Jane for another moment.


Then nodded.


Reluctantly.


"Okay."


Adam walked back toward her.


Kneeling in front of her.


"Hey."


Jane looked up.


"Hey."


"Do you want to come back with me?"


Another.


"Kirby knows Lizzy is my daughter."


Another.


"You don't have to."


Another.


"But I think it'll help."


Jane swallowed.


Hard.


Adam reached for her hand.


"You are the toughest woman I know."


Another.


"You can do this."


Another.


"I have complete faith in you."


A small laugh escaped through her tears.


"You do?"


Adam smiled.


"Jane."


Another.


"If you can deal with me and my DID..."


Another.


"You can help your husband recover."


Another.


"So we can sort all of this out sooner rather than later."


The word husband twisted painfully between them.


Neither acknowledged it.


Jane looked at him.


"And are you okay with this?"


Adam didn't answer immediately.


Instead he wrapped an arm around her and Lizzy.


Holding both of them close.


"Jane."


Another.


"You don't have to worry about me."


Another.


"I just want to help him."


Another.


"But if you tell me you're done..."


Another.


"If you tell me you can't do this anymore..."


Another.


"I'll walk into that room myself and tell Kirby everything."


Jane stared at him.


Adam's voice softened.


"Or I won't stop Andrew from doing it."


Another.


"Because he absolutely will."


That earned the tiniest smile from Andrew.


Only because it was true.


Jane closed her eyes.


Then nodded.


"You're right."


Another.


"We need to help him."


Another.


"I'll do whatever you need me to do."


Adam kissed her forehead.


Then stood.


Together they walked back toward Kirby's room.


Andrew stayed behind.


Watching.


Ready to intervene if necessary.


The moment they walked inside, Kirby looked up.


Immediately finding Jane.


Then Lizzy.


Then Adam.


"Hey, Kirby."


Adam smiled.


"I'm back."


Another.


"Jane's going to hold Lizzy while we finish our conversation."


Another.


"If that's okay."


Kirby nodded.


More than okay.


Jane sat down beside the bed.


Lizzy sleeping peacefully against her shoulder.


She focused entirely on her daughter.


Because she knew if she looked at Adam for too long...


She would give everything away.


Kirby watched her.


And something about the sight warmed him immediately.


The way she held the baby.


The way she smiled down at her.


The way she looked peaceful for the first time in days.


It settled something in his chest.


Adam noticed.


And part of him felt awful.


But he pushed the feeling aside.


He was here for a reason.


"So."


Adam sat down again.


"I wanted to check in."


Another.


"See if you had any questions."


Kirby looked at him.


Thoughtful.


Then finally asked:


"How do you do it?"


Adam blinked.


"Do what?"


Kirby looked toward Lizzy.


Then back at him.


"Be a dad."


Another.


"And deal with all of..."


He gestured vaguely.


"...that."


Adam smiled immediately.


Because that question was easy.


"Honestly?"


Another.


"I love being Lizzy's dad."


Another.


"She might be the best thing that's ever happened to me."


Another.


"She's the reason I go to therapy."


Kirby looked surprised.


Adam laughed.


"It's true."


Another.


"Right now I'm doing integration therapy."


Another.


"I'm trying to recover memories."


Another.


"The last time I lost three years..."


Another.


"Lizzy was conceived during that period."


Kirby stared.


Adam shrugged.


"So..."


Another.


"It's complicated."


That earned a smile.


"What about her mom?"


Kirby asked quietly.


Another.


"That must have been hard."


For a moment Adam looked toward Jane.


Just briefly.


"Yeah."


Another.


"It was."


His voice softened.


"Her mom is one of the toughest people I know."


Another.


"She's the kind of person who loves deeply."


Another.


"And fights for the people she cares about."


Jane looked down immediately.


Focusing on Lizzy.


Adam continued.


"But for a long time she looked at me and didn't see the person she loved."


Another.


"She saw a stranger."


Another.


"And honestly?"


Another.


"I felt like a stranger in my own life too."


The room grew quiet.


"It wasn't easy."


Another.


"There was a lot of guilt."


Another.


"A lot of therapy."


Another.


"Seriously."


Another.


"A lot of therapy."


That earned a laugh from Kirby.


Adam smiled.


"But we're trying."


Another.


"We work hard."


Another.


"And mostly..."


Another.


"We love our daughter enough to keep trying."


Without meaning to, Jane looked up.


Just for a second.


Just long enough.


Adam met her eyes.


And the love there was impossible to miss.


Jane immediately looked away.


But Kirby saw it.


He didn't say anything.


Not yet.


Instead he asked:


"Why are you helping me?"


Adam thought about that.


Then smiled.


"Because you're Andrew's brother."


Another.


"And I care about him."


Another.


"I had a brother too."


For a moment the smile grew sad.


"My brother died in a car accident."


Another.


"So did my mom."


Another.


"I was five."


The room quieted.


"That's what caused my DID."


Another.


"So Andrew and I bonded over having complicated lives."


Kirby smiled.


Fair enough.


He held out his hand.


Adam shook it.


"Yeah."


Another.


"I'd like for you to come by again."


Another.


"I'd say we should get a couple beers."


Another.


"But..."


Adam laughed.


"The apple juice here isn't terrible."


Another.


"Next time we'll have two."


That earned another laugh.


"It was nice meeting you, Kirby."


Adam stood.


"I'll see you again."


Then he walked over to Jane.


Carefully taking Lizzy into his arms.


Jane kissed her daughter's forehead.


Then looked up at Adam.


With so much love it almost hurt.


"Thank you."


Adam winked.


"You bet."


Then he walked out of the room.


Leaving Jane and Kirby alone.


The silence lasted only a few seconds.


Then Kirby looked at her.


Really looked at her.


And something in his expression changed.


"Jane."


His voice was still rough.


But stronger now.


"You talk about Karen."


Another.


"James."


Another.


"Scott."


Another.


"Kelly."


Another.


"Andrew."


He paused.


Looking her directly in the eyes.


"But you don't really talk about yourself."


Jane's heart immediately started racing.


Kirby continued.


"I refuse to believe that in eight years your life has only been work and therapy."


Another.


"So what's happening?"


Another.


"Why won't you tell me?"


The question wasn't angry.


Not accusing.


Just sad.


And hopeful.


As though he desperately wanted her to trust him.


"Please."


Another.


"Because otherwise..."


He looked away briefly.


Then back.


"...I'm going to have to tell you what's going through my head right now."


Jane felt the blood drain from her face.


Because suddenly she wasn't sure she wanted to know.


And for the first time since Kirby woke up...


She had absolutely no idea what to say.
