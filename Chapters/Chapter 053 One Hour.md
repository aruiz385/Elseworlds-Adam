# Chapter 53: One Hour


The park was quiet.


The world felt far away.


For the first time in days there were no doctors.


No hospital rooms.


No decisions.


No conversations about comas.


Just three people walking together.


Adam pushed Lizzy's stroller slowly along the path.


Jane held onto his arm.


Not because she needed help walking.


Because she needed him.


The evening air was cool.


The sky painted in shades of orange and blue.


Neither spoke for a while.


Neither seemed to need to.


Eventually Adam guided them toward a nearby bench.


He checked on Lizzy first.


Of course he did.


Making sure her blanket hadn't slipped.


Making sure she was warm.


Making sure she was comfortable.


Only then did he sit down.


Jane settled beside him.


For a moment they simply watched their daughter sleeping.


Peacefully.


Completely unaware that her parents' world had been turned upside down.


Adam turned toward Jane.


Slowly.


Then kissed her.


Softly.


Tenderly.


The way he always kissed her when words weren't enough.


When the kiss ended he rested his forehead against hers.


Then his head lightly against hers.


Closing his eyes.


Jane immediately felt tears filling her eyes.


"Adam?"


His eyes opened.


"Hmm?"


"I don't want to lose you."


The words landed like a punch.


Adam pulled back slightly.


Looking at her.


Really looking at her.


The hurt.


The fear.


The exhaustion.


Everything she had been carrying.


"The other night before Andrew told me about Kirby, I thought you gave up."


Another.


"I thought you didn't love me anymore."


Her voice shook.


"I had this whole dialogue in my head about how you probably got tired of me giving you back that stupid heart-shaped ring."


A tear slid down her cheek.


"Just when I finally realized how much I love you."


Another.


"And that I don't want to live without you."


Another.


"Then my world gets turned upside down."


Her voice cracked.


"Again."


Another.


"And I thought this is too much for a person to handle."


Another.


"I thought I wouldn't blame you if you chose to walk away."


Adam just stared at her.


The last few days flashed through his mind.


The hospital.


Andrew.


Kelly.


The impossible reality of Kirby.


Watching all of them break apart.


And feeling like an outsider standing at the edge of something he couldn't fix.


"Jane."


His voice was quiet.


"Seeing Kirby alive was the last thing I expected to see."


Another.


"I didn't want to keep anything from you."


Another.


"That was all it was."


Another.


"When Andrew confirmed it was him..."


He looked away.


Only briefly.


Then back at her.


"I felt like my whole world slipped away."


Jane's breath caught.


Adam nodded.


"I felt like I lost you."


The honesty hurt.


But she needed to hear it.


"Honestly?"


Another.


"Even hearing you tell me that you love me..."


Another.


"It makes me happy."


Another.


"But I am scared out of my mind."


His hand found hers.


Holding on.


"You're Lizzy's mother."


Another.


"The woman I love more than anyone in this whole world."


Another.


"You and Lizzy are my world."


Tears filled his eyes.


"I know I told you I was doing integration therapy for Lizzy."


Another.


"And that's true."


Another.


"But I'm doing it for us."


Another.


"I need you."


Another.


"I need Lizzy."


Another.


"You're my family."


Another.


"You're my home."


His voice broke.


"I am not walking away because I can't lose you."


Another.


"I can't be without you."


Another.


"I hope you understand that."


Jane was crying openly now.


Adam reached into his pocket.


Then slowly held something out.


The heart-shaped ring.


Jane immediately laughed through her tears.


"Oh God."


Adam smiled softly.


"I will never, ever stop putting this ring back on your finger."


Another.


"Ever."


Another.


"Because like I told you before..."


Another.


"It belongs to you."


Another.


"My heart belongs to you."


Another.


"It always has."


Another.


"Since the day I saw you in Scott's office."


Jane stared at him.


Completely overwhelmed.


Adam carefully took her hand.


"So I know this is incredibly ill-timed."


Another.


"And I know marriage is probably the last thing you're thinking about because of the obvious and..."


"Yes."


Adam froze.


"What?"


"Yes."


He blinked.


"Are you serious?"


Another.


"You're not fighting me this time?"


Another.


"No smart comment about taking it off in public?"


Jane smiled through her tears.


"Doctor."


Another.


"Take the win while you can."


Adam laughed.


The sound was warm.


Relieved.


He slipped the ring back onto her finger.


Looking at it like it had finally found where it belonged.


Jane looked at him.


Then grabbed his jacket.


Pulling him toward her.


Kissing him.


Long.


Passionately.


Everything she had spent months fighting.


Everything she had spent months trying not to feel.


Gone.


When they finally broke apart, Adam looked slightly stunned.


Jane smiled.


"So."


Another.


"You loved me since you met me in Scott's office."


A small smile appeared.


"Well..."


Jane pointed at him.


"But don't you forget that I was the one who asked you out first."


Immediately Adam grinned.


"Uhmm..."


Another.


"Technically I said it was a date."


Another.


"You called it a fact-finding mission."


Jane laughed.


"Not that time."


Adam paused.


Then recognition appeared.


"Thrive City."


Jane smiled.


"Exactly."


Another.


"I asked even after you adamantly told me that you actively avoided emotional attachment."


Adam stared.


"That was you asking me out?"


Jane groaned.


"Well..."


Another.


"Sort of."


Another.


"I got embarrassed."


Another.


"So I hid behind my hands."


Adam laughed softly.


"I remember."


Another.


"You thought you crossed some kind of line."


Another.


"It was cute."


Jane rolled her eyes.


Adam pulled her closer.


"But honestly?"


Another.


"I think you were right the first time."


Jane looked up.


"What first time?"


"The first day of group therapy."


Another.


"When you tried to run away because you realized I was invested."


Jane groaned.


Immediately.


Adam smiled.


"By then I already loved you."


Another.


"So much."


Another.


"Your stories."


Another.


"Your horrible Hulk jokes."


Another.


"The eye roll."


Another.


"Everything."


Jane rested her head against his shoulder.


"We are something else."


Another.


"These last four years have been so chaotic."


Another.


"And I never thought it could get more chaotic."


Another.


"Turns out?"


Another.


"I was very wrong."


Adam looked toward the sky.


"Please don't tempt fate anymore."


Jane laughed.


Then looked at him.


The man she loved.


The father of her daughter.


Her future.


Adam kissed the top of her head.


"Let's just be happy."


Another.


"Let's take the small moments and enjoy them."


Another.


"Whatever comes tomorrow..."


Another.


"We'll deal with tomorrow together."


His hand found hers.


"I am here with you for the long haul."


Another.


"And just like you keep telling me..."


Another.


"I'm not going anywhere."


Another.


"Not now."


Another.


"Not ever."


His smile softened.


"So, Ms. McFly."


Jane smiled.


"Yes, Dr. Carter?"


"I hope to call you Mrs. Carter when all of this is over."


The blush that spread across her face was immediate.


Adam looked entirely too happy about it.


"But today..."


Another.


"We go home."


Another.


"We change."


Another.


"We feed Lizzy."


Another.


"We read her a story."


Another.


"We put her to bed."


Another.


"And then..."


He leaned closer.


"I'm finally going to make love to you like I've wanted to for the last four months."


Jane's entire face turned red.


"Adam!"


He looked far too pleased with himself.


Jane kissed him.


Mostly to stop him talking.


It worked.


For a few seconds.


When they finally pulled apart, she rested her forehead against his.


"I love you."


Adam closed his eyes.


Smiling.


"Me too."


Another.


"Always."


And that is exactly what they did.


They went home.


They fed their daughter.


They read her a story.


They put her to bed.


And for one evening they chose not to think about hospitals.


Or comas.


Or impossible decisions.


They focused on Lizzy.


And each other.


Because neither of them knew what the coming weeks would bring.


Neither of them knew what would happen when Kirby opened his eyes.


But tonight belonged to them.


And for now...


That was enough.
