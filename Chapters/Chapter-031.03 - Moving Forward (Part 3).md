# Chapter 31:  Moving Forward (Part 3)

Jane hurried down the hallway toward Dr. Ryan's office carrying a clipboard, a notebook, and three separate lists.


One list for work.


One list for the nursery.


One list for things she kept forgetting to do.


Unfortunately, she'd forgotten where she put that list.


Which was becoming a problem.


Jane glanced down at the clipboard.


Then turned the corner.


And stopped.


Adam.


Sitting outside Dr. Ryan's office.


For a second neither of them moved.


It had been weeks.


Weeks since the apartment.


Weeks since the kitchen.


Weeks since she'd watched her friend look at her like she didn't belong in his life.


Adam stood immediately.


"Hey, Jane."


Jane blinked.


Because the look was gone.


No anger.


No fear.


No violation.


No guarded hostility.


Instead he looked exactly the way he'd looked during those first four months.


The version of Adam she'd spent countless hours drinking coffee with.


The version that listened more than he talked.


The version that became her friend.


Some of the tension left her shoulders immediately.


"Hey, Adam."


A pause.


"How are you?"


Unfortunately, at that exact moment one of her lists slipped.


Adam reached out and caught it before it hit the floor.


"Thanks."


"No problem."


He handed it back.


"I'm adjusting."


The answer was honest.


Then his eyes softened.


"How are you doing?"


Jane laughed once.


The exhausted kind.


"Uhm..."


A pause.


"You know."


Another.


"Trying to hold it together."


Another.


"Work."


Another.


"Therapy."


Another.


"Baby."


Another.


"Lots to do."


Another.


"Not enough time."


Adam nodded.


And immediately knew she wasn't kidding.


She looked tired.


The kind of tired that came from carrying too much for too long.


Before either of them could say anything else, Dr. Ryan appeared.


Then immediately frowned.


"Jane?"


A pause.


"What are you doing here?"


Another.


"I wasn't expecting you."


Jane winced.


"Dr. Ryan."


A desperate smile appeared.


"I just need five minutes."


Then she looked at Adam.


A silent plea.


*Please don't be upset.*


Dr. Ryan caught it immediately.


She turned toward Adam.


"Dr. Carter?"


A pause.


"Are you okay with pushing our session back five minutes?"


"Of course."


Adam nodded immediately.


"Yeah."


Jane mouthed a silent thank you.


Adam smiled faintly.


Then Dr. Ryan ushered Jane inside.


The office door swung partially closed.


Almost.


Almost being the important word.


Because it never actually latched.


The door remained slightly open.


Adam noticed.


He should probably close it.


He knew that.


Instead he found himself sitting perfectly still.


Then he heard Jane's voice.


"Dr. Ryan, I am freaking out."


Adam froze.


Inside the office Jane dropped the clipboard onto a chair.


"I'm so overwhelmed."


Another.


"That crib?"


Another.


"Still sitting in the box staring at me."


Dr. Ryan nodded patiently.


Jane ran both hands through her hair.


"And then I was driving this morning."


Another.


"And *Ain't Too Proud to Beg* came on."


Adam immediately closed his eyes.


His song.


Or rather...


The other Adam's song.


Inside the office, Jane's voice broke.


"And I just lost it."


A pause.


"The last few months I've been trying to be tough."


Another.


"And telling myself I can do this alone."


Another.


"But today I feel like I'm failing at everything."


Tears slipped down her face.


"I miss Adam."


The words hit him like a punch.


"He made everything easier."


Silence.


Dr. Ryan waited.


Then gently:


"Jane."


Jane wiped at her face.


"I know."


Another.


"Working through that is going to take time."


Another.


"It's only been two weeks."


Jane nodded.


Then handed over one of the lists she'd been carrying.


"The things you wanted me to work on."


Dr. Ryan accepted it.


Then Jane groaned.


"And I forgot to refill my prescription."


Another.


"I'm sorry."


Dr. Ryan laughed softly.


"You're forgiven."


She immediately pulled out a prescription pad.


"I'll write you another one."


Relief flooded Jane's face.


"Thank you."


Another.


"And I'm sorry for dropping in."


Another.


"I just felt like I was drowning."


Dr. Ryan smiled.


"It's okay."


Another.


"You're about to be a new mother."


Another.


"Being overwhelmed is practically part of the job description."


That made Jane laugh.


Just a little.


"We can work through the rest during your actual appointment."


Jane nodded.


"Thanks."


A few moments later she emerged.


Clipboard.


Notebook.


Lists.


Still carrying everything.


Still trying to carry everything.


She paused when she saw Adam standing.


"Thanks again."


She started to leave.


Then:


"Jane."


She stopped.


"Yeah?"


Adam swallowed.


Because suddenly this felt a lot harder than he'd imagined.


"I was wondering..."


A pause.


"If we could talk sometime."


Jane blinked.


That was not what she'd expected.


At all.


"I..."


Adam immediately shook his head.


"When you're ready."


Another.


"Not now."


Another.


"But eventually."


Jane looked down at her lists.


Then back at him.


"I'm kind of overwhelmed right now."


"I know."


The answer surprised her.


Because he really did sound like the old Adam.


The friend.


Not the stranger from the kitchen.


"I can help."


The words caught her off guard.


"If you need me to."


That surprised her even more.


"I..."


She hesitated.


"I don't know if that's a good idea."


Adam nodded.


"Maybe not."


Another.


"But I think we need to talk."


Another.


"I finished the videos."


The statement landed heavily.


"And I have questions."


Jane looked at him for several moments.


Trying to decide if this was a disaster waiting to happen.


Or something that needed to happen eventually.


Finally:


"I'll be home later."


Another.


"You can come by."


Adam looked almost relieved.


"Okay."


"I mean it."


Jane pointed.


"Are you sure?"


The corner of Adam's mouth lifted.


Immediately understanding.


Thrive City.


The missed meeting.


The joke.


"Short of a natural disaster..."


A grin appeared.


"I'll be there this time."


Jane shook her head.


Despite herself, she almost smiled.


Then she turned and walked away.


Adam watched her disappear down the hallway.


Only when she was gone did he realize he'd been holding his breath.


Behind him a familiar voice spoke.


"Interesting."


Adam turned.


Dr. Ryan was standing in the doorway.


Watching him.


Arms crossed.


Entirely too amused.


"I have no idea what you're talking about."


Dr. Ryan laughed.


"Of course you don't."


Then she stepped aside.


"Come on in, Dr. Carter."


A pause.


"I believe we have quite a bit to discuss."


And judging by the look on her face...


Adam suspected that was a dramatic understatement.
