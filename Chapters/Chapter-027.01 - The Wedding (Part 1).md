# Chapter 27: The Wedding (Part 1)

Jane woke up to someone staring at her.


Not a casual glance.


Not a sweet look.


Staring.


Intensely.


Jane opened one eye.


Adam was sitting in the chair by the window.

Coffee in hand.

Completely dressed.

And staring at her like she'd personally invented sunlight.


"That's unsettling."


Adam smiled.


"Good morning."


Jane groaned.


"What time is it?"


"Wedding day."


"That's not a time."


"It's the only time that matters."


Jane laughed despite herself.


Then noticed he was still staring.


"What?"


Adam's smile softened.


"You woke up as my fiancée today."


Jane immediately buried her face in a pillow.


"Oh no."


"Oh yes."


"Adam."


"Jane."


She pointed at him from under the pillow.


"I haven't decided if I forgive you yet."


"For what?"


"For unleashing Karen."


Adam looked completely unrepentant.


"That's fair."


"She almost tackled me."


"Love takes many forms."


Jane rolled her eyes.


"Maria took pictures."


"Of course she did."


"Kelly examined the ring like she was auditing it."


Adam laughed harder.


"And Chris made at least six comments."


"Only six?"


Jane threw a pillow at him.


Adam caught it.

Still smiling.


Then the smile softened.


He got up.

Crossed the room.


Knelt beside the bed.


And kissed her.


Slowly.

Tenderly.


When he pulled back, he rested his forehead against hers.


"I just want you happy."


The joking disappeared immediately.


Jane looked at him.


"I know."


Adam smiled.


"No."


A pause.


"I really do."


Another.


"As long as I'm here..."


His hand found hers.


"...I'm going to spend every minute doing everything in my power to make you happy."


Jane felt her eyes sting.


"Adam..."


"I mean it."


Another pause.


"Every minute."


He looked at her for a second.

Then grinned.


"Also."


Jane sighed.


"Oh no."


"Oh yes."


A pause.


"If at any point during this wedding you decide you'd rather get married..."


Jane immediately laughed.


"Adam."


"...let me know."


"Adam."


"I'll kidnap you."


"Adam."


"We'll make a run for it."


Jane shook her head.

Laughing.


"You are impossible."


His grin widened.


"It'd be fun."


She gave him a look.


A very specific look.


The look that said:


You know I'd do it.


And you know exactly why we can't.


The grin softened.


Because he did know.


Eventually Jane glanced at the ring.


"Remember."


Adam sighed dramatically.


"Yes."


"The ring comes off if he comes back."


The words pulled both of them abruptly back to reality.


For a moment neither spoke.


Then Adam kissed her forehead.


"Today doesn't belong to reality."


A pause.


"It belongs to Karen."


Jane laughed.


"That's probably how she'd describe it too."


A few hours later Jane disappeared into the bathroom carrying her dress, makeup bag, shoes, and what Adam considered to be an alarming number of hair accessories.


"How many of those do you need?"


"All of them."


"That seems statistically unlikely."


"It isn't."


Adam laughed.


Then waited.


And waited.


And waited.


Until finally the bathroom door opened.


Adam looked up.


And completely forgot what he was about to say.


Jane stopped in the doorway.


Immediately noticing.


"Oh no."


Adam didn't answer.


Because she looked beautiful.


The pale blue dress fit perfectly.


Elegant.

Simple.

Timeless.


Her hair fell softly around her shoulders.


The tiny curve of her stomach was just visible now.

Not enough for most people to notice.


But enough for him.


And somehow that made her even more beautiful.


"Adam."


Nothing.


"Adam."


Still nothing.


Jane sighed.


"You are staring again."


"I know."


"You have to stop doing that."


"I literally cannot."


That made her blush.


Adam stood.

Still looking at her.


"You look incredible."


Jane smiled.


"Thank you."


He crossed the room.


Slowly.


Then reached for her hand.


The ring immediately caught the light.


Adam's smile softened.


"There it is."


Jane looked down at it.


"Don't start."


"I'm not starting anything."


"You are absolutely starting something."


Adam lifted her hand and kissed her knuckles.


"I just like seeing it there."


Jane tried not to smile.

Failed.


Adam looked at her for another second.

Then another.


"What?"

Jane asked.


He shook his head.


"Nothing."


A pause.


"Just trying to memorize this."


That hit her harder than she wanted it to.


So instead she leaned forward and kissed him.


Briefly.


Before the moment could become too emotional.


"Now stop staring at me."


"No."


"Adam."


"Never."


A knock interrupted them.


Without waiting for permission, the door opened.


Andrew stepped inside.


Stopped.


And froze.


Jane immediately noticed.


So did Adam.


Andrew blinked.


Once.


Twice.


Then looked away.


"Wow."


A pause.


"Okay."


Another.


"That caught me off guard."


Jane laughed.


"What?"


Andrew pointed vaguely in her direction.


"You."


Adam looked extremely entertained.


Andrew immediately recovered.

Mostly.


"Anyway."


A pause.


"I came for my Maid of Honor."


Jane stood and smoothed her dress.


Andrew looked again.


And immediately regretted it.


"Wow."


Jane burst out laughing.


"There it is again."


Andrew pointed.


"I'm trying very hard to be professional."


"You literally work in logistics."


"Exactly."


The three of them laughed.


Then Andrew's expression softened.


For just a moment.


"You look beautiful."


Jane smiled.


"Thank you."


Andrew nodded.


Then added:


"And pregnancy looks good on you."


The comment hit unexpectedly.


For a second Jane's smile faltered.


Just slightly.


Because another memory appeared.


Another baby.


Another future.


One that never happened.


Andrew immediately saw it.


His expression softened.


Jane smiled again.

A sadder one.


"Thanks."


Andrew understood.


No further explanation needed.


Then he stepped forward.

Offering his arm.


"Come on."


A grin appeared.


"We have important wedding people things to do."


Jane laughed.


"I hate important wedding people things."


"You love them."


"I really don't."


"You really do."


Adam watched them leave.


Smiling.


Because they both looked happy.


And that mattered.


The ceremony was held beside the lake.


White chairs lined the grass.


Flowers framed the aisle.


The sky was clear.


Perfect.


Karen would never let anyone forget it.


As Best Man and Maid of Honor, Andrew and Jane walked together down the aisle.


From the audience, Adam watched her.


Completely distracted.


Kelly noticed immediately.


"You haven't looked at the bride once."


Adam smiled.


"I have."


"No, you haven't."


Maria laughed.


"He's hopeless."


Chris nodded.


"Completely."


Adam accepted this judgment without argument.


Because they weren't wrong.


The ceremony itself was beautiful.


Simple.

Honest.


Fifteen years of history distilled into a handful of promises.


By the time Karen and James kissed, half the audience was crying.


Including Karen.


Who was somehow crying before the vows even started.


The reception that followed was everything Jane expected.


Dancing.

Laughter.

Terrible decisions.


Chris attempting dance moves that should have been medically prohibited.


Karen refusing to sit down.


James looking permanently overwhelmed.


At one point Andrew stepped forward to give his Best Man speech.


The room quieted.


Andrew raised his glass.


"I've known James since high school."


A pause.


"And James has been in love with Karen since approximately five minutes after meeting her."


The room laughed.


James hid his face.


"I'm serious."


Andrew pointed.


"Day one."


Karen looked delighted.


James groaned.


Andrew smiled.


"It only took him fifteen years to get here."


The room erupted.


Chris loudly yelled:


"Progress!"


More laughter.


Andrew shook his head.

Still smiling.


"But honestly..."


The room quieted.


"I've never met a couple more solid than these two."


A pause.


"They've weathered everything."


Another.


"And somehow they always come back to each other."


His smile softened.


"I envy that."


Another pause.


"In the best possible way."


He looked toward Karen and James.


"It's not every day you get to spend the rest of your life with someone you've known for most of it."


Without meaning to, his eyes found Jane.


Just for a moment.


And the room suddenly felt different.


Jane smiled.


But her chest tightened slightly.


Adam noticed.


Kelly noticed too.


Neither said anything.


The moment passed.


Andrew lifted his glass again.


"To Karen and James."


A pause.


"May the next fifteen years be even better than the last."


The room cheered.


Later, Jane stepped up for her Maid of Honor speech.


She looked at Karen.

Then James.


And smiled.


"When we were in high school..."


A pause.


"I created an extra yearbook category."


Karen immediately started laughing.


"Oh no."


"Oh yes."


Jane grinned.


"The Couple Most Likely To Be Together In Ten Years."


The room laughed.


"To be fair."


Jane pointed at Karen.


"You were campaigning very aggressively for Class Couple."


The room erupted.


Karen looked deeply offended.


"I was not."


"You absolutely were."


James nodded from his seat.


"She was."


More laughter.


Jane lifted her glass.


"The point is..."


A pause.


"I was right."


The room quieted.


"You two built something extraordinary."


Another.


"Something steady."


Another.


"Kind."


Another.


"Patient."


Another.


"Something that never wavered."


Jane smiled.


"You are incredibly lucky."


A pause.


"To find a love that's non-negotiable."


Her eyes drifted to Adam for a brief second.


Then back to Karen and James.


"The kind of love that keeps choosing the same person."


Another.


"Every day."


Another.


"Over and over again."


Karen was already crying.


James wasn't far behind.


Jane laughed softly.


"I love both of you."


A pause.


"And I hope you never stop choosing each other."


Another.


"Just like you have for the last fifteen years."


She raised her glass.


"To Karen and James."


The room erupted.


And for one perfect afternoon, surrounded by the people they loved most in the world, it felt like the kind of magic that could last forever.
