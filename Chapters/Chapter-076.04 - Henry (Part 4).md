# Chapter 76: Henry (Part 4)


Eventually the afternoon began winding down.


The adrenaline faded.


The laughter softened.


And reality slowly started returning.


Henry was asleep in his bassinet.


Karen looked exhausted.


James looked equal parts proud and terrified.


Which, Jane suspected, was probably the correct reaction to becoming a parent.


Again.


Adam stood first.


"We should probably let them rest."


Karen pointed at him.


"Look at that."


Another.


"Responsible."


Another.


"Who are you and what have you done with Adam?"


Adam smiled.


"Fatherhood changed me."


Jane snorted.


"It absolutely did not."


The room laughed.


Andrew handed Lizzy back to Adam and kissed the top of her head one last time.


"See you soon, kiddo."


Lizzy rewarded him with a gummy smile.


Andrew immediately melted.


As always.


"Yep."


James laughed.


"You're hopeless."


"Correct."


Andrew replied.


There was no point denying it.


Goodbyes started.


Hugs were exchanged.


Promises were made.


Plans for the welcome-home party were discussed.


Eventually Adam moved toward the door with Lizzy balanced on his hip.


Andrew followed.


James walked them both out.


Leaving Jane alone with Karen for the first time all afternoon.


The moment the door closed, Karen's expression changed.


Jane noticed immediately.


"What's wrong?"


Karen hesitated.


Which was enough to make Jane sit up straighter.


"Kare?"


Karen took a slow breath.


"It's Kelly."


Jane immediately froze.


Not because she disliked Kelly.


Quite the opposite.


Because she knew exactly how complicated everything had become.


"What about her?"


Karen looked directly at her.


"Do you know why she really ended things with Andrew?"


Jane's stomach dropped.


Because she did.


Or at least she knew enough.


"Kirby."


Karen nodded.


"Kirby."


The word settled heavily between them.


Jane looked down immediately.


Karen continued.


"He wasn't the only reason."


Another.


"But he was part of it."


Another.


"Kelly spent years trying to build a future with a man who was still connected to another life."


Jane swallowed hard.


Because all she could think about was the folder sitting in her purse.


The unsigned divorce papers.


The papers Kelly had helped Kirby complete.


The papers she still hadn't signed herself.


"Kare..."


Karen frowned.


"What?"


Jane let out a long breath.


"There is something you don't know."


Karen waited.


Jane stared down at her hands.


"A few weeks before your wedding."


Another.


"The night Adam and I went on a double date with Kelly and Andrew."


Recognition crossed Karen's face.


"The night you got sick."


Jane nodded.


"The night Kelly cornered me in the bathroom and told me I should probably take a pregnancy test."


Karen blinked.


"You never told me that."


Jane laughed softly.


"I got a little distracted after that."


Karen snorted.


"Fair."


Jane's smile faded.


"But while we were in there..."


Another.


"She told me she used to imagine a future."


Karen stayed quiet.


Listening.


"A future where it was Andrew and me."


Another.


"And her and Kirby."


Karen's eyes widened slightly.


Jane nodded.


"Then she told me she'd been in love with him since college."


Another.


"That she should've fought for him."


Another.


"That she never stopped wondering what might've happened if she had."


Karen slowly exhaled.


"Oh."


Jane nodded.


"Yeah."


Silence settled between them.


"And now she helped him."


Karen frowned.


"Helped him?"


"The divorce papers."


Jane looked away.


"She helped Kirby get through that."


Another.


"She helped him understand what he needed to do."


Another.


"She helped him sign them."


Jane laughed bitterly.


"The papers he needed so I could move on."


Another.


"And I still haven't signed them."


Karen stared at her.


"Jane."


"I know."


Jane rubbed her forehead.


"I know how awful that sounds."


Another.


"Andrew found out today."


Another.


"He looked at me like I'd completely lost my mind."


Karen remained silent.


Jane continued.


"And when you tell me Kelly left because she couldn't build a future around an unresolved past..."


Another.


"I feel guilty."


Karen squeezed her hand.


"Oh honey."


Jane shook her head.


"I'm serious."


Another.


"What if she's right?"


Another.


"What if I've been doing exactly what hurt her?"


The room fell quiet.


Finally Karen spoke.


"Then talk to Adam."


Another.


"Figure out what you're feeling."


Another.


"But stop punishing yourself for being human."


Jane looked down.


Because she wanted to believe that.


"It isn't just that."


Karen frowned.


"What do you mean?"


Jane stared at the floor.


"I think Kelly still loves him."


Another.


"I know she does."


Karen nodded.


Not surprised.


Jane continued.


"And honestly?"


Another.


"I think Kirby might have feelings for her too."


That caught Karen off guard.


"You do?"


Jane nodded.


"I do."


Another.


"But I don't think he'll ever act on them."


"Because of Andrew."


Jane nodded again.


"Because of Andrew."


Another.


"Because Kirby would rather make himself miserable than intentionally hurt his brother."


Another.


"And because he already carries enough guilt."


Karen sighed.


Because that sounded exactly like Kirby.


Jane rubbed both hands across her face.


"And that's another problem."


Karen waited.


Jane laughed tiredly.


"If Kirby ever decides to talk about Kelly..."


Another.


"He's going to talk to me."


Karen's eyes widened.


Jane nodded.


"He's one of my best friends."


Another.


"I'm one of his."


Another.


"If he's struggling with this..."


Another.


"If he's confused..."


Another.


"If he doesn't know what to do..."


Another.


"I'm eventually getting that conversation."


Karen sighed.


Because she knew Jane was right.


"And I don't want to be in the middle."


Jane admitted.


Another.


"But somehow..."


Another.


"I'm standing there again."


Karen reached for her hand.


"Do you know why I'm asking you specifically to call Kelly?"


Jane looked up.


"No."


Karen squeezed her hand.


"Because you know exactly what this feels like."


Jane frowned.


"What?"


Karen didn't look away.


"Being the one outside the circle."


The words hit immediately.


Hard.


"When you and Andrew broke up..."


Another.


"Who got everybody?"


Jane looked down.


Because she knew the answer.


"Andrew."


Karen nodded.


"Andrew has all of us."


Another.


"Even after everything, he still has his people."


Jane closed her eyes.


Because Karen wasn't wrong.


Karen looked at her carefully.


"Who does Kelly have?"


Silence.


"Karen..."


"Who does she have?"


Jane didn't answer.


Because she didn't have one.


And that told Karen everything.


"She's our friend too."


Karen said softly.


Another.


"She stood by you."


Another.


"She stood by Andrew."


Another.


"She stood by all of us."


Another.


"And now she's alone."


Jane felt her chest tighten.


Because she remembered.


She remembered being lonely.


She remembered being the one left behind.


She remembered having only Karen.


Finally she spoke.


"I don't know how to do this."


Karen squeezed her hand.


"Call her."


Another.


"Have brunch."


Another.


"Listen."


Another.


"That's all I'm asking."


Jane sat quietly for a long moment.


Finally she nodded.


"I'll call her."


Karen visibly relaxed.


"Thank you."


Jane pointed at her.


"If this blows up in my face, I'm blaming you."


Karen laughed.


"That's fair."


For the first time since the conversation began, Jane smiled.


A real smile.


Then she leaned over and kissed Karen's forehead.


"Get some sleep."


Karen glanced at Henry.


Then toward the hallway where James had disappeared.


"I don't think that's how this works anymore."


Jane laughed.


"Good luck with that."


She kissed the top of Henry's tiny head.


Then headed for the door.


Outside she found Adam holding Lizzy.


Andrew standing beside him.


James explaining something about diapers with entirely too much enthusiasm.


The sight immediately calmed something inside her.


Family.


Messy.


Complicated.


Impossible.


But family.


Adam looked up first.


"You okay?"


Jane smiled.


A little tired.


A little overwhelmed.


A little emotional.


"Yeah."


It wasn't entirely true.


But it wasn't entirely false either.


Adam studied her.


Clearly unconvinced.


Then shifted Lizzy into one arm and held out his hand.


Jane took it immediately.


And together they started walking toward home.


Toward difficult conversations.


Toward unsigned papers.


Toward friends who needed help.


Toward whatever came next.
