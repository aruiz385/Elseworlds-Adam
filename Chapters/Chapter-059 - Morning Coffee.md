# Chapter 59: Morning Coffee


Jane turned toward the elevator.


And froze.


Adam.


Standing in front of the open elevator doors.


Coffee trays in both hands.


Her overnight bag hanging from one shoulder.


The sight of him hit her so hard she forgot how to breathe.


For a moment she just stared.


Then she ran.


Before Adam could even react.


Jane was already halfway down the hallway.


"Jane—"


She threw herself into his arms.


Nearly sending coffee flying everywhere.


Adam somehow managed to keep hold of everything.


Barely.


Fortunately Kelly appeared from nowhere.


Snatching the coffee trays before disaster struck.


"Saving the coffees before they become a casualty in the middle of a hospital hallway."


She pointed toward them and laughed.


Then winked at Jane.


Before walking away with the coffees.


Giving them space.


Jane didn't even notice.


She buried her face in Adam's neck.


Holding him so tightly that he almost dropped the overnight bag too.


God.


She'd missed him.


Adam wrapped his arms around her.


Just as tightly.


Then kissed her.


Slowly.


Like he needed to reassure himself she was actually there.


When they finally pulled apart, he rested his forehead against hers.


"Hi."


Jane laughed through the tears threatening to spill.


"Hi."


Adam smiled.


Then immediately said:


"Lizzy's okay."


The relief was immediate.


Jane closed her eyes.


Of course.


Of course he knew that was the first thing she was worried about.


"She's okay?"


Adam nodded.


"She's perfect."


Another.


"She slept great."


Another.


"Your parents are completely obsessed with her."


Jane laughed.


"As they should be."


"Agreed."


For a moment neither moved.


Neither wanting to let go.


Then Adam kissed her again.


Briefly.


"I missed you."


Jane smiled.


"It was one night."


"I know."


Another.


"It was awful."


That got a laugh out of her.


A real one.


The first since she'd seen Kirby wake up.


"Did you sleep?"


Adam gave her a look.


"Jane."


Another.


"You know I can't sleep without you."


She rolled her eyes.


Because he wasn't wrong.


Years of trauma.


Months of sleeping beside one another.


Late night feedings.


A four-month-old baby.


Their lives had settled into a rhythm.


And Adam sleeping without Jane tended to go very badly.


"So?"


She asked.


Adam sighed dramatically.


"Barely."


That only made her smile more.


And then his expression softened.


"How is he?"


The question immediately changed everything.


Jane looked back toward Kirby's room.


Then back at Adam.


"He's awake."


Adam nodded.


Listening carefully.


"He knows me."


Another.


"He trusts me."


Another.


"He won't let me leave."


Her voice cracked.


"He has no idea time passed."


Another.


"He doesn't know seven years are gone."


Another.


"As far as he's concerned, he woke up in his life."


She swallowed.


"Adam..."


Another.


"He's relying on me."


Adam took her hand immediately.


"I know."


Another.


"And that's okay."


Jane shook her head.


"I don't know what to do."


The confession came out small.


Honest.


Vulnerable.


Adam squeezed her hand.


"We'll figure it out."


Another.


"Together."


The tension in her shoulders eased immediately.


Because Adam never said things he didn't mean.


Then he lifted the overnight bag.


"That's why I brought this."


Jane looked at the bag and immediately laughed.


"Oh my God."


Adam groaned.


"Yes."


Another.


"The bag."


Jane smiled.


The overnight bag.


The one she'd used for years whenever she stayed at Adam's apartment.


Back when neither of them wanted to admit exactly what they were doing.


Back when she still insisted she wasn't practically living there.


Adam pointed at it.


"I hate this bag."


Jane laughed harder.


"You do not."


"I absolutely do."


Another.


"It's been haunting me for years."


Another.


"Every time I saw that bag, I knew you were staying over but neither one of us was willing to admit you basically lived with me."


Jane shook her head.


"I did not basically live with you."


Adam gave her a look.


A very unimpressed look.


Jane immediately started laughing again.


"Okay."


Another.


"Maybe towards the end."


"Towards the end?"


Another.


"Jane, the bag had its own spot in my closet."


That earned another laugh.


Adam sighed dramatically.


"But today?"


Another.


"Today I am willing to acknowledge its usefulness."


Another.


"These are clearly the kind of emergency circumstances this bag was designed for."


Jane smiled.


God, she'd missed him.


Adam slipped an arm around her waist.


"Okay."


Another.


"Here's the plan."


Jane immediately narrowed her eyes.


"You have a plan."


"I always have a plan."


Another.


"First."


He handed her a coffee.


"You drink this."


Another.


"Then I am personally escorting you to the doctors' lounge."


Another.


"You are taking a shower."


Another.


"You are changing into clean clothes."


Another.


"You are brushing your hair."


Another.


"And then I'm getting breakfast into you."


Jane opened her mouth.


Adam pointed at her.


"No arguments."


That earned another laugh.


"Bossy."


"Correct."


Another.


"After that I'll do my rounds."


Another.


"I'm going to talk to Scott."


Another.


"I'm going to find out how I can help."


Another.


"And while all of that is happening..."


He squeezed her hand.


"I'm making sure you're okay."


That almost broke her.


Because for the last twenty-four hours everyone needed something from her.


Andrew.


Jonathan.


Kirby.


The hospital.


Everyone.


But Adam?


Adam was worried about her.


Jane threw her arms around him again.


Holding him tightly.


"I love you."


The words came out muffled against his shoulder.


Adam kissed the top of her head.


"I love you too."


Another.


"And your parents are bringing Lizzy later."


Jane immediately looked up.


"They are?"


Adam nodded.


"So you can spend time with her."


Another.


"You don't have to worry."


Another.


"I've got you."


Jane felt tears sting her eyes again.


Not because she was sad.


Because suddenly everything felt manageable.


Kirby was awake.


Adam was here.


Lizzy was okay.


She wasn't doing this alone.


For the first time since Kirby opened his eyes...


Jane felt like she could breathe.


"Come on."


Adam handed her the coffee.


Then took her hand.


And together they headed toward the doctors' lounge.
