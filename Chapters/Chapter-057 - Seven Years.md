# Chapter 57: Seven Years

Jane didn't let go immediately.


Neither did Kirby.


For several long moments they simply stayed like that.


Holding onto each other.


Both crying.


Both overwhelmed.


Neither knowing what to say.


Eventually Jane pulled back enough to look at him.


Kirby's eyes never left her.


Not for a second.


As though he was afraid she might disappear.


As though he was still trying to convince himself she was real.


"Hey."


Jane wiped at her tears.


Attempting a smile.


A very unsuccessful smile.


"You're making me cry."


The corner of Kirby's mouth twitched.


The closest thing to a smile he'd managed.


He tried to speak again.


Nothing.


The effort frustrated him immediately.


Jane noticed.


"It's okay."


Another.


"Your throat is going to be sore for a while."


Another.


"You've been intubated."


Kirby frowned.


Confused.


The word clearly meaning very little to him right now.


Jane immediately regretted saying it.


Because she could practically see the questions multiplying in his head.


Where am I?


What happened?


Why am I here?


Why do I feel like this?


Jane reached for the call button.


"I need to get Scott."


Kirby's hand tightened around hers.


Instantly.


The message clear.


Don't leave.


Jane's heart broke all over again.


"I'm just getting Scott."


Another.


"I promise."


Another.


"I'm coming back."


Reluctantly he released her hand.


Only after making sure she wasn't actually going anywhere.


Jane stepped into the hallway.


Her heart hammering.


The ICU felt twice as bright now.


Too bright.


Too awake.


She found Scott first.


The monitor alerts had already reached him.


He was halfway down the hallway before she said a word.


Andrew wasn't far behind.


Then Kelly.


Then Jonathan.


"What happened?"


Andrew reached her first.


Jane could barely stop smiling through the tears.


"He's awake."


The words hit all of them at once.


Jonathan immediately covered his mouth.


Kelly grabbed Andrew's arm.


Scott looked relieved.


Genuinely relieved.


Then his expression shifted.


Doctor.


Professional.


Focused.


"What happened?"


Jane took a breath.


"He was calm."


Another.


"He recognized me."


Another.


"He squeezed my hand."


Another.


"And when I tried to come find you..."


Her voice cracked.


"He didn't want me to leave."


Scott nodded immediately.


Of course he didn't.


As far as Kirby knew...


Jane was his wife.


The last familiar thing in a world that suddenly made no sense.


Andrew immediately started toward the room.


Scott stopped him.


"Not yet."


Andrew froze.


"What?"


"We already tried this once."


Scott's voice remained calm.


"He woke up."


Another.


"He saw a room full of people."


Another.


"And he panicked."


Nobody argued.


Because they had all watched it happen.


Scott looked at Jane.


"Only Jane."


Andrew frowned.


"What?"


"Only Jane goes in."


Another.


"He's calm."


Another.


"We keep him calm."


Another.


"We don't overwhelm him again."


Jonathan nodded first.


Because deep down he knew Scott was right.


Kelly nodded too.


Reluctantly, Andrew stepped back.


Scott turned to Jane.


"Let's go."


Together they walked into the room.


Kirby immediately looked toward the door.


Searching.


Waiting.


The moment he saw Jane, relief flooded his face.


Immediate.


Instinctive.


Jane's heart almost broke from it.


She walked straight back to his side.


Took his hand.


And smiled through the tears.


"Hey."


Another.


"See?"


Another.


"I told you I'd be back."


Kirby's grip tightened immediately.


As though he never wanted to let go again.


Jane pointed toward Scott.


"And look who I brought with me."


That earned the tiniest hint of amusement.


Not much.


But enough.


Scott moved to the opposite side of the bed.


A familiar face.


A familiar voice.


One more piece of solid ground.


"Hey, kid."


Recognition immediately appeared in Kirby's eyes.


Not confusion.


Recognition.


Scott smiled.


"It's good to see you."


Kirby's eyes filled again.


And he gave the slightest nod.


Scott's expression softened for a moment.


Then he shifted into physician mode.


"Alright."


Another.


"We keep this simple."


Another.


"You don't have to talk."


Another.


"Just nod yes or no."


Kirby nodded.


Good.


"Do you know your name?"


A nod.


"Good."


"Do you know who Jane is?"


Kirby didn't even hesitate.


His hand tightened around hers.


And he looked directly at her.


Another nod.


Jane felt her chest ache.


Scott noticed.


But moved on.


"Do you know who I am?"


A nod.


Stronger this time.


Scott smiled.


"Good."


Another.


"That makes things easier."


The questioning continued.


Could he follow movement?


Could he squeeze a hand?


Could he move his feet?


Could he understand what was being said?


Everything looked encouraging.


Everything looked promising.


A few minutes later Nurse Hayle entered quietly.


Her expression immediately softened when she saw him awake.


"Well."


Another.


"It's about time."


A tired smile appeared on Kirby's face.


He recognized her too.


Not completely.


But enough.


Enough to trust her.


Hayle carefully helped him take a few small sips of water.


The relief on his face was immediate.


Jane almost laughed.


Such a small thing.


Such a normal thing.


And somehow it felt miraculous.


A few minutes later Scott seemed satisfied.


For now.


"Okay."


Another.


"You're doing great."


Another.


"Now I'm going to let you rest."


Kirby immediately looked toward Jane.


Then toward the door.


The concern was obvious.


Scott noticed.


"She's not going anywhere."


The reassurance visibly relaxed him.


A few moments later Scott and Hayle stepped out.


Leaving Jane and Kirby alone again.


The room fell quiet.


Peaceful.


For the first time since he'd opened his eyes.


Jane looked at him.


Really looked at him.


Alive.


Awake.


Here.


Slowly she reached up and ran her hand through his hair.


The familiar gesture immediately softened his expression.


Then she gently touched his face.


Kirby smiled.


Small.


Weak.


But real.


Relief flooded his features.


As though the simple fact that she was there made everything else bearable.


He closed his eyes for a moment.


Just grateful.


Just resting in the comfort of her presence.


Jane immediately started crying again.


Happy tears.


Heartbroken tears.


Everything tears.


"You did really great."


Kirby's eyes opened again.


Watching her.


Another smile.


"You need to rest."


Another.


"And I know what you're thinking."


The corner of his mouth twitched.


That familiar look.


The one that told her he was already disagreeing with her.


"You're tired of resting."


Another.


"But you have to get better."


Another.


"You have to let your body catch up."


Her voice softened.


"Your dad really wants to see you."


Another.


"And Andrew."


Another.


"Scott can only hold them back for so long."


That finally earned a genuine smile.


Tiny.


But there.


Jane laughed softly through her tears.


"See?"


Another.


"I'll wait until you're ready."


Kirby's eyes filled with gratitude.


Then he gave her that look.


The look.


The one she had been afraid of.


The one full of trust.


Love.


Relief.


The one that made her heart ache.


Jane put her own feelings aside.


For him.


For now.


She squeezed his hand.


"I'm not going anywhere."


Another.


"I'll be right here beside you."


Another.


"Close your eyes."


Then she leaned down.


And kissed the top of his head.


The gesture familiar.


Comforting.


Safe.


Almost immediately Kirby relaxed.


His eyes closing.


His grip finally loosening.


Trusting her completely.


Within minutes he was asleep again.


Jane waited.


Made sure he was really resting.


Made sure he was breathing evenly.


Made sure he was okay.


Then she turned away.


And completely fell apart.


The sob escaped before she could stop it.


Then another.


And another.


The door opened immediately.


Scott hurried back into the room.


Concern written all over his face.


"Jane."


She couldn't speak.


Couldn't stop crying.


Scott gently guided her out into the hallway.


Away from the bed.


Away from Kirby.


And wrapped his arms around her.


The same way he had her entire life.


"It's okay."


Another.


"Honey, it's okay."


Another.


"You did amazing."


Another.


"It's okay."


Jane buried her face against his shoulder.


Still crying.


Still carrying the weight of everything that had just happened.


But even through the tears she knew one thing.


She had made Kirby a promise.


And Jane McFly did not break promises.


She wasn't leaving his side.


Not until she knew he was going to be okay.
