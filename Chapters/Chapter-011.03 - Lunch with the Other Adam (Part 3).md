# Chapter 11: Lunch with the Other Adam (Part 3)

Jane studied him carefully.

And for the first time since meeting him, she felt afraid.

Not of him.

For him.

Because the thing she'd been worried about since yesterday was already happening.

She liked him.

The realization settled heavily in her chest.

They had been sitting there for over an hour.

Talking.

Arguing.

Trading sarcastic comments.

And somewhere along the way she'd stopped watching the clock.

Stopped counting the minutes.

For the first time since Adam disappeared, she had spent an entire hour without thinking about her friend.

That realization terrified her.

Because she wasn't supposed to like him.

She was supposed to miss Adam.

Instead, she found herself enjoying the company of the one person she never expected to understand.

The one person she'd never wanted to meet.

The one person she was already starting to care about.

The thought made her stomach twist.


Suddenly, Adam's eyes shifted past her shoulder.

The amusement disappeared from his face immediately.

Every trace of confidence vanished.

Jane had never seen him panic before.

Until now.


"Jane."

His voice was sharp.

Urgent.

"Don't turn around."


The seriousness in his tone immediately put her on edge.


"Why?"


His eyes remained fixed on something behind her.


"Andrew just walked into the restaurant."


Jane's heart stopped.


"He is with a girl who looks like she belongs on the cover of a magazine."

A pause.

"I'm assuming that's the lawyer girlfriend."


The blood drained from Jane's face.


"Oh my God."


She gripped the edge of the table.


"Adam, please tell me you're joking."


He wasn't.

She could see it in his eyes.


The panic she'd spent months avoiding came rushing back all at once.


"Okay."

Adam leaned forward immediately.


"Listen to me."


Jane barely heard him.


"He can't see me."


Her hands started shaking.


Not now.

Not today.

Not when she'd finally had a good hour.

Not when she'd finally managed to breathe.


"Jane."

Adam's voice became firmer.


"You cannot panic."


Too late.


"I can get you out of here."


The room was already narrowing around her.


"But you need to listen to me and do exactly what I say."


Jane heard the words.

She just couldn't process them.


Andrew was here.


With Kelly.


At the same restaurant.


Her chest tightened.

Breathing became difficult.


"Take my jacket."


Adam was already taking it off.


"Put it over your shoulders."


Jane didn't move.


The world had become distant.

Muffled.


She wasn't really hearing him anymore.


Adam immediately recognized the look.


He'd seen it before.


Not on Jane.


On Adam.


He remembered sitting helplessly in the back of Adam's mind for years.

Watching panic take over.

Calling out his name.

Trying to bring him back.

Sometimes succeeding.

Sometimes failing.


It was the same look.


Two nights earlier he'd watched it happen all over again.

The emergency room.

The little boy.

The code.

The collapse.


Now he was watching panic steal someone else away.


Someone Adam loved.


Because he knew the truth now.


Somewhere along the way, Adam had fallen in love with Jane.


The realization had become impossible to ignore.


Adam worried about her too much.

Thought about her too much.

Felt guilty about Andrew.

Looked forward to seeing her.

Missed her when she wasn't around.


He loved her.


And because Adam loved her, part of him loved her too.


That was the unfair reality of it.


He felt what Adam felt.

Carried what Adam carried.

Protected what Adam protected.


And right now Jane needed protecting.


He moved around the table.


Wrapped his jacket around her shoulders himself.


Then placed both hands firmly on her shoulders.


"Jane."


Nothing.


"Jane, look at me."


Her eyes remained unfocused.


"Jane, come back to me."


Still nothing.


For a moment he considered his options.


Then made a decision.


One she would absolutely hate later.


He cupped her face.


And kissed her.


Jane's eyes widened immediately.


The world snapped back into focus.


She stared directly at him.


Confused.

Shocked.

Fully present.


Relief washed across his face.


"There you are."


Jane looked like she might actually murder him.


"You—"


"You can be mad about the kiss later."


His tone was matter-of-fact.


"For the record, I had no choice."


She continued staring.


He pointed toward the exit.


"Now follow my lead."


A pause.


"And don't say anything."


Before she could argue, he slipped an arm around her shoulders.


"Put your head against my chest."


Jane glared.


"Adam."


"Trust me."


To her surprise, she did.


They started moving toward the exit.


Unfortunately, there was no avoiding it.


Andrew spotted them almost immediately.


"Adam!"


The Other Adam didn't miss a beat.


"Hey Andrew."


He smiled casually.

The kind of smile Jane was beginning to realize he only produced when necessary.


"Good to see you."


Then he glanced toward Kelly.


"That must be your girlfriend."


He extended a hand.


"Nice to meet you."


Kelly smiled politely.


The Other Adam tightened his arm slightly around Jane.


"I'm sorry."


He nodded toward her.


"My girlfriend is sick and I need to get her home."


Jane nearly choked.


"Can we catch up tomorrow at the hospital?"


Andrew immediately nodded.


"Yeah, of course."


His attention shifted toward Jane.

Though thankfully not long enough to truly recognize her.


"See you tomorrow."


A pause.


"Hope she feels better."


"Thanks."


The Other Adam didn't stop moving.


He guided Jane straight out of the restaurant.

Past the hostess stand.

Past the outdoor patio.

Out into the sunlight.


Only once they stepped through the doors did either of them breathe again.


That was when they nearly walked straight into Karen.


Karen stopped mid-stride.


"Dr. Adam?"


Of all the possible moments.

Of all the possible places.

Of all the possible people.


Karen had picked exactly the wrong time to show up.


The Other Adam looked at her urgently.


"Don't say a word."


Karen blinked.


"Walk with us."


Now she was officially concerned.


"I have to get her away from here."


He glanced back toward the restaurant.


"Andrew is inside with his lawyer girlfriend."


Karen's eyes widened.


"Jane is having a panic attack."


Karen immediately understood.


No further explanation needed.


Without another question, she fell into step beside them.

And together they headed toward the park.
