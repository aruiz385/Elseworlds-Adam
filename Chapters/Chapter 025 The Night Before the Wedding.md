# Chapter 25: The Night Before the Wedding

The night before they left for Karen and James' wedding, Jane and Adam sat in bed at his apartment.

The television was off.

The city lights glowed beyond the window.

Neither of them seemed particularly interested in sleeping.


Adam had been watching her for a while.

Watching the way she drifted into thought.

Watching the way she seemed different lately.

Stronger somehow.

Lighter.


She'd finally done it.

She'd opened the doors.

Both of them.


The room she used to share with Andrew.

The room she used to share with Kirby.


For years they had existed untouched.

Frozen.


Now she was turning one into a nursery.

The other into an actual bedroom.

A room she could walk into without feeling like she was trespassing inside a memory.


Adam was proud of her.

Incredibly proud of her.


But he was worried too.


"Why now?" he finally asked.


Jane looked over.

"What?"


"The apartment."


A pause.


"The rooms."


Another.


"All of it."


Jane smiled softly.


"Dr. Ryan."


Adam laughed.


"That's becoming the answer to everything."


"It kind of is."


She leaned back against the headboard.


"She keeps redirecting me."


"Toward what?"


"Planning."


A pause.


"Building things instead of catastrophizing things."


That sounded exactly like Dr. Ryan.


Jane looked toward the window.


"I want the baby to have a place to come home to."


Her hand rested unconsciously against her stomach.


"A real home."


Adam reached for her hand.


"You know my place is an option."


Jane gave him a look.


"Adam."


"What?"


"You have seen your apartment."


"I like my apartment."


"You can barely fit yourself in your apartment."


He laughed.


"Fair."


She squeezed his hand.


"It makes more sense to use mine."


Then her expression changed.


"And you know why."


Adam nodded.


Because suddenly they weren't talking about square footage anymore.


They were talking about him.

And the other Adam.

And the future neither of them could stop thinking about.


Jane looked down at their joined hands.


"If he comes back..."


Adam already knew.


They never had to specify which Adam anymore.


"I want a plan."


Adam frowned slightly.


"A plan?"


She nodded.


"I've been reading."


Adam groaned.


"There it is."


Jane laughed.


"What?"


"That sentence terrifies me."


"I'm serious."


"I know you are."


She shifted closer.


"I need you to tell me what to do."


The room became quiet.


"If Original Adam comes back..."


A pause.


"...what do you want me to do?"


Adam was silent for a few moments.

Then:


"I kept making the videos."


Jane blinked.


"The updates?"


He nodded.


"Every few weeks."


A pause.


"Five minutes."


Another.


"Highlights."


Jane stared.


"You kept doing them?"


Adam smiled softly.


"There are over two and a half years of them now."


Her eyes immediately filled.


"Adam..."


He shrugged.


"What was I supposed to do?"


A pause.


"If he comes back, he's going to need someone to explain what happened."


Another.


"So I did."


Jane squeezed his hand.


"What do you tell him?"


Adam smiled.


"The important things."


A pause.


"People."


Another.


"The really stupid things Chris says."


That earned a laugh.


"Karen and James."


Another.


"Andrew and Kelly."


His smile softened.


"You."


The room fell quiet.


"And now the baby."


Adam nodded.


"And now the baby."


He took a breath.


"You're right."


A pause.


"We need a plan."


Another.


"It would be irresponsible not to."


Jane nodded.


"So tell me."


Adam thought carefully.


"If I feel it coming..."


A pause.


"I'll tell you."


"You promise?"


"I promise."


Another pause.


"If he comes back..."


Another.


"Let him watch the videos."


Another.


"All of them."


Another.


"Don't skip anything."


Jane nodded.


"Okay."


"Don't force friendship."


Another.


"Don't force history."


Another.


"And don't force a relationship."


The words hurt.


"I wouldn't."


"I know."


The room grew quiet.


Then Adam looked away.


"There is something else."


Immediately Jane knew this was the part he'd been avoiding.


"What?"


Adam exhaled slowly.


"He loved you."


Jane froze.


"What?"


"My other self."


A pause.


"He loved you."


Jane stared at him.


"No."


"Yeah."


Adam smiled sadly.


"He fell in love with you the first week."


The room went completely still.


"He told me once he avoided attachments."


Adam nodded.


"He did."


A pause.


"That doesn't mean he wasn't already in love with you."


Jane looked away.


"He thought you were brilliant."


Another.


"He thought you were impossible."


Jane laughed despite herself.


"That sounds familiar."


"We're the same person."

Adam said gently.


The smile faded.


"He would've taken those feelings to the grave."


Another.


"He didn't want to put another burden on you."


Another.


"You were grieving."


Another.


"You'd already lost enough."


The room was silent.


"And then I showed up."


Adam laughed softly.


"And I just did what he was scared to do."


Jane swallowed hard.


"Why are you telling me this now?"


Adam looked at her carefully.


"Because if he comes back..."


A pause.


"...you need to understand what he's coming back to."


Another.


"When he disappeared he was excited to see you."


Jane's chest tightened.


"The Jane who told him he was the highlight of her week."


Another.


"The Jane he was looking forward to spending more time with."


Adam looked down at his hands.


"And then two and a half years disappear."


Another.


"He comes back and you're not that Jane anymore."


Another.


"You're in love with me."


Another.


"You're going to be a mom."


Another.


"And all the possibilities he never acted on are gone."


Jane felt tears gathering.


"He may feel sad."


Another.


"He may grieve."


Another.


"He may be angry."


"At me?"


"No."


A pause.


"Maybe at me."


Another.


"Maybe at the situation."


Another.


"But he may feel like something was taken from him."


The words hung painfully between them.


"He might watch those videos and feel like he missed his chance."


Another.


"He might feel like I got the life he wanted."


Jane reached for his face.


"Adam..."


He smiled sadly.


"I'm not saying it's fair."


Another.


"I'm saying be ready."


The room was quiet for a long time.


Then Jane whispered:


"I can't love him."


Adam nodded immediately.


"I know."


Another.


"And honestly?"


A pause.


"I wouldn't want you to."


Jane looked relieved.


Adam smiled softly.


"He deserves his own future."


Another.


"The chance to choose who he loves."


Another.


"The chance to choose his own life."


Jane nodded.


"That's exactly how I feel."


A pause.


"I can't put any of this on him."


Another.


"The relationship."


Another.


"The baby."


Another.


"None of it."


Adam nodded.


"I know."


For a while neither of them spoke.


Then Jane said quietly:


"No matter what happens..."


Adam looked up.


"What?"


"Our baby will know about you."


Immediately his eyes filled.


"Jane..."


"They'll know their dad."


Another.


"They'll know how much you loved them."


Another.


"They'll know how much you loved me."


Adam looked away.

Unable to speak for a moment.


Finally he laughed softly and wiped at his eyes.


"After the wedding..."


Jane smiled.


"What?"


"I want to work on the nursery."


A grin spread across her face.


"Of course."


"I thought of something."


"A quote."


"What quote?"


Adam smiled.

A little sadly.

A little hopefully.


"If ever there is tomorrow when we're not together..."


His voice softened.


"...you are braver than you believe, stronger than you seem, and smarter than you think."


Jane immediately started crying.


"Adam..."


"I want it on the wall."


A pause.


"For our baby."


Jane reached up and cupped his face.


"Then that's exactly what we're putting on the wall."


For a moment neither spoke.


Then Adam smiled at her.


"You know..."


"What?"


"I'm really proud of you."


The statement caught her off guard.


"What?"


"I'm proud of you."


A pause.


"Do you have any idea how far you've come?"


Jane immediately laughed.


"Adam."


"No."


He squeezed her hand.


"I'm serious."


The smile faded.


"When I met you..."


A pause.


"You were hurting."


Another.


"You were scared."


Another.


"You were having panic attacks."


Another.


"You were carrying the whole world by yourself."


Jane looked away.


Because she remembered.


The grief.

The anxiety.

The loneliness.


Adam continued softly.


"I worried about you all the time."


A small laugh escaped her.


"You still worry about me."


"Less."


That surprised her.


Adam nodded.


"A lot less."


Another.


"Because now I look at you and I see someone confident."


Another.


"Happy."


Another.


"Decisive."


Another.


"You've built a life again."


His eyes drifted toward her stomach.


"And you're going to be an incredible mother."


The tears returned immediately.


"Our baby is so lucky to have you."


Another.


"And honestly?"


A pause.


"I'm not worried about you."


Jane stared at him.


"What?"


"I'm not."


A pause.


"I know that whether I'm here or not..."


His voice cracked slightly.


"...you're going to fight for our baby."


Another.


"And you're going to be okay."


Jane felt her throat tighten.


"I know you."


Another.


"You're tougher than you think you are."


Another.


"And a lot stronger than you give yourself credit for."


Jane reached for his face.


"No."


"What?"


"I wouldn't be who I am if you hadn't shown up when you did."


The room grew quiet.


"You supported me."


Another.


"You believed in me."


Another.


"You pushed me."


Another.


"You made me reconnect with Andrew."


Adam smiled.


"And now look at you."


Jane nodded.


"Exactly."


A pause.


"I have Andrew again."


Another.


"I have Kelly."


Another.


"I have Karen."


Another.


"James."


Another.


"Scott."


A smile appeared.


"I even somehow have Chris."


"Poor you."


That earned a laugh.


Then Jane looked at him seriously.


"Don't ever worry about me."


A pause.


"I mean it."


Another.


"I'm going to be okay."


The words were steady.

Certain.


"I wish I didn't have to lose you."


Her voice broke.


"But I'm going to be okay."


Adam's eyes immediately filled.


Because for the first time he believed it too.


Really believed it.


He pulled her into his arms.


Held her tightly.


Jane curled against him.


For a long time neither spoke.


Finally Adam whispered into her hair:


"I just hope I get to see our baby."


Jane closed her eyes.


"So do I."


A pause.


"I really hope you do."


And for a little while they sat together in the quiet.

Holding each other.

Thinking about tomorrow.

The wedding.

The baby.

The nursery.

And the future they still hoped they had time to share.
