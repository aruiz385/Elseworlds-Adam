# Chapter 22: Orbit (Part 2)

By the time Jane and Kelly returned to the table, the atmosphere had shifted.

Not dramatically.

Just enough.

The sharp edges from earlier had softened.

The tension hadn't disappeared entirely, but it wasn't controlling the evening anymore.


Adam was standing before Jane had even reached the table.


"Jane?"


His eyes immediately found hers.

Checking.

Making sure.


She smiled.

A small one.


"I'm okay."


Adam still looked unconvinced.

Reasonably.

But after a moment he nodded and pulled her chair out for her.


"Let me know if you want to leave."


The offer came quietly.

Without pressure.

Without expectation.


Jane squeezed his hand.


"I know."


Only then did he sit back down.


For a few minutes nobody pushed the conversation.

Nobody wanted to be the first person to reopen whatever had almost happened.


Thankfully, Andrew solved that problem.


"So."


Everyone looked at him.


"I'm blaming Karen."


Adam blinked.


"For what?"


Andrew gestured vaguely.


"Everything."


That earned immediate laughter.


Kelly nodded.


"That's fair."


Jane looked offended.


"The bride isn't even here to defend herself."


"That's because she's busy creating her eighth spreadsheet."


Andrew pointed.


"See? This is what I'm talking about."


Kelly laughed.


"No, seriously. There are spreadsheets for the spreadsheets now."


Adam looked horrified.


"That sounds efficient."


The entire table stared at him.


"What?"


Andrew immediately pointed.


"See? He's one of them."


"What does that mean?"


"It means you're siding with Karen."


"I'm siding with organization."


"Same thing."


Jane laughed.


For the first time all evening, it felt easy again.


Comfortable.


Normal.


The conversation naturally shifted toward wedding planning.

How Andrew was managing James.

How Jane was managing Karen.

Or trying to.


"She called me at six-thirty in the morning."


Andrew nearly dropped his fork.


"What happened?"


Jane deadpanned.


"A centerpiece."


The table went silent.


"A centerpiece."

Adam repeated.


Jane nodded solemnly.


"A centerpiece."


Kelly buried her face in her hands.

Already laughing.


"Please tell me it was on fire."


"No."


"Destroyed?"


"No."


"Missing?"


"No."


Andrew sighed.


"The flowers."


Jane pointed.


"The hydrangeas looked sad."


The table erupted.


Andrew laughed so hard he had tears in his eyes.


Adam looked genuinely concerned.


"And this required a six-thirty phone call?"


"Apparently."


Andrew shook his head.


"James isn't doing much better."


That got everyone's attention.

Because James was the calm one.

Always.


"What happened?"

Jane asked.


Andrew took a drink.


"He sent me a twelve-paragraph text."


Adam blinked.


"No."


"Yes."


Kelly immediately looked delighted.


"About what?"


"Seating arrangements."


The table exploded again.


"Karen broke him."

Andrew announced proudly.


"That's actually impressive."

Adam admitted.


The laughter lingered.

Longer this time.


And gradually, without anyone forcing it, Adam and Kelly started talking.

At first it was just comments.

Then observations.

Then suddenly they were having their own conversation.


"You run?" Kelly asked.


Adam nodded.


"When I have time."


"I didn't expect that."


"Why?"


Kelly smiled.


"Doctor stereotype."


Adam laughed.


"That's offensive."


"But accurate."


"Unfortunately."


They discovered they liked many of the same books.

Had very similar opinions about traveling.

Both preferred quiet weekends over crowded social events.

And both secretly thought Karen's organizational skills were terrifying.


Kelly pointed at him.


"You're one of those people."


Adam narrowed his eyes.


"What people?"


"The healthy people."


Jane immediately burst out laughing.


"Oh, now you see it."


Kelly looked delighted.


"There it is."


Andrew nodded.


"Welcome to our world."


Adam looked betrayed.


"I thought we were having a nice conversation."


"We are."

Kelly assured him.


"We're just learning things."


For the first time all evening, Jane found herself sitting back and simply watching.


Adam and Kelly got along.

Not in a strange way.

Not in a threatening way.

Just...

normally.


And oddly enough, that made her feel better.

Not worse.


Across the table Andrew seemed to notice it too.


Their eyes met briefly.


Neither said anything.


Neither needed to.


A year ago this dinner would've been impossible.


Six months ago it would've ended in disaster.


Yet somehow they were here.

Talking about wedding centerpieces.

Making fun of James.

Watching Adam defend vegetables.


Life was strange.


The evening continued like that for another hour.

Easy.

Comfortable.

Unexpectedly good.


Eventually the check arrived.

Goodbyes began.

The night was winding down.


Andrew looked entirely too pleased with himself.


"I told you."


"Oh, don't start."

Jane replied immediately.


"I told you."


Adam was openly enjoying this.


"This is my favorite part."


"Of course it is."


Outside, the bay reflected the city lights.

The same view Adam and Jane had stared at months earlier during what was definitely a first date.

Regardless of what Jane claimed.


As everyone began saying goodbye, Kelly pulled Jane into a brief hug.

Simple.

Unexpected.

Warm.


"We should do this again sometime."


Jane blinked.

Then smiled.


"Let's not get carried away."


Kelly laughed.


"Fair."


They pulled apart.

Then Kelly's expression softened.


"And Jane?"


Jane looked up.


"Yeah?"


Kelly hesitated.

Only briefly.


"Take care of yourself."


Jane smiled.


"I will."


Kelly shook her head gently.


"No."


A small smile appeared.


"I mean it."


The seriousness in her voice caught Jane off guard.


"I know."


Kelly studied her for another moment.


"And talk to Adam."


The statement felt oddly specific.


"What?"


"You haven't been feeling great lately."


A pause.


"Don't keep that to yourself."


The memory of the restroom conversation came rushing back.

The nausea.

The questions.

The look Kelly had given her.


*Have you been nauseous?*

*Usually mornings?*

*Maybe you should take a test.*


Kelly wasn't really talking about stress.

Both of them knew it.


But neither said it out loud.


Instead Kelly squeezed her arm gently.


Then stepped back beside Andrew.


Across the parking lot Adam was saying something to Andrew that had both men laughing.


The sight immediately made Jane smile.


Kelly noticed.


"He's a good one."


Jane looked toward Adam.

The warmth in her chest immediate.

Effortless.

Certain.


"The best."


Kelly nodded.


"I figured."


A moment later Adam was beside her again.

His hand finding hers automatically.

The way it always did.


"You ready?"


Jane glanced back once.

At Kelly.

At Andrew.

At the strange dinner she'd been certain would be a disaster.


Instead it had been complicated.

Emotional.

Unexpected.

And somehow...

good.


"Yeah."

She squeezed his hand.


"I'm ready."


As they walked toward the car, Adam glanced down at her.


"You sure you're okay?"


Jane smiled.


"I'm okay."


Adam didn't look entirely convinced.


Neither was she.


But for tonight, that answer would have to be enough.
