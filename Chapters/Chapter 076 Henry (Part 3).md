# Chapter 76: Henry (Part 3)


The door opened again.


And Adam walked in carrying Lizzy.


Immediately Karen lit up.


"There's my girl!"


Lizzy looked around.


Saw Karen.


And squealed.


Karen laughed.


"Oh, come here."


Adam carefully handed her over.


Karen settled Lizzy against her chest with surprising ease for someone who had given birth only a few hours earlier.


"Hi sweetheart."


Lizzy immediately grabbed one of Karen's fingers.


Karen's entire face softened.


"Yep."


Another.


"Still perfect."


James laughed.


"You've known her for six months."


"Exactly."


Karen looked offended.


"I've had plenty of time to confirm."


Adam walked over and shook James's hand.


Firmly.


"Congratulations."


James smiled.


"Thanks."


Adam nodded.


Then smiled.


"Welcome to fatherhood."


Another.


"Where nights are long."


Another.


"Sleep is barely existent."


Another.


"But love is endless."


James laughed.


"That sounds terrifying."


"It is."


Adam replied.


Another.


"It's also the best thing I've ever done."


Then he leaned down and hugged Karen carefully.


"Hey, Mom."


Karen smiled immediately.


"Hey."


"Congratulations."


Another.


"I hear a welcome-home party is already being planned."


Karen narrowed her eyes at Jane.


"Of course she started planning my party before I even delivered the placenta."


Jane looked pleased with herself.


Adam laughed.


Karen's gaze drifted toward him.


"You really should stop flirting with me."


Adam smiled.


Then leaned down and hugged her again.


"Never."


Karen immediately blushed.


Then looked over at James.


"See?"


Another.


"Like I've told you."


Another.


"Step up your game."


The room burst out laughing.


James just smiled and shook his head.


Watching his wife's antics.


Loving every second of it.


Jane rolled her eyes.


Honestly.


Some things never changed.


Adam moved toward the couch and leaned down, kissing the top of Jane's head.


"Hey you."


Jane smiled.


"Hey."


"How does it feel to be a godmother?"


Jane's smile faltered.


Just slightly.


"Honestly?"


Another.


"A little traumatic."


Another.


"And completely amazing."


Adam's expression softened immediately.


The response told him everything.


Without telling him anything.


"We can talk later."


Jane nodded.


"Yeah."


Another.


"I think we should."


He squeezed her shoulder and sat beside her.


A few moments later James carefully handed Henry to Adam.


Adam blinked.


"Really?"


James nodded.


"Really."


Another.


"You're his honorary uncle."


Another.


"Seems appropriate."


Adam laughed.


"Fair enough."


Then he carefully accepted Henry.


Possibly more carefully than any chemotherapy treatment he'd ever administered.


Jane immediately narrowed her eyes.


Suspicious.


Very suspicious.


Adam looked down at Henry.


Tiny.


Perfect.


Asleep.


His entire expression melted.


Jane pointed immediately.


"Don't."


Adam looked up.


Innocent.


"What?"


"Don't even think about it."


The room laughed.


Adam looked offended.


"I have no idea what you're talking about."


"Sure."


Jane replied.


"Absolutely no idea."


Adam glanced down at Henry again.


Then back at Jane.


The look on his face made her groan.


The entire room noticed.


Even Karen.


"Oh my God."


Karen pointed.


"He wants another one."


James immediately started laughing.


Adam wisely remained silent.


Which somehow made it worse.


Jane looked at him.


Adam looked at Henry.


Then at Jane.


Then back at Henry.


Jane pressed her lips together.


"You are impossible."


Adam smiled.


A smile that suggested absolutely nothing.


And absolutely everything.


When Henry was safely returned to Karen, Andrew stepped forward.


Taking Lizzy from her almost automatically.


The little girl settled comfortably into his arms.


Like she belonged there.


Which, in many ways, she did.


Andrew kissed the top of her head.


"You and I have a pending playdate, Miss Lizzy."


Lizzy immediately reached for his face.


Andrew laughed.


"I know."


Another.


"I missed you too."


Jane watched from across the room.


Her heart softening.


Because Andrew loved her daughter so effortlessly.


Because Adam loved her daughter just as completely.


Because somehow this strange life had given Lizzy far more people to love her than Jane had ever imagined possible.


"You're smiling."


Adam murmured beside her.


Jane looked over.


"What?"


Adam nodded toward Andrew.


"You get that look whenever he's holding her."


Jane laughed softly.


"Maybe."


Another.


"She's lucky."


Adam followed her gaze.


Watching Andrew with Lizzy.


Watching James with Henry.


Watching Karen beam at both babies.


"Yeah."


He smiled.


"She is."


For a while nobody said much.


The room simply existed.


Filled with laughter.


Babies.


Family.


And love.


Exactly the way moments like this were supposed to be.


Eventually James looked around.


"How did we end up here?"


Karen laughed.


"What do you mean?"


James gestured toward everyone.


"Half these people weren't supposed to know each other."


Another.


"Now they're basically family."


Jane smiled.


Because he wasn't wrong.


None of it had gone according to plan.


And somehow that had made it perfect.


Then inspiration struck.


Dangerous inspiration.


Jane sat up.


"Oh."


Adam immediately groaned.


"No."


"Oh yes."


Jane pointed toward Karen.


"You'll never guess who we met today."


Karen narrowed her eyes.


"Why do I feel like I'm about to hear something ridiculous?"


Andrew visibly tensed.


James noticed immediately.


"Oh?"


Jane's grin widened.


"Ally Miller."


James froze.


Completely.


Karen's eyes widened.


Andrew closed his eyes.


Immediately.


"No."


James pointed.


"Not THE Ally Miller."


"The one and only."


Jane replied.


James looked at Andrew.


Then back at Jane.


Then back at Andrew.


"No way."


Jane nodded enthusiastically.


"Oh yes."


James laughed.


"Holy hell."


Another.


"Now there's a name I haven't heard in decades."


Karen leaned forward.


Interested immediately.


"The Ally Miller?"


James nodded.


"Beautiful."


Another.


"Smart."


Another.


"Friendly."


Another.


"Basically perfect."


He paused.


Then looked directly at Andrew.


"Is she single?"


The room exploded.


Andrew buried his face in his hands.


"James."


"What?"


"I'm just asking."


Jane looked entirely too pleased.


"I honestly don't know."


Another.


"But trust me."


Another.


"I'm absolutely finding out."


Karen gasped dramatically.


"You got her number?"


"I did."


Jane nodded.


Another.


"We're getting brunch."


Karen immediately pointed to herself.


"When I'm cleared to leave this house, I'm coming."


"Obviously."


Jane replied.


Then she looked over at Andrew.


A small smile tugging at the corner of her mouth.


Warm.


Playful.


Hopeful.


Andrew immediately recognized the look.


And exactly what it meant.


"Jane."


He groaned.


"What?"


She asked innocently.


Andrew pointed at her.


"I know that look."


Jane's smile widened.


Karen looked between them.


"Wait."


Another.


"There's a look?"


"Oh, there's definitely a look."


James confirmed.


Andrew shook his head.


"Can we please leave my personal life out of this for five minutes?"


Jane immediately looked at him.


"Oh?"


Another.


"So Ally is part of your personal life now?"


The room exploded.


Andrew closed his eyes.


"Jane."


She looked entirely too pleased with herself.


Adam shook his head.


"You literally handed her that comeback."


James pointed immediately.


"Completely self-inflicted."


Andrew looked around the room.


James.


Karen.


Jane.


Adam.


All of them smiling.


All of them thoroughly enjoying his suffering.


"I hate all of you."


Andrew informed the room.


Karen laughed.


"No you don't."


Andrew looked at Adam for help.


Adam took a long drink of coffee.


"Don't look at me."


Another.


"I don't disagree."


Another.


"And after you left, Kirby filled me in."


Andrew's eyes widened immediately.


"He what?"


Adam grinned.


"First love."


Another.


"First..."


Andrew immediately reached for the nearest tissue box.


Adam started laughing.


"I was going to say first girlfriend."


The smirk on his face suggested otherwise.


The tissue box hit him squarely in the chest.


Adam caught it effortlessly.


James immediately pointed.


"I concur."


Another.


"I fully support the accuracy of Kirby's briefing."


The room erupted.


Andrew looked betrayed.


"James."


James shrugged.


"What?"


Another.


"You know Kirby."


Another.


"The man enjoys details."


Karen laughed so hard tears started running down her cheeks.


Jane looked personally vindicated.


"Thank you, Adam."


"Happy to help."


Andrew pointed at both Adam and James.


"You're terrible."


James nodded.


"That's fair."


Adam shrugged.


"Worth it."


Karen wiped at her eyes.


"Oh, this is incredible."


Jane leaned back, thoroughly enjoying herself.


"It's honestly one of the best things that's happened all week."


Andrew looked around the room one final time.


At James.


At Karen.


At Adam.


At Jane.


At Henry.


At Lizzy.


At the family they'd somehow built.


And despite all the teasing.


Despite all the emotions of the last week.


Despite everything.


He smiled.


Then laughed.


And for the first time in a very long time...


It felt easy.
