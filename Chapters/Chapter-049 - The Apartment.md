# Chapter 49: The Apartment


Jane stood outside her apartment door for several seconds before opening it.


She didn't know what she was walking into.


She didn't know what to say.


She didn't know who she was anymore.


Only one thing felt certain.


Adam had known.


The lock clicked.


The door opened.


The apartment was quiet.


Too quiet.


Then she saw him.


Adam was sitting on the couch.


Lizzy asleep in his arms.


He looked up immediately.


The second he saw her face, he knew.


Andrew told her.


Neither spoke.


Not at first.


Neither knew how.


Finally:


"I'm sorry."


The words left Adam immediately.


Jane froze.


Because of course that was the first thing he would say.


Not hello.


Not are you okay.


Sorry.


Adam carefully stood.


Transferred Lizzy to her bassinet.


Then turned back toward Jane.


"I wanted to tell you."


His voice was quiet.


"I swear I did."


Jane nodded.


Because she believed him.


The horrible thing was...


Now she understood.


"Andrew told me."


Adam closed his eyes.


For a second.


Only a second.


Then nodded.


"I know."


Silence.


Jane looked at him.


Really looked at him.


The exhaustion.


The fear.


The sadness.


The look she'd mistaken for rejection.


Her eyes immediately filled.


"Oh God."


Adam frowned.


"What?"


"You weren't rejecting me."


The realization hit her all over again.


"You looked devastated."


Another.


"And I thought..."


She shook her head.


"I thought you were done."


Adam stared at her.


Then let out a disbelieving laugh.


"Done?"


The word sounded ridiculous.


"Jane."


Another.


"I'm not done."


Another.


"I'm never going to be done."


The certainty in his voice nearly broke her.


Tears spilled instantly.


Adam crossed the room immediately.


Stopping only when he reached her.


Not touching her yet.


Giving her the choice.


The choice he'd been giving her for months.


Jane grabbed him.


Immediately.


Wrapping both arms around him.


And suddenly she was crying again.


The kind of crying that physically hurt.


Adam held her.


Tightly.


As though he could somehow keep her world from collapsing.


"I'm sorry."


She whispered.


Into his chest.


"I'm sorry."


"Jane."


Another.


"You have nothing to apologize for."


She laughed.


A broken sound.


"I told you I loved you and then immediately ran away."


Adam almost smiled.


Almost.


Under different circumstances he might have.


Not tonight.


Not now.


Eventually she pulled back.


Looked up at him.


One question hanging between them.


Neither wanting to ask it.


Finally Jane whispered:


"You thought the same thing, didn't you?"


Adam's face fell.


Immediately.


Because he knew exactly what she meant.


She was still married.


Kirby was alive.


Everything had changed.


And for the first time all night Adam looked away.


"I don't know what happens now."


The honesty hurt.


But she appreciated it.


Because she didn't know either.


Neither of them did.


After a moment she reached for his hand.


Adam looked up.


Jane squeezed it.


"I'm still here."


His eyes closed.


Briefly.


Because hearing it almost hurt.


Almost.


"I know."


For a moment neither spoke.


Then Jane glanced toward Lizzy.


Their daughter.


Sleeping peacefully.


Completely unaware that the world had shifted.


"Andrew's arranging people."


Adam nodded.


"To watch Lizzy."


Another.


"Then we're going to the hospital."


Adam swallowed hard.


"Okay."


The answer sounded simple.


Nothing about this was simple.


Meanwhile upstairs, Andrew stared at his phone.


Dreading the next call.


More than Jane's.


More than Adam's.


More than anyone's.


Kelly.


His thumb hovered over her name.


For several seconds.


Because this wasn't just Kirby for Jane.


It wasn't even just Kirby for him.


It was Kirby for Kelly.


The love of her life.


The person she'd spent years grieving.


The person she'd slowly learned to live without.


The person she'd once admitted she never completely stopped loving.


And now she was five years into building a life with Andrew.


Five years.


A home.


A future.


A relationship neither of them had expected to survive as long as it had.


Andrew closed his eyes.


Then pressed call.


Kelly answered almost immediately.


"Hey."


The moment she heard his silence, her voice changed.


"Andrew?"


He couldn't speak.


Not right away.


Because suddenly he realized something.


Jane wasn't the only person whose world had just shattered.


Kelly's was about to shatter too.


And for the second time that night, Andrew found himself trying to figure out how to tell someone that a dead man wasn't dead anymore.
