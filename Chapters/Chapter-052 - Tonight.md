# Chapter 52: Tonight


By the time Jane and Adam got home it was dark.


The apartment felt different.


Quieter.


Smaller.


Safer.


For the first time all day, Jane stopped moving.


Stopped answering questions.


Stopped making decisions.


Stopped pretending she was okay.


Then she heard Lizzy.


A soft happy noise coming from the living room.


Immediately her heart tightened.


When they stepped inside, Melissa was sitting on the couch holding her granddaughter.


Marty sat beside them.


Talking quietly.


The second Melissa looked up and saw Jane, her expression changed.


Because suddenly she wasn't looking at the successful woman her daughter had become.


Or the project manager trusted with impossible deadlines.


Or the new mother.


Or the woman everyone seemed to depend on.


She was looking at her little girl.


And her little girl looked exhausted.


"Jane."


Melissa's eyes immediately filled.


Without hesitation she stood.


Handed Lizzy to Adam.


And crossed the room.


The second her mother wrapped her arms around her, Jane broke.


"Oh honey."


Melissa kissed her temple.


"Are you okay?"


Jane shook her head.


Tears falling immediately.


No pretending.


No brave face.


No carefully constructed answer.


Just honesty.


"Oh Mom."


Her voice cracked.


"No."


Another.


"I'm not okay."


Another.


"The last few days have been terrible."


Another.


"I don't know what's happening anymore."


Melissa held her tighter.


The same way she had when Jane was five.


The same way she had when Jane was fifteen.


The same way she always had.


"Sweety."


Melissa's own voice trembled.


"You're going to be okay."


Another.


"You have Lizzy."


Another.


"You have Adam."


Another.


"They'll help you get through this."


Jane nodded against her shoulder.


Trying desperately to believe her.


After a moment Melissa stepped back.


Wiping her eyes.


Marty stood.


Looking equally concerned.


"Jonathan called."


Jane looked up.


Marty nodded.


"He's having a really hard time."


Of course he was.


Kirby's father.


Andrew's father.


A man who buried one son and now somehow had him back.


Or almost back.


"We're going upstairs."


Another.


"Spend some time with him."


Another.


"Keep him company."


Jane nodded.


Nobody should be alone tonight.


Marty stepped closer.


Kissed her forehead.


The same way he had her entire life.


"We'll see you both later tonight."


Then he turned toward Adam.


Shook his hand firmly.


The kind of handshake that said:


Take care of her.


The kind Marty had been giving Adam ever since he realized Adam wasn't going anywhere.


Melissa hugged Adam too.


Then kissed Lizzy's forehead.


Before finally heading toward the door.


A few moments later the apartment became quiet again.


Just Jane.


Adam.


And Lizzy.


Adam sat on the couch with Lizzy sleeping against his chest.


Jane curled up beside him.


For the first time all day they were alone.


Finally:


"How was Dr. Ryan?"


Jane laughed softly.


A tired laugh.


"That's a dangerous question."


Adam smiled.


"So I've been told."


Silence settled between them.


Then Jane sighed.


"We talked about Kirby."


Adam's expression immediately softened.


"I told her it wasn't fair."


Another.


"Being the decision maker."


Another.


"Everyone keeps reminding me I'm his wife."


Another.


"That legally this falls on me."


Her eyes filled.


"But I don't want it to."


Another.


"It should be Andrew."


Another.


"He's Andrew's brother."


Another.


"He knows him better than anyone."


Adam listened quietly.


"But instead everybody keeps looking at me."


Another.


"And if something goes wrong..."


Her voice cracked.


"What if I tell them to wake him up and he dies?"


Another.


"What if I lose him twice?"


Adam reached for her hand.


Immediately.


"You aren't making him live or die."


Another.


"You're giving him a chance."


Jane nodded.


She knew that.


Logically.


Emotionally was another story.


After a moment she whispered:


"I'm scared he'll wake up."


Adam looked at her.


"I know."


Another.


"What if he thinks no time passed?"


Another.


"What if he still thinks we're married?"


The tears returned.


"What do I do then?"


Another.


"We were married for twenty-four hours."


Another.


"Twenty-four hours."


Another.


"And seven years later I'm making life-or-death decisions on his behalf."


Another.


"How is that fair?"


Adam rubbed his thumb across her hand.


"It isn't."


The answer surprised her.


Because nobody had said it.


Not Scott.


Not Andrew.


Not the hospital.


Adam.


Just Adam.


"It isn't fair."


Another.


"But it is real."


Another.


"And we'll deal with it when we get there."


After a few moments Jane spoke again.


"We talked about Andrew too."


Adam remained quiet.


Listening.


Not threatened.


Just listening.


"The memories keep coming back."


Another.


"The beach."


Another.


"The baby."


Another.


"Everything."


She looked down.


"I spent years working through all of that."


Another.


"And now it feels like it's all sitting right in front of me again."


Adam wrapped an arm around her shoulders.


Gently.


She leaned into him.


"The thing is..."


Another.


"I'm not confused."


Adam looked at her.


Jane's eyes met his.


"I know who I love."


Another.


"I love you."


Another.


"I love Lizzy."


Another.


"I love our life."


Tears slipped down her cheeks.


"I love what we're building."


Another.


"I've never been more sure of anything."


Another.


"I hope you believe that."


Adam nodded immediately.


Because he did.


With every part of him.


Jane laughed bitterly.


"Who wants to be with a married person?"


Another.


"I never thought I'd have to say that."


Another.


"Not in a million years."


She wiped at her eyes.


"And honestly?"


Another.


"I wouldn't blame you if you decided this was too much."


Adam stared at her.


For several long moments.


Then stood.


Carefully lifting Lizzy from his chest.


Jane frowned.


"What are you doing?"


Adam shifted Lizzy against his shoulder.


"Getting ready."


"For what?"


Instead of answering, he walked over to the coat closet.


He grabbed his jacket.


Then reached for her sweater.


The oversized one she always wore when she was stressed.


The one he knew she loved.


He carried it back to her.


"Put this on."


Jane blinked.


"What?"


Adam glanced down at Lizzy.


Already checking to make sure she was warm enough.


"We need a break."


Another.


"A real one."


Another.


"Not a hospital cafeteria break."


Another.


"Not a five minute cry in a bathroom break."


Despite herself, Jane laughed.


Adam smiled.


"A time out."


Another.


"From all of this."


He adjusted Lizzy's blanket.


Carefully.


Making sure her little feet were covered.


Then reached for the heavier blanket folded over the couch.


Bundling their daughter securely.


The way he always did.


Like she was the most important thing in the world.


Which she was.


Jane watched him.


Her chest aching.


Because she loved this man.


So much.


Adam finished covering Lizzy.


Then looked up.


"One hour."


Another.


"That's all I'm asking."


Another.


"No hospitals."


Another.


"No decisions."


Another.


"No thinking about tomorrow."


Jane shook her head.


"I don't think that's possible."


Adam walked over.


Brushed a strand of hair behind her ear.


"Then think about it less."


His voice softened.


"Jane."


Another.


"You've been carrying everybody."


Another.


"Andrew."


Another.


"Kelly."


Another.


"Me."


Another.


"Kirby."


Another.


"You don't have to carry us for the next hour."


The tears immediately returned.


Adam smiled sadly.


Then nodded toward Lizzy.


"For the next hour..."


Another.


"I want you to focus on her."


Then gently touched her cheek.


"And you."


Jane swallowed hard.


"Adam..."


He kissed her forehead.


Tenderly.


"Come on."


Another.


"Let's take our daughter for a walk."


Another.


"The world can wait an hour."


And a few minutes later the three of them were walking through the park together.


For the first time in days...


Just a family.
