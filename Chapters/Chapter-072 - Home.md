# Chapter 72: Home


By the time Jane got home, she was emotionally exhausted.


The apartment was quiet.


Dark except for the small lamp glowing in the corner of the living room.


Jane stepped inside and immediately stopped.


Adam was asleep on the couch.


Lizzy was sprawled across his chest.


Tiny arms wrapped around him.


One of Adam's hands resting protectively against her back even in his sleep.


For a moment Jane simply stood there.


Watching them.


Watching the life she'd built.


The life she'd fought for.


The life she'd nearly lost so many times.


And somehow that made her cry harder.


Fresh tears slid down her face.


Because today had been too much.


Too much joy.


Too much grief.


Too much letting go.


Too much being told who she needed to be.


Kirby.


Andrew.


Both of them.


She stared at Adam and Lizzy and felt something ugly creep into her thoughts.


A question.


One she hadn't allowed herself to ask in five weeks.


Was it worth it?


The thought made her feel sick immediately.


Five weeks.


Five weeks of living at the hospital.


Five weeks of barely seeing Adam.


Five weeks of missed bedtimes.


Missed dinners.


Missed nights.


Missed moments.


Five weeks where Adam had picked up every piece she couldn't carry.


Five weeks where Lizzy had spent more time being parented by everyone else than by her.


Had she been wrong?


Should she have said no?


Should she have chosen them first?


The guilt hit all at once.


And Jane hated herself for even thinking it.


A movement on the couch pulled her from the spiral.


Adam's eyes slowly opened.


For a moment he looked confused.


Then he saw her.


Immediately sat up.


And immediately knew something was wrong.


"Jane?"


His voice was still rough from sleep.


Jane tried to smile.


Failed.


Adam's expression changed instantly.


Concern replacing sleepiness.


He carefully lifted Lizzy from his chest.


The little girl barely stirred.


Years of hospital naps had prepared her for almost anything.


Adam carried her down the hallway.


A few moments later he returned.


And found Jane standing exactly where he'd left her.


Still crying.


Still trying unsuccessfully to hold herself together.


"Hey."


He crossed the room immediately.


Wrapping his arms around her.


Jane melted into him.


The way she always did.


The way he always allowed.


For a long moment neither spoke.


Then:


"It was a bad day."


Adam closed his eyes.


Because he'd suspected as much.


"What happened?"


Jane laughed bitterly.


A terrible sound.


Then reached into her bag.


Pulled out the folder.


And handed it to him.


Adam frowned.


Opened it.


Then went completely still.


Divorce papers.


Signed.


Already finalized.


His eyes widened.


"Jane..."


She looked away.


"Kirby figured everything out."


Adam closed the folder.


Carefully.


Waiting.


Letting her tell the story in her own time.


"He told me he knew Lizzy was mine."


Another.


"He figured it out weeks ago."


Another.


"He knew you were her dad."


Another.


"He told me he listened every time you talked about me."


Adam looked down.


Suddenly uncomfortable.


Jane laughed softly.


"He said you gave yourself away."


A tiny smile flickered across Adam's face.


Then vanished.


Because the tears hadn't stopped.


That was the important part.


"What else happened?"


Jane looked down.


"He thanked me."


Another.


"For taking care of him."


Another.


"He told me I lived up to being his wife."


Her voice cracked.


Then:


"He said he wished he'd stayed with Kelly."


Adam froze.


Jane continued.


"He said he wished he'd let Andrew come after me at UCLA."


Another.


"He said he should've picked her."


The words hurt even repeating them.


Adam immediately understood why she'd been crying.


"Jane."


"He probably didn't mean it that way."


Another.


"I know."


Another.


"But it still hurt."


Adam nodded.


Because feelings didn't care about logic.


And Jane looked devastated.


"What happened after that?"


Jane laughed again.


Another broken sound.


"I found Andrew."


Adam already knew he wasn't going to like whatever came next.


She told him everything.


The breakup.


The conversation.


The boundaries.


Hill Dale.


The phone calls.


The apartment.


The fight.


Everything.


Adam listened quietly.


Patiently.


Holding her the entire time.


When she finally finished—


Silence.


Then:


"They're idiots."


Jane blinked.


"What?"


"They're idiots."


Another.


"Both of them."


Despite everything...


A tiny laugh escaped her.


Adam shook his head.


Frustration obvious now.


"Jane."


Another.


"Do you honestly think for one second that you are the problem here?"


She looked away.


That told him everything.


Adam sighed.


Then gently guided her to the couch.


Pulling her down beside him.


"Look at me."


Jane did.


Reluctantly.


"I saw what you did for the last five weeks."


Another.


"I saw every sacrifice."


Another.


"I saw every missed night."


Another.


"I saw every dinner you skipped."


Another.


"I saw you spend every waking moment trying to make sure everyone else was okay."


His voice softened.


"You did the right thing."


Jane felt fresh tears gathering.


"But Lizzy—"


"Is fine."


Adam said immediately.


"And so am I."


Another.


"You didn't fail us."


Another.


"You're allowed to help people you love, Jane."


He brushed tears from her cheeks.


"I'm glad you were there for Kirby."


Another.


"Scott was right."


Another.


"You were exactly who Kirby needed."


Another.


"And I'd make the same choice again tomorrow."


Jane looked down.


Unable to speak.


Adam continued.


"I love you."


The words came easily.


Always.


"I love you."


Another.


"I love Lizzy."


Another.


"And I'm incredibly happy that I get to plan a future with both of you."


He reached over.


Closing her hand around the folder.


"And now?"


Another.


"I get to marry you."


Jane laughed through her tears.


Which was progress.


Small progress.


"But Kirby and Andrew?"


Adam shook his head.


"They're both idiots."


Another.


"Because I can't imagine my life without you."


His jaw tightened.


"If someone spent five weeks helping me, loving me, supporting me..."


Another.


"I certainly wouldn't make them feel like they were the problem."


Jane buried her face in his shoulder.


The tears returning immediately.


Because that was exactly how she'd felt.


The problem.


Something everyone needed from.


Until they didn't.


Adam wrapped his arms around her tighter.


"You're not the problem."


Another.


"Do you hear me?"


Another.


"You're not."


He kissed the top of her head.


"I think they'll come around."


Another.


"I really do."


Another.


"But if they don't?"


He shrugged.


"It's their loss."


Jane wanted to believe him.


She really did.


But her heart hurt too much.


Everything hurt too much.


After several minutes Adam spoke again.


Quietly.


"Go see Dr. Ryan tomorrow."


Jane looked up.


"What?"


"Talk to her."


Another.


"Work through this."


Another.


"You've been carrying a lot."


Another.


"And I think it might help."


Jane nodded slowly.


Because he was probably right.


Eventually she stood.


Walked down the hallway.


And looked into Lizzy's room.


Their daughter was sound asleep.


Completely unaware of the emotional disaster her mother had become.


Jane smiled sadly.


Then leaned down.


And kissed the top of her head.


"I love you."


Lizzy sighed in her sleep.


That was it.


No profound wisdom.


No life-changing insight.


Just a sleepy baby.


Jane found herself smiling anyway.


When she turned around, Adam was waiting in the doorway.


Like always.


He opened his arms.


And Jane walked directly into them.


No hesitation.


No thought.


Adam held her close.


Feeling her shake as silent tears returned.


Feeling her heartbreak.


Feeling her exhaustion.


And wishing he could take it away.


He couldn't.


But he could hold her.


So he did.


Long after the apartment went quiet.


Long after Jane finally stopped talking.


Long after the tears slowed.


Adam held her.


And somewhere in the back of his mind, a decision settled into place.


Tomorrow.


He was going to find Andrew.


Because Adam understood grief.


He understood pain.


He even understood why Andrew thought he needed space.


What he didn't understand—


What he would never understand—


Was making Jane feel unwanted.


And he wasn't about to stand by while the people she loved most made her question her worth.


Not after everything she'd survived.


Not after everything she'd done.


Not if he could help it.
