# Chapter 38: Meeting the Parents

Adam sat alone in the physician lounge.


His coffee had gone cold thirty minutes ago.


He hadn't touched it.


The room was empty except for him.


Which was exactly why he'd come here.


To think.


Or maybe to avoid thinking.


Neither strategy was working.


The door opened.


Andrew stepped inside.


One look at Adam's face.


And he knew.


"Well."


Andrew grabbed a chair.


"That bad?"


Adam laughed.


Not because anything was funny.


Because he was exhausted.


"Jane and I agreed it might be best if I'm not there when her parents arrive."


Andrew blinked.


Then laughed.


Actually laughed.


Adam stared.


"Glad you're enjoying my suffering."


"Oh, I am."


Another laugh.


"Very much."


Adam groaned.


"Andrew."


"What?"


Andrew leaned back.


"You know the biggest difference between you and the other you?"


Adam looked up.


"Enlighten me."


Andrew smiled.


"The other you never took no for an answer."


Adam frowned.


"That's not exactly a good thing."


"Normally?"


Andrew shrugged.


"No."


Another.


"When it came to Jane?"


Another.


"Different story."


Adam looked unconvinced.


Andrew continued.


"The other you knew when Jane was saying no because she actually meant no."


Another.


"And when she was saying no because she was scared."


Another.


"And if it was fear?"


Andrew pointed at him.


"He just did it anyway."


Adam immediately understood.


The parents.


The labor room.


The integration.


All of it.


Andrew smiled.


"Maybe you should take a page out of his book."


Another.


"If you two are the same person."


Another.


"It's probably in there somewhere."


Adam shook his head.


"Jane is right."


"What?"


"You have a disturbing talent for making unreasonable things sound reasonable."


Andrew grinned.


"So I've been told."


At that moment another voice joined them.


"Kid."


Scott appeared in the doorway.


Smiling.


"It's time."


Adam frowned.


"For what?"


Scott and Andrew exchanged a look.


Then Scott answered.


"It's time to meet Lizzy's very overbearing grandparents."


Andrew laughed.


"And two of the people we love most."


Adam sighed.


"Oh God."


The three of them walked toward the elevators.


And before they reached Jane's floor Scott stopped.


A familiar couple was approaching.


"Scott!"


Melissa McFly practically ran toward him.


Marty wasn't far behind.


"How's our girl?"


Scott hugged both of them.


"Your girls are doing great."


Another.


"Mom and baby are both healthy."


Melissa immediately teared up.


"Oh thank God."


Andrew stepped forward.


Melissa pulled him into a hug.


"Honey."


Another.


"It's so good to see you."


"You too, Mrs. M."


Marty hugged Andrew next.


Then both parents turned.


Toward Adam.


And smiled.


Immediately.


"Oh my goodness."


Melissa walked straight toward him.


"Adam."


Before he could react she wrapped him in a hug.


Marty followed a second later.


"Good to finally meet you."


Adam stood frozen.


Completely confused.


"We're so happy to finally meet you in person."


Melissa smiled.


Then everything suddenly clicked.


Video call.


The pregnancy announcement.


Of course.


His other self.


That determined son of a bitch.


He had somehow convinced Jane to call her parents.


And apparently introduced himself.


Without ever mentioning it in any recording.


Adam wasn't sure whether to be impressed or annoyed.


Probably both.


Melissa kept talking.


"It was so wonderful when you and Jane called us."


Another.


"We're still waiting for a visit."


Adam felt like he was learning new information every thirty seconds.


Then Melissa smiled.


"Forgive our daughter."


Another.


"She's a bit commitment-shy."


Marty laughed.


"With good reason."


Melissa nodded.


"But the ring is beautiful."


Adam froze.


The ring.


Of course they knew about the ring.


Because his other self had apparently told them everything except the DID.


"People can be engaged for a long time."


Melissa continued warmly.


"Life doesn't have to be rushed."


Another.


"We're just happy."


Another.


"Thank you for loving our daughter."


Another.


"And giving us our granddaughter."


Adam swallowed.


Hard.


Because for a second he could almost see it.


The life his other self had been building.


The future.


The connections.


The family.


Then he smiled.


A genuine one.


This, he could work with.


"It's really good to see you both."


Another.


"And I'm sorry we never made it up before the baby came."


Another.


"But we'll make plans soon."


Melissa beamed.


"Good."


Adam gestured down the hall.


"Come on."


Another.


"Let's go meet Lizzy."


As they walked, Melissa asked about the name.


Adam explained.


His mother.


Elizabeth.


The story behind it.


The loss.


Melissa immediately understood.


"It must have been hard."


Adam nodded.


"It was."


Another.


"But she would've loved Lizzy."


The conversation shifted.


His father.


Oakland.


His childhood home.


Jack.


By the time they reached the room, the nervousness had faded.


A little.


Adam pushed the door open.


Jane looked up.


Surprised.


He smiled.


Then winked.


"You have visitors."


Jane stared.


Completely confused.


Because only twenty minutes earlier Adam had looked ready to disappear.


Now he was escorting her parents into the room.


Melissa immediately crossed to the bed.


"Baby."


She hugged Jane carefully.


Marty wasn't far behind.


Then Melissa scooped Lizzy into her arms.


And immediately dissolved into grandmother mode.


Adam quietly moved to Jane's side.


Sat beside her bed.


Took her hand.


Jane looked at him.


Still confused.


Still trying to understand what had happened.


Adam squeezed her hand.


And looked back with an expression that said exactly one thing:


*We're definitely comparing notes later.*


Because neither of them had any idea how his other self had managed to arrange all of this.


But somehow...


He had.


The visit lasted almost two hours.


Melissa cried three separate times.


Marty took approximately fifty pictures.


Scott held Lizzy.


Andrew held Lizzy.


Melissa refused to surrender Lizzy.


Eventually, however, the grandparents left.


Promising to return tomorrow.


The room finally became quiet again.


Lizzy slept peacefully beside the bed.


Jane looked over at Adam.


He looked back.


Neither spoke for a moment.


Then:


"What happened?"


Adam laughed.


"That narrows it down."


"My parents."


Another.


"Twenty minutes before they arrived you were prepared to step aside."


Another.


"Then you showed up escorting them into my room."


Adam nodded.


"Andrew."


Jane smiled.


"Of course."


"He told me the other me would've met them."


Another.


"He would've made sure your parents knew him."


Another.


"He would've done it because it mattered to you."


Another.


"And because family mattered to him."


Jane's smile softened.


That sounded exactly right.


Adam continued.


"Then he reminded me that if all this stuff about us becoming one person is true..."


Another.


"Then it's in me too."


Silence settled.


Then Jane sighed.


"I owe you an explanation."


Adam settled back in his chair.


"The video call?"


Jane groaned.


"The video call."


Another.


"I completely forgot about it."


Adam laughed.


"You forgot your parents knew I existed?"


"I had a baby today."


Another.


"I think I'm allowed a mistake."


"Fair."


Another.


"You literally created a human."


Another.


"The standards are very low."


That earned a laugh.


Then Jane grew quiet.


"He thought it mattered."


Adam immediately knew who she meant.


The other Adam.


"He wanted my parents to know."


Another.


"Not because he wanted approval."


Another.


"Not because he wanted attention."


Another.


"He just believed family mattered."


Jane looked toward Lizzy.


"He kept saying that if he wasn't around..."


Another.


"If something happened..."


Another.


"He didn't want Lizzy's father to be some faceless person my parents had never met."


Adam listened quietly.


"He wanted them to know who her dad was."


Another.


"He wanted them to know I wasn't alone."


Another.


"And he wanted them to understand how much he loved me."


The room became still.


"He also knew my parents would be there for me."


Another.


"Especially after Lizzy was born."


Another.


"And he wanted to make sure I had that support."


Adam smiled softly.


That sounded exactly like him.


Or at least...


Like the person he was becoming.


"So we called them."


Jane continued.


"Together."


Another.


"We told them about Lizzy."


Another.


"The engagement."


Another.


"Our plans."


Adam nodded slowly.


"The Oakland plan."


Jane nodded.


"The Oakland plan."


Another.


"The assumption was that when you came back..."


Another.


"You'd want your own life."


Another.


"You'd go home."


Another.


"We never thought you'd stay."


Adam looked toward the bassinet.


Toward his daughter.


Then back to Jane.


"Neither did I."


A sad smile crossed Jane's face.


"Adam."


Another.


"I'm sorry I didn't tell you."


Adam immediately shook his head.


"No."


Another.


"You don't get to apologize."


Jane blinked.


"What?"


"You had major surgery."


Another.


"You pushed a human being into existence."


Another.


"You are officially excused from remembering anything today."


Jane laughed.


Then wiped at her eyes.


"Thank you."


"For what?"


"For not telling my parents."


Adam understood immediately.


The DID.


Jane nodded.


"I'm not sure they'd understand."


Another.


"Honestly..."


Another.


"I'm not sure I understand anymore."


The confession hung heavily between them.


"During labor..."


Jane looked down.


Another.


"I thought I saw him."


Adam froze.


"I really thought he was there."


Another.


"I know how crazy that sounds."


"It doesn't."


Jane looked at him.


Eyes filled with tears.


"I was so sure."


Silence.


And suddenly Adam understood.


The operating room.


Recovery.


The forehead kiss.


The words she'd whispered.


*I love you too.*


She hadn't been talking to him.


At least not completely.


Jane looked away.


"I'm so confused."


Another.


"About everything."


Adam moved closer.


Then gently took her hand.


"I know."


Another.


"And I understand why."


A long pause followed.


Then Adam spoke again.


"But right now..."


Another.


"I need you to focus on Lizzy."


Another.


"Focus on healing."


Another.


"Focus on getting home."


Jane listened.


"When you get home..."


Another.


"I'll help."


Another.


"With Lizzy."


Another.


"With everything."


Her eyes filled immediately.


Adam squeezed her hand.


"I'm not planning on going anywhere."


A tear slipped down her cheek.


"I know."


His voice softened.


"I want to be there."


Another.


"If you'll have me."


Jane nodded.


Immediately.


Without hesitation.


The answer surprised them both.


Adam opened his arms.


Carefully.


Giving her every chance to refuse.


She didn't.


Jane leaned into him.


Resting against his chest.


Adam wrapped his arms around her.


Holding her gently.


Safely.


For a long time neither spoke.


Jane listened to his heartbeat.


And thought about the things she'd said.


The things she'd felt.


The things she no longer understood.


The terrifying part wasn't that she'd told him she loved him.


The terrifying part was that now...


She wasn't entirely sure who she'd been saying it to.


And for the first time in her life...


That answer no longer felt simple.
