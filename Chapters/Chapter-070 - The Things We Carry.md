# Chapter 70: The Things We Carry


The doctors' lounge was unusually quiet.


No televisions.


No pagers screaming.


No residents frantically searching for caffeine.


Just Scott Howard and Andrew Call sitting across from each other with mediocre coffee and entirely too many thoughts.


Scott leaned back in his chair.


Studying him.


"Hey, Kid."


Andrew looked up from his cup.


"Yeah?"


"How are you doing?"


Another.


"You've gone through a lot this week."


Another.


"With your breakup and all."


Andrew let out a slow breath.


For a moment he considered lying.


Then decided Scott would see through it immediately.


"I'm doing better than I expected."


Scott arched an eyebrow.


That answer earned exactly zero points.


Andrew laughed softly.


"Okay."


Another.


"Maybe not."


He looked down at his coffee.


Turning the cup slightly between his hands.


"Ever since my brother came back to life, Kelly's been different."


Another.


"Shaken."


Another.


"And I saw it."


He swallowed.


"I knew she'd loved him in college."


Another.


"I just didn't think that..."


The words fell apart before he could finish them.


Scott nodded.


"You didn't think she'd still be in love with Kirby."


Andrew winced.


"Maybe."


Another.


"I don't know."


Another.


"Maybe part of me always knew."


He laughed once.


The sound hollow.


"You know what's funny?"


Scott immediately looked concerned.


Because nothing good ever followed Andrew asking that question.


"Five years ago Jane broke things off with me because she thought Kelly could give me things she couldn't."


Another.


"A wife."


Another.


"A family."


Another.


"Kids."


Another.


"All those stupid things."


Scott stayed quiet.


Andrew stared into his coffee.


"And here we are."


Another.


"I'm standing in the exact same place I was five years ago."


Another.


"And Jane..."


A smile appeared despite himself.


"Jane has a daughter."


Another.


"A fiancé."


Another.


"A future."


His voice softened.


Makes me wonder."


Scott knew better than to interrupt.


"Maybe if I'd fought harder."


Another.


"Maybe if I'd told her I only wanted those things with her..."


He laughed.


Sarcastically.


Painfully.


"Maybe things would've turned out differently."


The room settled into silence.


Scott stared at him for several long seconds.


Then slowly nodded.


"You know, Kid..."


Another.


"You remind me a lot of myself."


Andrew blinked.


That was not where he'd expected this conversation to go.


"How so?"


Scott smiled.


Small.


Regretful.


"Have you ever wondered how Jane became my goddaughter?"


Andrew frowned.


"I figured you and Mr. and Mrs. McFly were friends."


Scott laughed.


"Oh, we were."


Another.


"Eventually."


He looked toward the window.


A thousand memories passing through his expression.


"Melissa Park and I grew up together."


Another.


"Same town."


Another.


"Same schools."


Another.


"Same everything."


A smile tugged at the corner of his mouth.


"She was my high school girlfriend."


Andrew immediately straightened.


Interested.


Scott rarely talked about his personal life.


Almost never.


"The summer she turned seventeen, her parents were having problems."


Another.


"Bad problems."


Another.


"Divorce-level problems."


Another.


"So they sent her to stay with family friends for the summer."


Andrew already knew where this story was heading.


"The McFlys."


Scott pointed at him.


"The McFlys."


Another.


"In Hill Valley."


He smiled.


"Melissa met Marty."


Another.


"He already had a girlfriend."


Another.


"They became friends."


Another.


"Really good friends."


Scott laughed quietly.


"I remember driving down to visit."


Another.


"I met Marty."


Another.


"Thought he was a great guy."


Another.


"We all hung out."


He shrugged.


"No jealousy."


Another.


"No drama."


Another.


"Nothing."


Andrew listened carefully.


Already knowing there was a disaster hiding somewhere ahead.


"Melissa came home."


Another.


"We picked up where we left off."


Another.


"Then I got accepted to pre-med."


Another.


"Two hours away."


The smile disappeared.


"And I broke her heart."


Andrew frowned.


"What?"


Scott nodded.


"Thought I was doing the right thing."


Another.


"Thought she deserved better."


Another.


"Thought becoming a doctor was going to be long and difficult."


Another.


"And I didn't want her putting her life on hold for me."


Andrew stared.


Because suddenly this story felt very familiar.


Scott laughed.


"Sounding familiar yet?"


Andrew groaned.


"Oh no."


"Oh yes."


Scott pointed at him.


"Same hero complex."


Another.


"Different decade."


Andrew buried his face in his hands.


Scott continued.


"Melissa and Marty saw each other again in college."


Another.


"They reconnected."


Another.


"The friendship became something more."


Another.


"And by the time I realized I'd made the biggest mistake of my life..."


He shrugged.


"They were married."


Silence settled between them.


Scott smiled sadly.


"I started my residency in Hill Dale."


Another.


"By then they already had the twins."


Another.


"And then Jane."


The smile softened.


"The day Jane was born..."


His voice grew distant.


Warm.


"Melissa almost died."


Another.


"It was touch and go for a while."


Another.


"I was there through all of it."


Andrew listened quietly.


Scott looked down.


"Marty was out of town for work."


Another.


"He didn't make it back in time."


Another.


"And when he finally got there..."


Scott chuckled.


"He thanked me."


Another.


"Then asked me to be Jane's godfather."


Andrew felt his chest tighten.


Because suddenly so many things made sense.


Why Scott loved Jane the way he did.


Why he worried.


Why he hovered.


Why he never stopped showing up.


"The day Jane was born was one of the greatest days of my life."


Scott smiled.


"Probably the only time in my adult life I got to truly be there for Melissa after I broke her heart."


Another.


"I watched Jane grow up."


Another.


"I watched her become this amazing woman."


Another.


"And when she had Lizzy..."


His eyes softened.


"Seeing Melissa hold her granddaughter..."


He shook his head.


"Still gets me."


For a moment neither spoke.


Then Andrew asked quietly:


"Do you still love Mrs. McFly?"


Scott laughed.


Immediately.


"Well."


Another.


"Yeah."


Another.


"I suppose I always will."


His expression stayed peaceful.


Not sad.


Not longing.


Just honest.


"But eventually I learned something."


Another.


"I'd rather be something in her life than nothing at all."


Andrew looked down.


Because that one landed.


Hard.


Scott noticed.


Of course he did.


"And I love Jane."


Another.


"She's the daughter I never had."


Another.


"The daughter I always imagined I'd have if I'd trusted Melissa enough to make her own choices."


He took a sip of coffee.


Then looked directly at Andrew.


"Have you told Jane about Kelly?"


Andrew immediately shook his head.


"No."


Another.


"I can't."


Scott waited.


Andrew sighed.


"Jane's carrying too much already."


Another.


"Kirby's recovery."


Another.


"Lizzy."


Another.


"Adam."


Another.


"Everything."


He looked away.


"I can't be one more person she has to worry about."


Another.


"I won't do that to her."


Scott understood.


The logic made perfect sense.


Which was exactly why it worried him.


Andrew stood.


Moved toward the window.


"I think I'm going to take a few days off."


Another.


"Go see my dad."


Another.


"Clear my head."


His voice grew quieter.


"And I think I need some space from Jane."


Scott's eyebrow rose.


There it was.


The real problem.


"Andrew."


"No."


Another.


"Listen."


Andrew rubbed the back of his neck.


"Kelly leaving..."


Another.


"I thought I was mourning Kelly."


He laughed once.


Painfully.


Turns out..."


His voice cracked.


Just slightly.


"Now that I lost Kelly..."


Another.


"I feel like I'm mourning Jane all over again."


The confession hung between them.


Heavy.


Honest.


Raw.


Scott closed his eyes.


Because twenty-five years ago he'd said almost exactly the same thing about Melissa.


"I can't do that to her."


Andrew continued.


Another.


"I can't make my feelings her problem."


Another.


"Not now."


Another.


"Maybe not ever."


He looked exhausted.


Completely exhausted.


Scott was just opening his mouth to argue—


When he noticed movement through the glass doors.


Someone running.


Fast.


Very fast.


Scott immediately recognized her.


Of course he did.


He'd known her since the day she was born.


"Oh boy."


Andrew frowned.


"What?"


Scott pointed.


"Kid."


Another.


"Brace yourself."


Andrew turned.


And saw Jane sprinting toward the lounge.


Crying.


Terrified.


And moving with purpose.


His stomach immediately dropped.


Because Jane only ran like that when something was very wrong.


Or very right.


And with Jane McFly...


There was rarely an easy way to tell the difference.


Scott stood.


Already smiling despite himself.


"I don't think Jane is going to let you get away with whatever it is you're trying to do to keep her out."


Andrew's eyes widened.


The doors burst open.


And Jane came flying into the room.
