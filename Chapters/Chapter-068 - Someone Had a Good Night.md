# Chapter 68: Someone Had a Good Night


The morning began in the hospital parking garage.


Adam parked the car and immediately turned around.


Lizzy was happily kicking her feet in her car seat.


"Ready for your big day?"


Lizzy responded with enthusiastic nonsense.


Adam nodded seriously.


"Excellent point."


Jane rolled her eyes.


"You two are ridiculous."


Adam grinned.


"We're adorable."


Another.


"There's a difference."


Before Jane could argue, he leaned over and kissed her.


Quick.


Easy.


Comfortable.


The kind of kiss people shared when they knew exactly where they belonged.


"I'll see you later."


Jane smiled.


"Don't let her buy anything expensive."


Adam looked horrified.


"Have you met our daughter?"


That earned a laugh.


Then the three of them headed inside.


Rehab


By the time Jane walked into Kirby's room carrying a fresh cup of coffee...


Andrew was already there.


And immediately started laughing.


Jane stopped.


"Don't."


Andrew laughed harder.


"Oh, someone had a good night."


Jane's eyes widened immediately.


She pointed at him.


Behave.


Andrew looked completely unrepentant.


Kirby glanced between them.


Confused.


"What?"


Andrew shook his head.


"Nothing."


Another.


"Absolutely nothing."


Jane grabbed the nearest object.


Which unfortunately happened to be a tissue box.


Andrew immediately put his hands up.


"Violence."


Another.


"Always violence."


Kirby looked from one to the other.


Then back at Jane.


And smiled.


"Well."


Another.


"You look like a completely different person."


Another.


"I'm glad."


Another.


"This is more like it."


Jane felt herself blush.


Which only made Andrew laugh harder.


"Thanks."


Another.


"It was nice to finally be home."


Another.


"Sleep in my own bed."


Andrew snorted.


"I bet it completely was."


The tissue box flew.


Again.


This time Andrew barely dodged it.


"Ow."


Another.


"Maybe you need to go home and get some more of that miraculous night's sleep."


Jane pointed at him.


"You are incredibly lucky that was the closest thing to me."


Andrew looked delighted.


"I know."


Kirby shook his head.


Hopeless.


Absolutely hopeless.


And despite himself...


He laughed.


Project Manager Jane


Once the chaos settled down, Jane pulled out her tablet.


Instant project-manager mode.


"Okay."


Kirby groaned.


Jane ignored him.


"Today you have physical therapy."


Another.


"Occupational therapy."


Another.


"Your appointment with Dr. Beck."


Another.


"I'm seeing Dr. Ryan."


Another.


"I have to stop by my office."


Another.


"Drop off some paperwork."


Another.


"Check in with Lara."


Another.


"Then I'll come back this afternoon."


Another.


"We can spend the rest of the afternoon together before I head home tonight."


She looked up.


"Okay?"


Kirby smiled.


"Sure, Jane."


Another.


"Very project-manager of you."


Jane rolled her eyes.


"We have to be organized."


Kirby laughed.


Then watched her curiously.


The same curiosity that seemed to be growing every day.


Bright Horizons


Later that morning Jane met Adam outside the Bright Horizons daycare center.


Lizzy occupied one of Adam's arms.


Several informational brochures occupied the other.


The tour went surprisingly well.


The facility was bright.


Safe.


Cheerful.


Full of children.


Teachers.


Tiny tables.


And enough artwork to wallpaper half the hospital.


Lizzy immediately seemed interested.


Which somehow reassured and offended Adam at the same time.


"She didn't even hesitate."


Jane laughed.


By the end of the visit they'd made up their minds.


Adam looked around.


"You know..."


Another.


"This could actually be perfect."


Jane nodded.


"Especially once I start transitioning back to work."


Another.


"My office isn't that far away."


Another.


"Twenty minutes on a bad traffic day."


Adam smiled.


"And she's still close to one of us."


Another.


"If something happens, I'm already here."


Jane nodded.


That was the part she liked most.


Not just the daycare.


The proximity.


The safety.


Knowing Lizzy would be close to Adam while Jane was at the office with Lara and her team.


Adam shifted Lizzy on his hip.


"I can bring her most days."


Another.


"My only concern is overnight shifts."


Jane nodded.


"I'll bring her those days."


Another.


"We'll figure it out."


Together.


Like they always did.


Before leaving, they agreed they would come back in a month and officially enroll Lizzy.


Couples Therapy


Dr. Cohen looked delighted to see them.


Mostly because they both looked rested for once.


"Well."


Another.


"You two look significantly less stressed than last week."


Adam laughed.


Jane immediately hid her face.


"Not touching that."


Dr. Cohen smiled knowingly.


The session focused on practical things.


Jane transitioning back to work.


Childcare.


Lizzy.


Kirby's continued recovery.


Their routines.


The fact that they'd finally spent an uninterrupted night together in over a month.


For the first time in weeks...


The conversation wasn't about surviving.


It was about living.


And that felt like progress.


The Hallway


The session ended.


Adam picked Lizzy up.


Jane gathered her purse.


And together they stepped into the hallway.


Only to nearly run into Kirby.


Literally.


Kirby looked surprised.


Then smiled.


"Hey."


Adam smiled back.


"Hey yourself."


Kirby looked between them.


Then toward Dr. Cohen's office.


"What are you two doing here?"


Jane answered first.


"I'm actually on my way to see Dr. Ryan."


Adam nodded toward Lizzy.


"And we're checking out the daycare."


Kirby frowned slightly.


"The daycare?"


Adam nodded.


"Yeah."


Another.


"Bright Horizons."


Another.


"Thought we'd take a look."


Kirby glanced down at Lizzy.


"Probably a good idea."


Another.


"She's getting big."


Adam groaned dramatically.


"Way too fast."


For a moment the conversation settled.


Then Kirby nodded toward Lizzy.


"So is your wife around?"


Adam adjusted Lizzy on his hip.


"Lizzy's mom and I aren't married."


Another.


"I tried asking her many times."


Another.


"She kept throwing the ring back at me."


Kirby laughed.


"No way."


Adam nodded.


Completely serious.


"Repeatedly."


Another.


"It was very traumatic."


Jane rolled her eyes.


"You are ridiculous."


Adam ignored her.


"We live together."


Another.


"And we're raising Lizzy together."


Kirby nodded.


Then frowned slightly.


Because that seemed odd to him.


"If you live together..."


Another.


"And you're raising your daughter together..."


Another.


"Why doesn't she want to marry you?"


Adam smiled.


The knowing kind.


The one Jane recognized immediately.


"It's complicated."


Kirby laughed.


"That sounds dangerous."


Adam shrugged.


"Probably."


Just then one of the rehab therapists appeared.


"Kirby?"


Another.


"Ready?"


Kirby nodded.


Then looked down at Lizzy.


His entire expression softened.


"Bye sweetheart."


Another.


"You are so beautiful."


Another.


"Your parents are lucky to have you."


Lizzy rewarded him with a huge smile.


Which completely melted him.


Adam laughed.


"We'll see you later."


Then he looked at Jane.


"See you later."


Jane shook her head.


Smiling.


Then stepped forward.


Brushed a curl away from Lizzy's face.


And kissed her forehead.


"Bye sweetheart."


Adam smiled.


Then headed down the hallway with Lizzy.


Leaving Jane standing with Kirby.


The therapist waited patiently nearby.


Kirby watched Adam and Lizzy disappear around the corner.


Then looked back at Jane.


The smile on his face was gentle.


Warm.


Familiar.


Then he said quietly:


"You're a good mom, Jane."


Jane froze.


Completely.


Kirby's expression didn't change.


As if he'd said something perfectly ordinary.


As if the statement made complete sense.


Then he turned.


And followed his therapist toward his appointment.


Leaving Jane standing alone in the hallway.


Her heart pounding.


Her eyes wide.


Watching him disappear around the corner.


"What..."


She whispered to herself.


"...did he just say?"


The hallway suddenly felt very quiet.


And for the first time all day...


Jane wasn't thinking about rehab.


Or daycare.


Or paperwork.


Or therapy.


She was thinking about Kirby.


And how somehow...


Without anyone telling him anything...


He had looked at her and seen a mother.
