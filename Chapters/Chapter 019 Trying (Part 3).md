# Chapter 19 Trying (Part 3)

The movie continued playing.

Neither of them were paying much attention anymore.

Jane was curled against his side, her head resting against his chest, Adam's arm around her shoulders.

The same way they always sat together.

On screen, Clark Gable was probably saying something important.

Neither had any idea what it was.

Eventually Jane looked up.

Adam was already looking down at her.

She smiled.

Then kissed him.

Softly.

Adam kissed her back immediately.

One kiss became another.

And then another.

At some point the movie disappeared completely.

They couldn't have said when.

Only that somewhere between laughter, movie therapy, and sitting together on the couch, they found themselves somewhere neither of them had planned to be.

And that was when Adam felt her tense.

One second she was kissing him back.

The next she was somewhere else entirely.

In her head.

Worried.

Thinking.

Overanalyzing.

Immediately he pulled back just enough to see her face.

"Hey, are you ok?" he said, looking genuinely worried.

Jane hesitated.

"I'm not sure."

"Let's talk about it."

His hand moved gently beneath her chin, raising her eyes to meet his.

The same way he always did when they had to tackle something difficult.

"What's going through your head?" he asked.

"Fear? Worry? I'm not sure..." she said.

"Jane, you know we don't have to do anything you're not ready for. Not the intent of tonight at all. I just want to spend time with you. Sleep next to you, hold you. Anything outside of that doesn't matter to me. You know this, right?"

"I know..."

She looked down.

"I'm just worried that it's not enough. That I'm not enough."

"You know that's not true."

His voice was immediate.

Firm.

"God, you have given me more than I could possibly ask for or imagine, Jane, and I don't ever want you to think that."

He squeezed her hand.

"This, being with you, watching your therapy movies and sometimes adding some of my own, is everything I've wanted."

A smile appeared.

"Just us. Anything that comes with it is just icing on the cake."

"Really?" she asked.

Adam looked almost offended.

"Really."

Silence settled between them.

Then Jane took a breath.

"Adam, I need to tell you something. Please promise you won't laugh."

A grin appeared.

"No promises, but go ahead."

"I'm scared."

The grin disappeared immediately.

Now he knew this wasn't banter.

This was her anxiety.

The same anxiety he'd watched her wrestle with since he'd known her.

"What are you scared of?" he asked carefully.

"Andrew is the only person I've ever..."

She trailed off.

She didn't need to finish.

Adam understood.

For a second he smiled because this wasn't where he'd expected the conversation to go at all.

Then another thought hit him.

A bigger one.

Jane saw it immediately.

"Ask."

Adam rubbed the back of his neck.

"Well, uhm...what about Kirby?"

A pause.

"You guys were together for six years. Granted you were only married for twenty-four hours but you can't tell me that..."

He trailed off.

Understanding he hadn't realized before beginning to settle into place.

Jane looked down.

"I used to get anxious about this. Every single time we tried, this would happen."

Adam's expression shifted.

"And Kirby being Kirby thought that maybe once we got married, things would be different."

"But it wasn't about getting married, was it?" he asked quietly.

Jane met his eyes.

"Uh huh."

Adam nodded slowly.

"Alright. I think I get it."

He squeezed her hand.

"For the record, you know tonight was not about...that, right?"

"I know."

"I wish you would've told me this was something that YOU were thinking about, Jane."

A small smile appeared.

"We talk about everything under the sun. This one's sort of a big one."

"I just assumed that you thought about it too."

That surprised him enough that he laughed.

Then he let out a long breath.

"Honestly?"

"What?"

"It's not something I was actively thinking about."

Jane blinked.

Adam shrugged.

"Between taking in the fact that we're together and that you actually love me, I really just felt lucky that we have this."

His eyes met hers.

"Us."

A small smile appeared.

"But if this is something you want, let's talk about it."

"You are ridiculous."

Adam frowned.

"How so?"

"Do you understand how attractive you really are?"

Adam blinked.

Twice.

Completely caught off guard.

"A girl would have to be blind not to want you or want to think about being with you in that way."

For perhaps the first time all night, Adam actually blushed.

Jane immediately noticed.

"See?"

"Oh my God."

She laughed.

"So the fact that you're even questioning that I would think about this, it's beyond me."

Adam swallowed hard.

Then forced himself back to what mattered.

"So what I'm hearing is that you want me but you're scared because of...Andrew?"

"It's not about Andrew."

Jane shook her head.

"I just never thought I could trust or love anyone enough to want to go there with."

Her voice softened.

"I never thought there would be anyone else that wasn't him."

A pause.

"I thought it would always be him."

Shame immediately followed the admission.

Adam shook his head.

"Jane. You don't have to feel ashamed of that."

A pause.

"Andrew is your first everything."

His voice softened.

"I get it now."

Another pause.

"It's okay if you're not ready and I'm not asking you to be."

"But I am."

That made his eyes widen.

A nervous laugh escaped her.

"Every time we kiss, every time you touch me, I feel it."

A pause.

"I want it."

Another.

"I want you."

Her eyes dropped.

"But something stops me."

She took a shaky breath.

"It wasn't like that with Kirby."

Adam listened quietly.

"Andrew once told me that the problem I had with Kirby ultimately was that I didn't trust him completely."

A pause.

"I could never get over the Kelly of it all."

The old hurt slipped into her voice.

"The breakup."

Another pause.

"The fact that he slept with Kelly in college during our breakup."

She looked down.

"I always thought that was ridiculous."

A small sad smile appeared.

"Maybe it wasn't."

Adam sat quietly for a moment.

Thinking.

Really thinking.

The pieces were starting to fit together.

Kirby.

Andrew.

The stories.

The grief.

The love.

Yesterday's conversation with Andrew.

Everything Jane had told him over the last several months.

And suddenly something clicked.

The issue had always had a face.

A name.

Andrew.

Not because Jane had loved Kirby less.

Adam knew better than that.

The stories she'd told him made that impossible.

Jane had loved Kirby fiercely.

Completely.

But Andrew had always been there too.

Always.

The friend who became her first love.

The person who knew her better than anyone.

The person who somehow occupied a permanent place in her heart whether she wanted him to or not.

Adam remembered Andrew's face the day before.

The sadness.

The affection.

The acceptance.

And suddenly what Andrew had said about Kirby made perfect sense.

Not because Andrew had been jealous.

Because he'd known Jane.

Maybe better than anyone.

"Andrew figured it out."

Jane blinked.

"What?"

"He figured it out before you did."

A pause.

"Didn't he?"

Jane looked away immediately.

And Adam knew he was right.

A silence followed.

Then he smiled softly.

Not judgment.

Not jealousy.

Just understanding.

"That must've been really hard."

Jane laughed weakly.

"You have no idea."

"Oh, I think I might."

Adam squeezed her hand.

"Not because you didn't love Kirby."

The words came quietly.

"Because you did."

A pause.

"But sometimes loving one person doesn't magically erase what you feel for somebody else."

Jane stared at him.

Because somehow he understood.

Without her having to explain.

"Jane."

"Yeah?"

"You loved Kirby."

A pause.

"You loved Andrew."

Another.

"And those things can both be true."

Tears immediately filled her eyes.

"You're not a bad person because love got complicated."

For a moment neither spoke.

Then Adam squeezed her hand.

"So what do we do here, Jane?"

"Well, do you..."

She laughed nervously.

Already feeling exposed.

Adam immediately understood.

A warm laugh escaped him.

"Just because I'm not actively thinking about how to get you into bed doesn't mean I don't want you."

Jane covered her face.

"Oh my God."

"I'm serious."

He laughed.

"Of course I do. Always."

A grin appeared.

"I thought that was implied when I touch you. When I kiss you."

That finally made her laugh.

"Fair."

Then she grew serious again.

"Adam."

"Yeah?"

"I love you completely."

The words landed heavily between them.

"And the reason that it seems like I'm struggling is not because I don't love or trust you."

A pause.

"It's because this is new."

Her voice cracked.

"I feel like this is the first time all over again."

She laughed weakly.

"I know it's ridiculous."

"No."

Adam shook his head.

"It's not."

Again he gently made her look at him.

"Well, it's because it is for us."

Jane stared at him.

"I'm not going to lie."

A small smile appeared.

"I'm scared too."

That surprised her.

"You are?"

"Jane, I'm not exactly an expert at this either."

That earned a laugh.

Adam smiled.

"But when we do this..."

His thumb brushed her cheek.

"I'll be there every step of the way."

A pause.

"I'll support you."

Another.

"I'll make sure you're okay."

His voice softened.

"Because I love you so damn much."

The emotion in his eyes nearly undid her.

"I always will."

And that was it.

The thing that finally quieted the noise in her head.

Not courage.

Not logic.

Love.

Adam.

The man sitting in front of her.

The man who never stopped choosing her.

She looked directly into his eyes.

And this time, there was no fear.

Only love.

"Adam?"

"Yeah?"

"I need you."

For a moment he simply stared at her.

Then he smiled.

The smile that always made her feel safe.

The smile that somehow managed to hold all the love he felt for her.

And before she could second-guess herself, before fear could find its way back into the conversation, Adam leaned forward and kissed her forehead.

"I'm here."

The words settled somewhere deep inside her.

Not because they were a promise.

Because she believed them.

Completely.

He took her hand.

And together they left the couch behind.

The movie continued playing in the background, forgotten by both of them.


Later that night, Jane lay with her head resting against Adam's chest.

The apartment was quiet.

The city lights reflected through the windows overlooking the bay.

For a long time neither of them spoke.

Adam's arm remained wrapped around her.

Holding her the same way he always did.

Like he couldn't quite believe she was there.

Like he never wanted her to doubt that she was loved.

At some point Jane felt tears gathering in her eyes.

She tried to hide them.

She failed.

Naturally, Adam noticed immediately.

"Hey."

His voice was soft.

Concerned.

Jane laughed weakly.

"Sorry."

Adam frowned.

"Don't do that."

The response was immediate.

Automatic.

The same response he always gave whenever she apologized for having feelings.

A smile tugged at her lips.

Adam brushed a hand through her hair.

"What happened?"

Jane stared at the ceiling.

Trying to find words.

Trying to explain something she wasn't entirely sure she understood herself.

"I just..."

Her voice cracked.

"I never thought I'd get here."

Adam stayed quiet.

Listening.

She loved him for that.

For never rushing her.

For never trying to fix things that simply needed to be felt.

"I never thought I would love someone this much again."

The confession sat quietly between them.

Jane swallowed.

For years she had convinced herself that part of her life was over.

That some doors only opened once.

That some loves only happened once.

That her story had already been written.

And yet here she was.

With Adam.

The man who had walked into her life and turned everything upside down.

The man who somehow made her laugh even when she wanted to cry.

The man who sat through therapy assignments and terrible movies and endless conversations.

The man who loved her exactly as she was.

Adam pressed a kiss against the top of her head.

Jane closed her eyes.

"I don't know how long we'll have."

The words escaped before she could stop them.

They both understood why she said it.

Life had taught them that lesson too many times.

Adam's arms tightened around her.

Neither of them pretended otherwise.

But neither of them pulled away from it either.

A quiet understanding settled between them.

They had today.

They had each other.

And that mattered.

Jane buried her face against his chest.

As long as he was here, she decided, she was going to love him fully.

Completely.

Without fear making decisions for her.

Adam looked down at her.

At the woman he loved more than he ever thought possible.

The woman who somehow made him feel whole in ways he was still trying to understand.

He couldn't remember the last time he'd felt this happy.

This certain.

This grateful.

"I love you," he whispered.

Jane smiled against his chest.

"I love you too."

And for the first time in a very long time, tomorrow felt less frightening than it did hopeful.
