# Chapter 28: Baby Reveal

The prenatal appointment itself had gone smoothly.


The baby looked healthy.


Everything measured exactly where it should.


The sonogram technician had patiently answered all of Adam's questions.


Which had been approximately one thousand questions.


"What about that?"


"Normal."


"And that?"


"Also normal."


"Are you sure?"


The technician laughed.


"Very sure."


Jane had spent most of the appointment trying not to laugh at him.


Because somehow Adam had become both the calmest and most anxious father-to-be on earth.


When the appointment was over, they went home happy.


The doctor would call with the results in a few days.


So they waited.


Or at least Jane waited.


Adam asked for updates roughly every four hours.


"Any call?"


"No."


"Now?"


"No."


"How about now?"


"Adam."


A few days later, Jane was at work when her phone rang.


A nurse from the doctor's office.


Jane smiled as she listened.


Asked a few questions.


Thanked her.


Then hung up.


And immediately sat there grinning like an idiot.


The rest of the day felt endless.


Because she wanted to tell Adam in person.


That night they were lying in bed together at his apartment.


Jane was curled against his side.


Adam was halfway through a completely unnecessary discussion about nursery colors.


"I still think yellow is underrated."


"You know absolutely nothing about nursery design."


"I know colors."


"That isn't the same thing."


Adam looked offended.


Then noticed the smile she was trying to hide.


Immediately his eyes narrowed.


"No."


"What?"


"That's a look."


Jane laughed.


"I don't know what you're talking about."


"Jane."


"What?"


"You're doing the thing."


"What thing?"


"The smiling thing."


Jane immediately started laughing harder.


Adam sat up.


"Oh my God."


"What?"


"We got the results."


Jane couldn't hold it in anymore.


"We got the results."


For a moment Adam just stared at her.


Then sat completely upright.


"The baby?"


Jane nodded.


"The baby."


Adam grabbed both of her hands.


"Jane."


She laughed.


"Adam."


"Tell me."


"No."


"Jane."


"Adam."


"I am serious."


"So am I."


"Jane."


She let him suffer for another three seconds.


Then finally smiled.


"We're having a girl."


Silence.


Complete silence.


Adam blinked once.


Then twice.


Then his entire face lit up.


"A girl?"


Jane nodded.


"A girl."


Adam laughed.


Actually laughed.


Then immediately pulled her into his arms.


Holding her so tightly she squeaked.


"Adam!"


"A girl."


"I know."


"A little girl."


"I know."


His smile somehow kept growing.


"We're having a daughter."


The emotion in his voice nearly broke her.


Then suddenly:


"I've got sunshine..."


Jane immediately started laughing.


"Oh my God."


"On a cloudy day..."


"Adam."


"When it's cold outside..."


"Adam."


"I've got the month of May..."


Jane was laughing so hard she could barely breathe.


"You are ridiculous."


"What can make me feel this way?"


Adam looked down at her.


Then kissed her.


"My girl..."


The kiss stole the last words away.


When they finally pulled apart, Adam rested his forehead against hers.


"I'm going to be a girl dad."


The wonder in his voice melted her.


"Yeah."


Adam laughed softly.


"I'm going to spoil her."


"Adam."


"I already know."


"You absolutely are."


"I have no self-control."


"That's true."


Adam smiled.


Then grew thoughtful.


"You know what I hope?"


Jane looked up.


"What?"


His hand settled gently against her stomach.


"I hope she's brave."


A pause.


"I hope she's stubborn."


Jane laughed.


"Oh God."


"What?"


"Of all the things."


"It is an important quality."


"It really isn't."


"It is if she's anything like her mother."


Jane rolled her eyes.


Adam continued.


"I hope she's kind."


Another.


"I hope she laughs a lot."


Another.


"I hope she knows how loved she is."


His voice softened.


"And when things get hard..."


A pause.


"I hope she remembers that she comes from incredibly strong women."


Jane felt her eyes sting.


"Adam."


"What?"


"You're making me cry."


A grin appeared.


"I thought that was my full-time job."


That earned a laugh.


Then Jane shifted slightly against him.


"I've been thinking about something."


Adam immediately looked at her.


"What?"


"The baby's name."


That got his full attention.


"Oh?"


Jane nodded.


"Now that we know she's a girl."


Adam smiled.


"Okay."


A pause.


"What are we thinking?"


Jane hesitated.


Then smiled softly.


"I've always loved your mom's name."


The smile immediately disappeared from Adam's face.

Replaced by something softer.


"Elizabeth."


Jane nodded.


"Elizabeth."


The room fell quiet.


Adam looked down for a moment.


Then smiled.


"My dad used to call her Lizzy."


Jane's face immediately lit up.


"Lizzy?"


Adam nodded.


"All the time."


A small laugh escaped him.


"I don't think he called her Elizabeth once in my entire childhood."


Jane smiled.


"I love that."


A pause.


"I really love that."


Then she leaned her head against his shoulder.


"I was thinking..."


Another pause.


"I'd love to name her Elizabeth."


Adam looked at her.


Jane smiled.


"And call her Lizzy."


For a moment Adam just stared.


Like he couldn't quite believe what she'd said.


"Jane..."


His eyes immediately filled.


"What?"


"You want to name our daughter after my mom?"


Jane nodded.


"If that's okay with you."


Adam laughed softly.


Shook his head.


Then looked at her like she was the most incredible person he'd ever met.


"You are unbelievable."


Jane smiled.


"Good unbelievable or bad unbelievable?"


"Very good unbelievable."


A pause.


"The best kind."


His voice softened.


"I'm so grateful."


Another.


"That you would even think of that."


Another.


"That you'd want that for her."


Jane reached for his hand.


"I know how much she meant to you."


Adam nodded.

Unable to speak for a moment.


Then he leaned over and kissed her hair.


Long and slow.


Taking in the moment.


Taking in her.


Taking in the knowledge that somehow this extraordinary woman wanted to name their daughter after his mother.


Eventually he looked down at Jane's stomach and smiled.


"Elizabeth Carter."


He tested the name.


Then smiled even bigger.


"Lizzy."


Jane laughed.


"You like it?"


Adam looked at her like she'd asked the dumbest question on the planet.


"Jane."


A pause.


"I love it."


Then he rested his hand gently against her stomach.


And for a while neither of them spoke.


Just sat together imagining her.


A little girl.


Their little girl.


Elizabeth.


Lizzy.


And for the first time since they'd learned she existed, she felt just a little bit more real.
