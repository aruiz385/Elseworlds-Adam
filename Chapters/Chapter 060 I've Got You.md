# Chapter 60: I've Got You


The doctors' lounge was quiet.


Which was unusual.


Hospitals were rarely quiet.


But somehow this room always felt separate from the rest of the building.


Like a small pocket of calm carved out of the chaos.


Jane sat on the couch.


Freshly showered.


Clean clothes.


A cup of coffee in her hands.


And for the first time in almost twenty-four hours she felt human again.


Adam sat beside her.


One arm stretched across the back of the couch.


Close enough that she could lean into him.


Which she immediately did.


Resting her head against his shoulder.


Without asking.


Without thinking.


Adam smiled.


"There she is."


Jane groaned.


"Don't."


Another.


"I've had a very emotional twenty-four hours."


Adam nodded seriously.


"I can tell."


Another.


"You've hugged me three times."


Jane looked at him.


"Only three?"


That earned a laugh.


A real one.


The kind she hadn't heard from him since yesterday.


For a while neither spoke.


Just enjoying the quiet.


Eventually Jane sighed.


"I don't know how to do this."


Adam didn't ask what she meant.


He knew.


Kirby.


The missing years.


The truth.


All of it.


Jane stared into her coffee.


"He keeps looking at me."


Another.


"As if everything is okay."


Another.


"As if nothing happened."


Her voice cracked.


"Adam..."


Another.


"He trusts me."


Adam squeezed her shoulder.


"I know."


Jane shook her head.


"No."


Another.


"I don't think you understand."


Another.


"He trusts me completely."


Her eyes filled.


"He woke up and the first thing he saw was me."


Another.


"And every time he gets confused, he looks for me."


Adam listened.


Quietly.


Because this wasn't something that needed fixing.


It needed hearing.


Before he could answer, the lounge door opened.


Scott walked in.


Coffee in hand.


Looking tired.


But relieved.


"Well."


Another.


"This looks significantly better."


Jane looked up.


Scott pointed at her.


"Shower."


Another.


"Clean clothes."


Another.


"Coffee."


Another.


"Good work, Adam."


Adam nodded solemnly.


"I am a highly skilled physician."


Scott snorted.


"Sure."


Another.


"Let's go with that."


Even Jane laughed.


The sound seemed to relax Scott.


Just a little.


He sat down across from them.


Immediately becoming serious.


"Kirby's doing well."


The room stilled.


Jane sat forward.


"How well?"


Scott thought for a moment.


"Better than I expected."


Another.


"He's responding appropriately."


Another.


"His memory appears intact."


Another.


"Neurologically, I'm encouraged."


Jane exhaled.


She hadn't realized she'd been holding her breath.


Scott nodded.


"We're still bringing in physical therapy."


Another.


"Cardiology is going to evaluate him."


Another.


"Neurology will continue following him."


Another.


"I want every specialty touching this case."


Adam nodded.


"Good."


Scott leaned back.


"But that's actually not what I'm here to discuss."


That got their attention.


Scott looked directly at Jane.


"We need a plan."


Jane already knew where this was going.


The truth.


The missing years.


Everything.


Scott folded his arms.


"I'm not dropping seven years on him all at once."


Another.


"That would be cruel."


Another.


"Right now he knows he's in the ICU."


Another.


"He knows he's recovering."


Another.


"He knows the people he loves are here."


Another.


"And for the moment, that's enough."


Jane nodded.


Agreed.


Scott continued.


"We'll start slowly."


Another.


"We orient him."


Another.


"We tell him where he is."


Another.


"We tell him why he's here."


Another.


"We begin filling in the missing years."


His eyes moved to Jane.


"There are going to be some very difficult conversations."


Jane looked down.


Because she knew exactly which conversations he meant.


Scott sighed.


"Eventually we tell him the date."


Another.


"We tell him how much time has passed."


Another.


"And we start helping him understand where everyone is now."


His gaze lingered on Jane for a second.


Long enough that she understood.


Long enough that she looked away.


Scott finally turned toward Adam.


"Which brings me to you."


Adam raised an eyebrow.


"That never ends well."


Scott ignored him.


Professionally.


Mostly.


"I want you to talk to him."


Jane immediately looked up.


"What?"


Scott nodded.


"I think you're uniquely qualified."


Adam's expression became thoughtful.


Scott continued.


"Kirby lost seven years."


Another.


"Not the same way you did."


Another.


"But the experience of waking up and discovering a piece of your life is missing..."


Another.


"You understand that."


The room went quiet.


Jane stared at Scott.


Then at Adam.


"No."


Both men looked at her.


"No?"


Scott asked.


Jane shook her head.


"Scott."


Another.


"That's asking a lot."


Adam reached for her hand immediately.


"It's okay."


She looked at him.


"Adam."


Another.


"You don't have to do that."


His expression softened immediately.


Because he knew exactly what she meant.


Exactly what she was worried about.


Adam squeezed her hand gently.


"I know."


Another.


"But if it helps him..."


Another.


"If it helps him understand what's happening to him..."


Another.


"Then I want to."


Jane looked unconvinced.


Adam smiled softly.


"Jane."


Another.


"I'm not pretending to understand what he's going through."


Another.


"Nobody understands exactly what he's going through."


Another.


"But I do understand waking up and realizing that life has moved on without you."


Another.


"That you lost time."


The room became very still.


Adam's voice remained calm.


Steady.


"The details are different."


Another.


"But the fear?"


Another.


"The confusion?"


Another.


"The feeling that the world kept moving without you?"


Another.


"Yeah."


Another.


"I know that part."


Jane's eyes immediately filled.


Because that was what she was afraid of.


Adam squeezed her hand again.


"I'm okay."


Another.


"Really."


Another.


"And if I can make this even a little easier for him..."


Another.


"Then I want to try."


Scott nodded.


"That's exactly why I asked."


After a moment, Adam nodded too.


"Okay."


Another.


"I'll talk to him."


Jane still looked unhappy.


Very unhappy.


Adam noticed immediately.


Which unfortunately gave him an idea.


A terrible idea.


The kind of idea that occurred to Adam far too often.


"You know."


He looked at Jane.


"I'm sure we can manage to keep our hands off each other for fifteen minutes while I talk to your husband."


Jane blinked.


Then immediately grabbed a throw pillow and launched it at him.


Directly at his face.


Adam caught it.


Barely.


Scott laughed.


Actually laughed.


The first genuine laugh Jane had heard from him in days.


Adam looked entirely too pleased with himself.


Which made it worse.


"You are impossible."


Jane informed him.


"Yet somehow you keep me around."


Adam replied.


Scott shook his head.


Still smiling.


Because in the middle of all this chaos...


In the middle of comas.


And grief.


And impossible conversations.


Somehow these two still found a way to make each other laugh.


And honestly?


It was a relief.


Scott stood.


Finishing his coffee.


"We'll give him a couple more days."


Another.


"Physical therapy first."


Another.


"Get him stronger."


Another.


"Then we start dealing with reality."


The room fell quiet again.


Because everyone knew what that meant.


But as Scott walked out of the lounge, he felt more hopeful than he had in a week.


Because Kirby wasn't doing this alone.


None of them were.


And that mattered.
