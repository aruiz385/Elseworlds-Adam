# Chapter 64: The Truth Between the Lines


Jane stared at him.


Completely frozen.


Because she wasn't sure she wanted to hear what was going through his head.


Kirby watched her carefully.


Waiting.


For the first time since he'd woken up, he wasn't looking confused.


He looked certain.


And somehow that scared her more.


"Kirby..."


Her voice came out softer than she intended.


He shook his head.


"No."


Another.


"Please."


Another.


"Just tell me if I'm wrong."


Jane's heart hammered.


Because she already knew where this was going.


Or at least she thought she did.


Kirby looked down at his hands.


Thinking.


Trying to organize thoughts that felt increasingly impossible.


Then finally:


"When I woke up..."


Another.


"You were there."


Another.


"You've been here every day."


Another.


"Every time."


Jane didn't speak.


Didn't move.


Kirby continued.


"When I'm scared, you know."


Another.


"When I'm overwhelmed, you know."


Another.


"When I don't understand something..."


Another.


"You always know."


His eyes lifted to hers.


"And everybody keeps looking at you."


Jane's stomach dropped.


Kirby wasn't wrong.


Scott.


Andrew.


Jonathan.


Everyone did.


Because everyone knew.


And everyone was waiting.


Waiting for the inevitable collision between the past and the present.


"You keep telling me about everyone else."


Another.


"But nobody tells me about you."


Another.


"You tell me what happened to Karen."


Another.


"To James."


Another.


"To Andrew."


Another.


"But not you."


Jane looked away.


Unable to meet his eyes.


Kirby's voice softened.


"Why?"


The question hurt.


Because she didn't have an answer she could give him.


Not yet.


Not one that wouldn't destroy everything Scott had spent the last week carefully building.


Kirby let out a slow breath.


Then said the thing she'd been dreading.


"I think you're protecting me."


Jane closed her eyes.


Briefly.


Because he was.


Because everyone was.


And because Kirby was beginning to notice.


When she opened her eyes again, he was still watching her.


Still waiting.


Still hoping she'd trust him enough to tell him.


"I am."


The admission slipped out before she could stop it.


Kirby's expression changed.


Not angry.


Not hurt.


Just sad.


"Why?"


Jane swallowed hard.


Because the truth was simple.


Because she loved him.


Because she cared about him.


Because she didn't want to watch him break.


But none of those were answers she could give right now.


So instead she reached for his hand.


The same way she had every day since he woke up.


The same way she always had.


"Because you've been through enough."


Kirby stared at her.


Then looked down at their hands.


At the way she held his.


At the familiarity.


The instinct.


The history.


And suddenly Jane saw another question forming.


A worse one.


One she wasn't prepared for.


Fortunately a knock sounded at the door.


Both of them looked up.


Scott.


Standing in the doorway.


Jane had never been happier to see another human being in her entire life.


Scott took one look at her face.


Then at Kirby's.


And immediately knew he'd interrupted something important.


"Bad timing?"


Kirby actually smiled.


A little.


"Depends."


Scott laughed softly.


Good.


Talking.


Humor.


Progress.


He'd take it.


"I've got physical therapy waiting downstairs."


Another.


"And apparently they've decided you need to torture yourself walking today."


Kirby rolled his eyes.


Which immediately reassured Scott.


Because that felt normal.


Healthy.


Familiar.


"Wonderful."


Kirby muttered.


Scott looked pleased.


"See?"


Another.


"Already sarcastic."


Another.


"That's a good sign."


Jane laughed.


And for a moment the tension broke.


Just enough.


Scott looked toward her.


Noticed the anxiety she was still carrying.


The fear.


The exhaustion.


And for the first time all day, Jane saw something reassuring in his expression.


Not today.


The unspoken message was clear.


They hadn't run out of time.


Not yet.


Scott turned back toward Kirby.


"Come on."


Another.


"Let's get you back on your feet."


Kirby nodded.


Then looked at Jane.


Again.


And the look lingered a second longer than it should have.


Long enough that she felt her chest tighten.


Long enough that she wondered if he already knew more than anyone realized.


Then he smiled.


Small.


Tired.


But genuine.


And followed Scott out of the room.


Leaving Jane alone.


With the question he hadn't finished asking.


And a growing fear that sooner rather than later...


They were all going to run out of things to hide behind.
