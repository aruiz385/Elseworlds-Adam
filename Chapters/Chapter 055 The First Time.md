# Chapter 55: The First Time


The hospital felt quieter than it had the day before.


Not calmer.


Just quieter.


The kind of quiet that came before something important.


Or something terrible.


Nobody seemed willing to guess which.


Jane sat beside Kirby's bed.


Andrew stood near the window.


Jonathan remained in a chair beside the wall.


Kelly sat beside him.


Adam stood near the door.


Scott reviewed the chart one final time.


Everybody was waiting.


Nobody was ready.


Scott finally looked up.


"Okay."


Another.


"We're going to reduce the sedation."


The room immediately became still.


Jane reached for Kirby's hand.


Almost instinctively.


Her heart pounding.


Scott stepped forward.


Explaining the process even though everyone already knew it.


Mostly because talking was easier than waiting.


"It may take time."


Another.


"He may wake up gradually."


Another.


"He may be confused."


Another.


"He may not remember everything."


Another.


"Don't overwhelm him."


Nobody promised anything.


Because nobody knew if they were capable of that.


The medications began to decrease.


Then they waited.


Minutes passed.


Long minutes.


Kirby didn't move.


Then—


A twitch.


Barely visible.


Jane immediately sat forward.


Andrew stopped breathing.


Jonathan stood.


Scott lifted a hand.


"Easy."


Nobody listened.


Another twitch.


Then movement.


Small.


Weak.


But undeniably there.


Kirby's eyes fluttered.


Opened.


Closed again.


Opened a second time.


The room disappeared.


For everyone.


Every person there focused entirely on him.


Alive.


Actually alive.


His eyes moved slowly around the room.


Disoriented.


Confused.


Trying to understand.


Andrew.


Jonathan.


Kelly.


Jane.


Scott.


Faces.


Familiar faces.


But wrong.


Something was wrong.


His heart rate immediately increased.


Scott noticed first.


"Kirby."


Another.


"Hey."


Another.


"Look at me."


Kirby didn't.


He kept looking around.


His eyes moving rapidly now.


Fear growing.


Because nothing made sense.


People looked older.


Tired.


Different.


The room looked wrong.


Everything looked wrong.


His breathing became uneven.


The monitor immediately reacted.


Scott stepped closer.


"Kirby."


Another.


"Slow breaths."


Andrew moved toward the bed.


"It's okay."


Another.


"It's okay, buddy."


That made it worse.


Because Andrew looked older.


Years older.


Kirby stared.


Confusion becoming panic.


His chest rose rapidly.


The monitor alarmed.


Scott swore quietly.


Then turned toward the nurse.


"We're sedating."


Jane froze.


"Scott—"


"We're not doing this."


His voice was gentle but firm.


"Not like this."


Another.


"He's overstimulated."


Another.


"We'll try again later."


The medication flowed into the IV.


Slowly.


Carefully.


Kirby's breathing began to settle.


The panic faded.


His eyes drooped.


Still confused.


Still frightened.


Then closed.


The room became silent.


Nobody moved.


Nobody knew what to say.


Finally Jonathan sat down.


Covering his face.


The first sob escaped before he could stop it.


Kelly immediately moved beside him.


Andrew looked like somebody had hollowed him out.


Then suddenly he turned and left.


Jane watched him go.


For a moment she stayed where she was.


Then she followed.


The ICU doors closed behind her.


She found him halfway down the hallway.


His hands braced against the wall.


Head lowered.


Trying to breathe.


Trying to stay upright.


Trying desperately not to fall apart.


"Andrew."


He didn't answer.


"Andrew."


This time she was already beside him.


The second he looked at her she knew.


He was drowning.


Not because of what happened five minutes ago.


Because of everything.


The last week.


The last seven years.


The guilt.


The fear.


The hope.


All of it.


Jane didn't say anything.


She simply stepped forward and wrapped her arms around him.


Immediately.


Andrew held onto her.


Hard.


Like a drowning man grabbing a life preserver.


Like if he let go he might come apart completely.


Jane tightened her arms around him.


"Andrew."


His breathing was uneven.


Shaky.


"Andrew, it's okay."


Another.


"Kirby's going to be okay."


The words felt inadequate.


She knew they did.


Nothing she said was going to fix this.


Nothing she said was going to make the fear disappear.


So she stayed.


And she held him.


Eventually Andrew broke.


"Jane."


His voice cracked.


"I feel useless."


The admission tore right through her.


"I couldn't save him then as a cop."


Another.


"And I can't save him now as a doctor."


Jane immediately pulled back just enough to look at him.


"Andrew."


Another.


"Stop."


His eyes closed.


Because he knew exactly what she meant.


The shooting.


The day everything changed.


The day Kirby took a bullet meant for him.


The day Andrew's life split into before and after.


"You need to stop blaming yourself for that."


Another.


"It wasn't your fault."


Another.


"You need to understand that."


Another.


"I thought you'd dealt with it."


Andrew laughed bitterly.


"I thought I did too."


Another.


"I really thought I did."


His eyes filled.


"And then this happened."


Another.


"And it all came back."


Another.


"All of it."


Jane stayed quiet.


Letting him talk.


"Walking to the car."


Another.


"Hearing the gunshot."


Another.


"Thinking that was it."


Another.


"Thinking he was dead."


His voice broke.


"And now he's here."


Another.


"Jane, he's here."


Another.


"And I have no idea how that's even possible."


He laughed again.


A broken sound.


"I asked Chris to help me figure it out."


Another.


"Chris is driving himself crazy trying to investigate how this could have happened."


Another.


"But I should've known."


Another.


"I should've figured it out."


Jane immediately shook her head.


"No."


Another.


"There is no way you could have known."


Another.


"None."


Her voice was firm now.


The same voice he'd used on her countless times before.


"You used to tell me that."


Andrew looked up.


"When I blamed myself."


Another.


"When I couldn't forgive myself."


Another.


"When I thought everything was my fault."


Jane held his gaze.


"You kept telling me I wasn't responsible."


Another.


"And I'm telling you the exact same thing."


Another.


"You aren't responsible either."


Silence.


"He is alive."


Another.


"Scott is going to make sure he's okay."


Another.


"We're all going to make sure he's okay."


Another.


"But you can't save everyone, Andrew."


Another.


"You're human."


Andrew looked away.


Jane smiled softly.


"You know Adam used to call you Super Cop turned Med Student when he first met you?"


Andrew blinked.


"What?"


A small laugh escaped her.


"When he first met you."


Another.


"He thought it was completely ridiculous that one person had that many accomplishments."


The corner of Andrew's mouth twitched.


Just slightly.


Jane saw it.


Good.


"You are one of the best cops I know."


Another.


"One of the best doctors I know."


Another.


"The best brother Kirby could ask for."


Her voice softened.


"And the best friend I've ever had."


Andrew's eyes immediately filled.


Because only Jane knew exactly what to say.


She always had.


Since they were kids.


Through every version of themselves.


Every mistake.


Every heartbreak.


Every impossible thing.


She somehow always knew.


Andrew looked at her for a long moment.


Then leaned forward and kissed the top of her head.


The way he had done a thousand times throughout their lives.


Then pulled her into another hug.


"Thanks."


His voice was barely above a whisper.


Another.


"I miss you, Jane."


The words hung between them.


Neither pretending not to understand.


Not the friendship.


Not entirely.


The life.


The future they once thought they'd have.


The version of themselves that existed before grief and loss changed everything.


Jane closed her eyes.


For a moment neither of them moved.


"I miss you too, Andrew."


And she did.


Not because she regretted Adam.


Not because she wished her life were different.


But because there would always be a part of her that remembered the future they once imagined together.


A future that belonged to another version of their lives.


Eventually Andrew took a deep breath.


Then another.


The weight still there.


But lighter now.


Manageable.


He nodded once.


Straightened his shoulders.


And together they walked back toward the ICU.


Toward Kirby.


Toward whatever came next.
