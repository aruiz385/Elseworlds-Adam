# Chapter 71: Boundaries


Jane burst into the doctors' lounge so fast that both Scott and Andrew were on their feet before the doors had finished swinging closed behind her.


Andrew took one look at her face and immediately knew something was wrong.


"Jane?"


She crossed the room and threw her arms around him.


Andrew caught her automatically.


Holding her tightly.


"Hey, Jane, what happened?" he asked, his voice thick with concern.


Jane pulled back just enough to look at him.


"First, why didn't you tell me that Kelly left you?"


Andrew blinked.


Of all the things he expected her to say, that wasn't one of them.


"Jane, I didn't want to add another weight to your load. You finally got to go and spend a night at home with Lizzy and Adam. You were happy this morning and I wasn't going to take that away from you by giving you something else to worry about. Something else you felt you had to fix."


Jane stared at him.


Disbelief.


Hurt.


Frustration.


All of it at once.


"Andrew, we promised that we would always be honest. It's been a week. Were you ever going to tell me?"


Andrew sighed.


"Jane, how did you even find out?"


The tears returned instantly.


"Kirby."


Andrew straightened.


"What?"


"Kirby told me right after he told me that he knew I was Lizzy's mom and handed me this."


She shoved the folder into his hands.


Andrew opened it.


The second he saw the signed paperwork his eyes widened.


Divorce papers.


Already signed.


Already finished.


Andrew looked up at her and immediately wrapped his arms around her again.


"I'm sorry."


He looked down at the papers.


At Kirby's signature.


At the date.


Trying to understand how his brother had gotten there.


Trying to understand how Jane had gotten there.


"I'm sorry...I'm not sure what he was thinking."


The truth was he wasn't sure what to feel.


Part of him was angry that Jane was standing there crying.


Part of him understood exactly why Kirby had done it.


And by the look of the paperwork...


Kelly had probably helped.


Jane wiped at her eyes.


"He first thanked me for taking care of him since he woke up and told me that I lived up to the title of wife."


Andrew closed his eyes.


That sounded exactly like Kirby.


"He told me that he figured out Lizzy was my daughter weeks ago. He said that at first he thought that Lizzy was yours but after talking with Adam increasingly he realized that Adam was really Lizzy's dad."


A tiny smile appeared on Andrew's face despite everything.


"Yeah."


That sounded exactly like Adam.


Jane continued.


"He said he really listened to Adam when Adam talked about Lizzy's mom and he knew right away that Adam was talking about me."


Andrew nodded.


Of course he did.


Adam had never been subtle where Jane was concerned.


Not once.


"Then he said that he wished he would have let you come after me that day in the courtyard at UCLA fourteen years ago."


Andrew's smile vanished.


Jane looked away.


"He wished he would have stayed with Kelly instead."


Silence filled the room.


Heavy.


Complicated.


Painful.


Then Jane took another shaky breath.


"And then he told me that Kelly left you."


Andrew looked down.


"And he told me to please not leave you alone."


For a moment nobody spoke.


Not even Scott.


Finally Andrew looked back at her.


"Jane, I am glad that it's finally out in the open so you don't have to continue to have to hide your daughter from him."


Jane blinked.


Andrew continued.


"You shouldn't have ever been placed in that position."


His voice softened.


"And honestly, anyone who sees you with Lizzy can tell that you're her mom. It's hard to miss."


A faint smile tugged at the corner of Jane's mouth despite herself.


"And Adam..."


Andrew shook his head.


A small laugh escaping.


"Well, he never lies, so if being honest about the woman he loves is what made Kirby realize the truth, well then that's a good thing."


Jane swallowed.


Trying to process all of it.


Trying and failing.


Andrew looked down at the folder.


Then back at her.


"Now you can go back to your life."


The words immediately felt wrong to Jane.


Andrew didn't notice.


Or maybe he did.


"As for me and Kelly breaking up..."


He exhaled.


"You don't have to worry about me. I'm actually taking this better than I thought I would."


Jane narrowed her eyes.


A warning sign.


Andrew kept going.


"I knew as soon as my brother came back to life that Kelly was not okay."


Another breath.


"Maybe I should have made her talk about it a month ago."


His gaze dropped to the floor.


"I suppose I always knew she was in love with Kirby."


He shrugged.


"I don't know."


Then he looked directly at Jane.


"My point is that you don't need to carry this, Jane."


And there it was.


The thing she immediately hated.


"I'm going to go home to Hill Dale to be with my dad for a bit."


Jane's stomach dropped.


"I need to deal with this alone."


Immediately—


"Andrew, I'm here for you. I won't leave you alone. We're—"


"No."


The word stopped her.


Andrew never interrupted her.


Almost never.


But this time he did.


Firmly.


"Jane, we're not doing this."


She stared at him.


"What?"


"I need to handle this alone."


His voice remained calm.


Steady.


Resolved.


"Not with you constantly knocking on my door to make sure I'm okay, or you checking in on me twenty-four seven."


Jane felt her chest tighten.


Andrew kept going.


Because he'd clearly rehearsed this.


"I am setting some boundaries for us."


Scott immediately looked away.


He had a very bad feeling about this.


"You are going to go home to your family and you are going to focus on what the next steps for you and Adam will be."


Jane stared at him.


Utterly stunned.


"You and I? We are going to put some space in between us so you can do this."


Every word made it worse.


Andrew didn't even notice.


"I refuse to let you blow up your world to try to help me carry something that has nothing to do with you."


Another pause.


"Something that maybe I should have dealt with a long time ago before Kelly and I decided to even attempt something."


His voice softened.


"When I deal with it, I will try to come back around."


Silence.


Complete silence.


Scott suddenly feared for Andrew's life.


Because he knew that look.


He'd seen that look before.


Unfortunately for Andrew...


So had Jane.


Jane stood.


Slowly.


Dangerously.


And fixed Andrew with a look filled with absolute disdain.


"Okay."


Her voice was calm.


Too calm.


"If that's what you want, sure."


Andrew immediately regretted everything.


Jane folded her arms.


"Oh, just so you know, Adam, Lizzy and I owe my parents a visit to Hill Dale, so we will see you there."


Andrew closed his eyes.


Here we go.


"Furthermore, you can try to ignore my phone calls when I check in on you, because I will."


Scott immediately looked down to hide his smile.


"But I also have the spare key to your apartment so I will not be knocking to check in on you."


Jane pointed at him.


"I will simply let myself in."


Andrew groaned.


Jane wasn't done.


"And you can take your boundaries and..."


She stopped herself.


Barely.


"...well, you know the rest."


Scott laughed.


Actually laughed.


Jane ignored him.


"So know that I'm not going anywhere."


Her voice cracked.


Not with anger.


With hurt.


Real hurt.


"If you need to work through whatever this is, you will have to do it with me in your face twenty-four seven."


Andrew opened his mouth.


Jane cut him off immediately.


"I will not spend another year of us ignoring each other."


The room went very still.


"It will not happen again, Andrew."


There it was.


The real wound.


The old wound.


The one neither of them talked about.


The one both of them remembered.


"So whatever this is, figure it out."


Jane shook her head.


Tears building again.


"I already have enough with your brother telling me that he wishes our time together didn't happen."


Andrew frowned immediately.


"Jane, that's not—"


"It's probably not what he meant."


She waved him off.


"But it's what he said."


Another tear escaped.


"He said he wished he could go back in time and pick Kelly."


Her voice broke.


"Successfully erasing the six years we spent together."


Andrew looked heartbroken.


Not for himself.


For her.


"Wonderful."


Jane laughed bitterly.


"Now I have you, who is supposed to be my best friend, my daughter's godfather, telling me that he wants me out of his life too?"


Andrew immediately shook his head.


"No, Jane—"


"You know, you'd think that after all these years things would be different."


The exhaustion finally showed.


The hurt.


The frustration.


The sheer unfairness of the day.


"But all I get is both of you wanting me out of your lives."


Silence.


Heavy.


Painful.


Final.


Jane wiped her eyes.


Straightened her shoulders.


And pointed at him.


"I may let him get away with it."


Another beat.


"But not you."


Andrew froze.


Because that was the truth.


She could walk away from the divorce papers.


She could eventually make peace with Kirby's decision.


But Andrew?


No.


Not him.


"You and I have too much history for me to let you do what you always do."


Her voice softened.


Just slightly.


"Push me away."


Andrew had no response.


None.


Jane nodded once.


Satisfied.


"Now."


She pointed toward the door.


"I am going to go tell Adam that he can rest easy because he is no longer engaged to a married woman."


Then she turned.


Made it halfway to the door.


Stopped.


Turned around.


Walked over to Scott.


And kissed him on the cheek.


"Love you, Uncle Scott."


Scott smiled immediately.


"Love you too, kid."


Jane pointed at Andrew.


Without even looking at him.


"Get your mentee in line please before I break something."


Then she stormed out.


The doors slammed shut behind her.


Silence.


Long silence.


Scott looked at Andrew.


Andrew stared at the closed doors.


Still processing what had just happened.


Then Scott started laughing.


Slowly at first.


Then harder.


"And to think..."


He shook his head.


"...you thought you were going to get away with that."


Andrew groaned.


Scott stood.


Still laughing.


"Thirty years, Kid."


Another shake of his head.


"You've known her for thirty years and you thought that speech was going to do what exactly?"


Andrew stared at the door Jane had disappeared through.


Then slowly shook his head.


Completely defeated.


Completely unsurprised.


And somehow...


A little in awe.


Because after thirty years...


Jane McFly was still the only person in the world who could tell him exactly where to go.


And make him feel better afterward.
