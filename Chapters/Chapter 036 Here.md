# Chapter 36: Here

The drive to the hospital was a blur.


Adam drove with one hand.


Jane's hand was wrapped tightly around the other.


Every few minutes another contraction hit.


Every few minutes she squeezed harder.


Not once did he complain.


Not once did he let go.


The instant they got into the car, Adam called Scott.


"She's in labor."


A pause.


"Water broke."


Another.


"Contractions are close together."


Scott was already moving.


"I'm heading in."


Then Adam called Andrew.


The phone rang twice.


"Adam?"


"She's in labor."


Silence.


Then:


"I'm on my way."


As he drove, another memory surfaced.


A conversation from two weeks earlier.


Andrew had found him in the physician lounge.


"The plan was always that I would be there."


Adam frowned.


"There for what?"


"Lizzy's birth."


The room had gone silent.


Andrew looked uncomfortable.


"Your other self asked me."


Another pause.


"He assumed you'd be in Oakland."


Adam remembered the familiar feeling.


Another plan.


Another decision.


Another future somebody had built for him.


Then Andrew surprised him.


"But I don't think that's right anymore."


Adam had looked up.


"What?"


Andrew sighed.


"I promised him I'd take care of Jane."


Another.


"And I will."


Another.


"But if you're staying..."


Another.


"If you're trying this hard to be part of your daughter's life..."


He shook his head.


"I don't think I should be standing in your place."


The memory faded.


Another contraction hit.


Jane cursed.


Loudly.


Adam immediately refocused.


"You're doing great."


"I'm absolutely not."


Another contraction.


"I hate everyone."


Adam nodded.


"Fair."


That somehow made her laugh.


A minute later they pulled into the hospital.


Scott was already waiting.


The moment he saw them he moved into physician mode.


"How far apart?"


"Under three minutes."


Adam answered immediately.


"Getting stronger."


Scott nodded.


"Let's move."


Within minutes Jane was in Labor and Delivery.


Adam scrubbed in.


Pulled on a gown.


Mask.


Cap.


The routine familiar.


Comforting even.


Then he stepped inside.


Jane looked exhausted.


Pale.


Scared.


Her eyes found him immediately.


And for a second she froze.


There it was again.


That look.


The one she'd been seeing for weeks.


The one she couldn't explain.


The one that hurt.


The one that felt familiar.


"Jane."


His voice was calm.


Steady.


"I'm here."


Another contraction hit.


She cried out.


Adam immediately took her hand.


"You are doing great."


Another.


"Just keep breathing."


Another.


"I've got you."


The words settled something inside her.


For one brief moment.


Enough.


Hours passed.


Labor dragged on.


Jane became increasingly exhausted.


At one point Scott looked up from the monitors.


Then toward Adam.


"Keep talking to her, Carter."


Adam nodded.


Immediately leaning closer.


"Hey."


A pause.


"Look at me."


Jane's eyes fluttered open.


Barely.


"That's it."


Another.


"Stay with me."


His thumb brushed gently across her hand.


Without thinking.


Without planning.


The words simply came.


"Sweetheart."


Another.


"Look at me."


Her eyes opened slightly wider.


"I promised I'd be here."


Another.


"And I am."


Another.


"So work with me."


Another.


"Just a little longer."


Jane stared at him.


Through exhaustion.


Through pain.


Through fear.


And for one impossible second...


She let herself believe.


Not because she was confused.


Not because she was hallucinating.


But because somewhere deep down she understood what he'd tried so desperately to explain.


Then everything changed.


Monitor alarms.


Voices.


Movement.


Scott looked up sharply.


"Baby's breech."


Another.


"Heart rate's dropping."


The room went silent.


Then:


"We're taking her now."


Everything accelerated.


Operating room.


Nurses.


Doctors.


Lights.


Machines.


The last thing Jane felt before everything faded was Adam's hand holding hers.


Then darkness.


The procedure happened quickly.


Too quickly for Adam's liking.


He stood where they told him to stand.


Watching.


Waiting.


Trying not to imagine every possible thing that could go wrong.


Then suddenly—


A cry.


Sharp.


Tiny.


Angry.


Alive.


The sound hit him like a freight train.


For a moment Adam couldn't breathe.


Couldn't think.


Couldn't move.


The nurse lifted her.


And everything stopped.


Lizzy.


His daughter.


Their daughter.


Perfect.


The nurse carried her over.


"Would you like to hold her, Dad?"


Dad.


The word nearly destroyed him.


Adam nodded.


Unable to speak.


The nurse carefully placed Lizzy into his arms.


And just like that...


Everything changed.


She was so small.


Smaller than he'd imagined.


Wrapped in blankets.


Tiny fingers.


Tiny nose.


Tiny cries.


Real.


Completely and undeniably real.


Adam stared down at her.


Tears immediately filling his eyes.


"Hi, Lizzy."


His voice broke.


"Hi."


A shaky laugh escaped him.


"You have absolutely no idea how much trouble you caused."


The nurse smiled.


Adam couldn't stop looking at her.


Every video.


Every sonogram.


Every nursery picture.


Every argument.


Every conversation.


Every fear.


Everything had led here.


For a split second he understood his other self perfectly.


Completely.


Without reservation.


Without confusion.


Without resentment.


Love.


Just love.


The kind that existed before understanding.


Before logic.


Before reason.


Adam carefully kissed the top of Lizzy's head.


Then reluctantly handed her to the nurses.


His eyes immediately turned toward Jane.


The room suddenly felt wrong.


Too quiet.


Too still.


Adam crossed the room.


Then stopped beside her.


Jane looked impossibly pale.


Exhausted.


Unconscious.


But here.


Still here.


Adam reached for her hand.


The same hand that had nearly broken his fingers during labor.


And held it gently.


"Hey."


The word barely escaped.


A tear slid down his cheek.


"She's here."


Another.


"She's perfect."


Adam squeezed her hand.


"You did it."


His voice cracked.


"And I know you can't hear me right now."


Another.


"But she's okay."


Another.


"You're both okay."


Then Jane's fingers twitched.


Adam froze.


Another movement.


Small.


But unmistakable.


"Jane?"


Her eyes fluttered.


Slowly.


Trying to focus.


Trying to fight through the anesthesia.


Adam immediately leaned forward.


"Hey."


A smile broke across his face.


"Hey."


Jane blinked.


Confused.


Then found him.


The mask.


The scrubs.


The eyes.


Those eyes.


For a moment she simply stared.


Then her lips moved.


"Lizzy?"


Adam laughed.


A mixture of relief and tears.


"She's okay."


Another.


"She's perfect."


Jane's eyes immediately filled.


The fear she'd been carrying for nine months finally breaking.


"Really?"


"Really."


Adam squeezed her hand.


"You have a daughter."


A tear slid down Jane's cheek.


She smiled weakly.


"Our daughter."


The words were barely above a whisper.


So quiet he almost thought he'd imagined them.


But then Jane opened her eyes again.


Still exhausted.


Still fighting the lingering effects of anesthesia.


"Our daughter."


Another tear slipped free.


"Is she okay?"


Adam froze.


Completely.


Because for a moment he couldn't breathe.


Not after the conversation in the apartment.


Not after all the arguments.


Not after pleading with her to understand.


Not after begging her to let him be there.


Whatever the reason...


Whether it was exhaustion.


Relief.


Or simply the truth finally slipping past her fear...


It felt like hope.


Real hope.


Adam leaned forward.


And pressed a gentle kiss against her forehead.


"You did good."


His hand brushed a loose strand of hair away from her face.


"You did so good, Jane."


The emotion he'd been holding back since the operating room finally caught up to him.


The fear.


The relief.


The love.


Everything.


He looked at her for a long moment.


At the woman who had fought for hours.


The woman who had just brought their daughter into the world.


And before he could stop himself...


The truth escaped.


"I love you so much."


The words were barely above a whisper.


Not meant to ask anything from her.


Simply true.


Jane's eyes fluttered open again.


Heavy.


Unfocused.


Drugged.


Exhausted.


She smiled.


The smallest smile.


And squeezed his hand weakly.


"I love you too."


Adam's heart stopped.


For one impossible second.


Then immediately started breaking all over again.


Because he didn't know.


He honestly didn't know.


Whether she was talking to him.


Or to the man she thought was standing beside her.


The man who had dreamed about Lizzy for three years.


The man who had promised to be there when she was born.


The man she'd lost.


Or maybe...


For the first time...


The distinction wasn't quite as clear anymore.


Jane's eyes drifted closed again.


Resting.


Safe.


Still holding his hand.


Adam sat there for a long time.


Unable to let go.


Unable to stop looking at her.


And for the first time since waking up three years too late...


He allowed himself to hope.


Just a little.
