# Chapter 78: Bear Tracks


Jane woke slowly.


Warm.


Comfortable.


Safe.


For a moment she didn't move.


Her head rested against Adam's bare chest. One of his arms was wrapped around her waist while the other lay loosely across her shoulders. She could hear the steady rhythm of his breathing beneath her ear.


A small smile appeared.


She was happy.


Really happy.


Lizzy had slept through the night.


For the first time in what felt like forever, she and Adam had gotten an entire night to themselves.


No midnight bottle.


No diaper emergencies.


No six-month-old demanding immediate attention.


Just them.


Jane glanced at the clock.


5:02 AM.


She smiled.


She couldn't remember the last time she'd woken up feeling this content.


Then she heard it.


Thump.


Thump.


Thump.


Jane closed her eyes.


And sighed.


Andrew.


Upstairs.


She could practically see it.


Coming back from one of his ridiculous morning runs.


Moping.


Sulking.


Pacing.


Like a five-year-old who'd just had his favorite toy taken away.


The thumping continued.


Adam slept right through it.


Of course he did.


Nothing short of a natural disaster could wake him.


Jane looked up at him.


Even asleep he looked peaceful.


She leaned forward and kissed him softly.


Adam made a sleepy sound, pulled her a little closer, kissed her back without opening his eyes and immediately drifted back to sleep.


Jane laughed quietly.


"Good answer."


Carefully disentangling herself, she slipped from the bed and pulled on her robe.


A moment later she was peeking into Lizzy's nursery.


Lizzy was already awake.


Wide-eyed.


Waiting.


"Well, good morning, beautiful girl."


Lizzy immediately kicked her legs.


Jane smiled.


"You know, your godfather has apparently decided to turn into a bear this morning."


Lizzy made a happy sound.


"Mm-hmm."


Jane nodded.


"He stomped your mommy out of her well-deserved night with daddy."


Another nod.


"But you and I are going to go do something about him right now."


Lizzy seemed delighted by this plan.


A few minutes later Jane had changed her, prepared a bottle, and headed for the elevator.


She rode upstairs.


Stepped into the hallway.


Used her key.


And walked straight into Andrew's apartment.


Andrew was sprawled across the couch.


Still wearing his running clothes.


Barefoot.


Slightly sweaty.


And looking profoundly annoyed by life.


He looked up at the intrusion.


His expression immediately darkened.


Then Jane shoved Lizzy into his arms.


The transformation was instantaneous.


Andrew's entire face softened.


"Well, good morning, sweetheart."


He kissed the top of Lizzy's head.


"Only one day and I already missed you."


Lizzy immediately grabbed his shirt.


Andrew smiled.


"You missed me too, didn't you?"


Jane crossed her arms.


"Well good morning to you too."


Andrew glanced at her briefly.


Then looked right back at Lizzy.


"Hi Jane."


Another kiss on Lizzy's forehead.


"Did your mother wake you up at an unreasonable hour?"


Jane stared at him.


"You are impossible."


Andrew ignored her.


"Hey Lizzy."


Another.


"Did I ever tell you how annoying your mother can be?"


Jane opened her mouth.


Andrew kept going.


"Who invites themselves into somebody's apartment at five in the morning?"


Another.


"That is not normal behavior."


He looked down at Lizzy.


"She's certifiable."


Another.


"You, however, are always welcome."


Another.


"Just leave your mother at home."


Jane threw a couch pillow at him.


Andrew caught it one-handed.


Still holding Lizzy.


Still not looking at Jane.


"Show off."


"Thank you."


Jane rolled her eyes.


"You know some of us like sleep."


Another.


"You may want to stop stomping around up here like a five-year-old."


Andrew smirked.


"Well."


Another.


"I hope I didn't interrupt anything."


Jane immediately pointed the pillow at him.


"Why are you up so early?"


Another.


"I know you do these ridiculous morning runs every day."


Another.


"But five in the morning?"


She dropped onto the couch beside him.


Letting her head fall back.


Already tired again.


"I couldn't sleep."


He shrugged.


"So I went to run it off."


Another kiss to Lizzy's head.


Another complete refusal to look at Jane.


"Don’t you and Adam have a shift in a few hours?"


Jane groaned.


"Unfortunately."


"You."


Another.


"Adam."


Another.


"And Ally."


He finally looked over.


"How exactly are you planning on functioning today?"


Jane didn't even open her eyes.


"Coffee."


Andrew nodded.


"And more Coffee."


Another.


"Lots of coffee."


"Exactly."


For a moment they sat quietly.


Then Andrew finally turned toward her.


"What are you doing here, Jane?"


Another.


"I literally saw you a few hours ago."


Jane opened one eye.


Andrew was watching her now.


Not annoyed.


Concerned.


Curious.


"Are you already worried about me?"


Her shoulders dropped.


"No."


Another.


"Well..."


Another.


"Maybe."


Andrew laughed.


"What?"


Jane rubbed her face.


"I heard you stomping around."


Another.


"So yeah."


Another.


"I guess I got worried."


Andrew leaned back into the couch.


Lizzy sprawled happily across his chest.


"I'm fine."


Another.


"I promise."


Jane didn't look convinced.


Andrew sighed.


"This."


He pointed at her.


Another.


"This is exactly what I was afraid you'd do."


Jane frowned.


"What?"


"Don't get me wrong."


Another.


"I'm grateful you care."


Another.


"But you need to get a handle on those protective instincts."


His voice softened.


"I promise you I'm okay."


Jane stared at him.


He stared back.


Neither budged.


Finally:


"Okay."


Andrew raised an eyebrow.


"You don't believe me."


"Not even a little."


"Fair."


That earned a laugh from both of them.


Jane sat up.


"Do you want to come downstairs for breakfast?"


Andrew laughed.


"That's why you're here?"


"Maybe."


Another.


"Adam and I have a favor to ask."


This was a complete lie.


Or at least mostly a lie.


Andrew knew it immediately.


The look on his face said so.


Jane knew he knew.


Andrew knew she knew.


Neither acknowledged it.


"Okay."


Another.


"But for the love of God tell me Adam is making breakfast."


Jane narrowed her eyes.


Andrew continued.


"Because I don't need food poisoning on top of a broken heart."


Jane grabbed the pillow again.


Andrew barely had time to laugh before Lizzy started giggling uncontrollably.


The attack was abandoned.


Immediately.


"Traitor."


Jane informed her daughter.


Lizzy looked delighted.


Andrew handed her the bottle.


Lizzy accepted it happily.


For a few minutes neither adult spoke.


They just watched her.


Then Jane looked over again.


Really looked.


At the exhaustion.


The sleeplessness.


The sadness he was trying not to show.


"Are you really okay?"


This time Andrew didn't laugh.


Didn't dodge.


Didn't deflect.


He looked down at Lizzy.


Then back at Jane.


"No."


Another.


"I'm not."


Jane's heart immediately tightened.


Andrew smiled softly.


"But I will be."


Another.


"Stop worrying."


Another.


"I promise I'll live."


He kissed the top of Lizzy's head.


Another.


"Besides."


Another.


"With you."


Another.


"Lizzy."


Another.


"And Adam downstairs."


His smile widened slightly.


"I think I'm going to be okay."


Jane felt herself relax.


Just a little.


"Kirby's being released in a couple weeks."


Andrew nodded.


"I know."


"He'll be staying with you."


"Yep."


Jane smiled.


"Maybe once he's here I'll worry less."


Andrew laughed.


"Oh."


Another.


"Once he's here Adam will be up here constantly."


Jane laughed too.


"Did you know they're friends now?"


Andrew shook his head.


Still amused by the entire thing.


"Yeah."


Another.


"They hit it off remarkably fast."


Another.


"It took me months to break through Adam's defenses."


Another.


"Kirby wakes up from an eight-year coma and suddenly they're best friends."


Jane laughed.


"Fair."


Andrew smiled.


"I get it though."


Another.


"They actually have a lot in common."


He thought for a moment.


Then:


"Weirdly enough..."


Another.


"I think I had more in common with the other guy."


Jane blinked.


Then laughed.


Because Adam had practically said the same thing the night before.


"We're a strange family."


Andrew looked over at her.


Really looked.


Then smiled.


A warm.


Tender.


Entirely unguarded smile.


"Yeah."


Another.


"We are."


He shifted Lizzy slightly.


Then reached over and pulled Jane into a side hug.


"I am lucky to have you all."


Jane immediately wrapped her arms around him.


Andrew rested his cheek against the top of her head.


"Seriously, Jane."


Another.


"Thank you."


He kissed her forehead.


"I love you."


Jane smiled.


Tighter.


"I love you too."


For a moment none of them moved.


Then Andrew gently nudged her.


"Okay."


Another.


"I'm kicking you out now."


Jane laughed.


"I thought you were coming to breakfast."


"I am."


Another.


"In a couple hours."


Another.


"I promise."


He kissed Lizzy's forehead.


"Go back to your mother, sweetheart and keep your mom downstairs otherwise, she will drive your godfather completely crazy!"


Another.


"I'll see you in a little while."


Reluctantly, Jane took her back.


She stood.


Adjusted Lizzy on her hip.


Then headed toward the door.


Andrew stood with them.


Of course he did.


Jane looked back.


"You know we're only going downstairs, right?"


Andrew opened the door.


"I'm aware."


Jane laughed.


"Andrew."


"Jane."


Another.


"Just get in the elevator."


She rolled her eyes.


But smiled.


Because some things never changed.


Together they walked down the hallway.


Andrew carrying the bottle.


Jane carrying Lizzy.


When they reached the elevator, Andrew handed the bottle back to Jane.


Then leaned forward and kissed Lizzy's forehead one last time.


"I'll see you later, sweetheart."


Lizzy immediately reached for his face.


Which made him smile.


Jane watched the whole thing.


Warm.


Fond.


Entirely unsurprised.


The elevator doors opened.


Jane stepped inside.


Then turned.


"A couple hours."


Andrew nodded.


"I said I'd be there."


Another.


"I'll be there."


Jane smiled.


The doors started to close.


Andrew stood there watching them.


One hand in his pocket.


The other raised in a small wave toward Lizzy.


He didn't move until the doors fully closed.


Then he stood there for another moment.


Quietly staring at the brushed metal doors.


Finally he smiled.


Shaking his head.


Feeling fortunate.


Loved.


And for the first time since the hospital, maybe just a little less alone.
