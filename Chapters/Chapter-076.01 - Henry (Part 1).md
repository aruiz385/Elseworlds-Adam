# Chapter 76: Henry (Part 1)


Jane and Andrew arrived at Labor and Delivery slightly out of breath.


Not because they were late.


Because somehow, whenever it involved James and Karen, they always ended up running.


Some things never changed.


The waiting room was unusually quiet.


No rushing nurses.


No alarms.


No chaos.


Just anticipation.


The kind that sat in your chest and waited.


Jane dropped into one of the chairs.


Andrew sat beside her.


For a few moments neither spoke.


Then Jane smiled.


Andrew glanced over.


"What?"


She shook her head.


"I can't believe this was us six months ago."


Andrew laughed softly.


"The running?"


"The chaos."


Jane leaned back in her chair.


"The panic."


Another.


"The complete certainty that I had absolutely no idea what I was doing."


Andrew nodded.


"That part was accurate."


Jane rolled her eyes.


"Thanks."


"Anytime."


She laughed.


Then her expression softened.


"It feels like yesterday."


Another.


"And somehow it feels like ten years ago."


Andrew nodded.


Because he understood exactly what she meant.


Jane looked down at her hands.


Then over at him.


"You were supposed to be the one with me that day."


Andrew's smile faded.


For a moment he didn't answer.


Then he nodded.


"I know."


A quiet settled between them.


"I actually wanted to talk to you about that."


Jane turned toward him.


Andrew leaned forward, resting his forearms on his knees.


"When Adam's alter asked me to be there if he couldn't..."


Another.


"I agreed without hesitation."


Another.


"Because I wasn't going to let you go through that alone."


Jane smiled softly.


"I know."


Andrew nodded.


"But by the time Lizzy was born..."


Another.


"Everything had changed."


Jane stayed quiet.


Listening.


"I saw how hard Adam was trying."


Another.


"Not because anyone expected him to."


Another.


"Not because he thought he'd get something out of it."


Another.


"Because he loved you."


Jane felt her chest tighten.


Andrew continued.


"He built Lizzy's crib."


Another.


"He finished the nursery."


Another.


"He helped you move furniture."


Another.


"Built shelves."


Another.


"Carried things you had absolutely no business carrying while you were pregnant."


Jane rolled her eyes.


"I was pregnant. Not disabled."


"You were carrying a human being."


Andrew corrected.


Another.


"My point is..."


He shook his head.


"He kept showing up."


Another.


"Every day."


Another.


"Every chance he got."


Another.


"And then he walked away from an attending position at UCSF Oakland."


Jane looked down.


Andrew continued.


"An incredible opportunity."


Another.


"Because he wanted to stay here."


Another.


"With you."


Another.


"With Lizzy."


A quiet settled between them.


"And eventually it stopped feeling right."


Jane frowned slightly.


"What did?"


"The idea of me being there instead of him."


The words landed softly between them.


"I knew what his alter had asked."


Another.


"And for a while I thought that's what was going to happen."


Another.


"But the more I looked at everything..."


Another.


"The more obvious it became that he'd already earned that place."


Another.


"He just needed somebody to tell him to take it."


Jane's eyes filled.


"So you talked to him."


Andrew nodded.


"So I talked to him."


Jane reached over and squeezed his hand.


"Thank you."


Andrew shrugged.


"It worked out exactly the way it was supposed to."


Another.


"You got Adam."


Another.


"Adam got you."


Another.


"Lizzy got both of you."


His smile softened.


"I'm really happy for you, Jane."


The sincerity in his voice made her chest ache.


Because she knew he meant it.


"Thank you."


For a moment they sat quietly.


Then Andrew's eyes narrowed.


Immediately.


Jane followed his gaze.


Straight to her purse.


Specifically the manila folder sticking out of it.


The divorce papers.


Andrew stared.


Once.


Twice.


Then looked at her.


Slowly.


"Jane."


She immediately groaned.


"No."


Andrew pointed.


"Why are those still in your bag?"


Jane looked away.


Andrew frowned.


"Jane."


His eyes narrowed.


"You filed them, right?"


Silence.


Andrew blinked.


"Jane."


She stared at the opposite wall.


Andrew sat up straighter.


"You signed them, right?"


Jane couldn't look at him.


The silence said everything.


Andrew's eyes widened.


"Oh my God."


Jane rubbed her forehead.


"Andrew..."


"You haven't even signed them?"


She still couldn't look at him.


Andrew stared.


Genuinely stunned.


"Jane."


Another.


"Kirby signed them weeks ago."


"I know."


Her voice was small.


Andrew leaned back.


Running a hand through his hair.


Trying to process what he'd just heard.


"Jesus Christ."


Jane glared at him.


"That's helpful."


"I'm serious."


He looked at her.


"Jane, you realize how much this would hurt Adam if he knew?"


The words landed exactly where he intended them to.


Jane flinched.


"I know."


"Do you?"


Another.


"Because from where I'm sitting—"


Jane cut him off.


"Adam and I are talking about it tonight."


That stopped him.


Slightly.


"It isn't that I don't want to sign them."


Another.


"It isn't that I don't want to file them."


Another.


"It isn't that I don't want to marry Adam."


She looked down at her hands.


"It isn't any of that."


Andrew waited.


Jane sighed.


"It's bigger than the papers."


Another.


"I need time to process what they mean."


Andrew looked at her.


Trying to understand.


Struggling.


Because every protective instinct he possessed was suddenly on high alert.


"Jane."


Another.


"You're standing in a doorway."


Another.


"Kirby already made the decision."


Another.


"You're the only person still standing there."


Jane shook her head.


"You don't understand."


"Then help me understand."


Jane swallowed.


"It isn't about choosing Adam."


Another.


"I chose Adam."


Another.


"A long time ago."


Andrew nodded.


Because he believed her.


"It just..."


She struggled.


"Making it official means something."


Another.


"It means letting go of something I've been holding onto for years."


Andrew's expression softened slightly.


But not much.


Because he was still worried.


"So process it after it's filed."


Jane's eyes narrowed immediately.


"Andrew."


"I'm serious."


Another.


"If it were me, it'd already be done."


Jane sat back.


Annoyed now.


"There it is."


Andrew frowned.


"What?"


"The difference between you and Adam."


Another.


"Adam lets me make my own decisions."


Andrew looked offended.


"I'm not trying to make the decision for you."


Another.


"There isn't a decision to make."


Jane stared.


Andrew continued.


"Kirby already made it."


Another.


"You just have to make it official."


Another.


"So you can marry Adam and ride off into the sunset."


Jane looked visibly irritated.


Andrew immediately realized he'd stepped on a landmine.


Again.


Fortunately for both of them, the Labor and Delivery doors opened before either could say something they would regret.


James stepped into the hallway.


Exhausted.


Emotional.


Completely overwhelmed.


And happier than either of them had ever seen him.


Both Jane and Andrew stood immediately.


James grinned.


"You two ready to meet your godson?"


And just like that, everything else disappeared.
