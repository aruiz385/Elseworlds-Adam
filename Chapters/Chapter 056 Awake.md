# Chapter 56: Awake


The next several hours passed slowly.


Scott came and went.


Nurses checked on Kirby.


Machines beeped steadily.


Nobody wanted to leave.


Nobody wanted to miss anything.


The failed wake-up had shaken all of them.


Because for a few brief minutes Kirby had been awake.


Alive.


Looking at them.


And terrified.


By evening Andrew looked worse than he had that morning.


Kelly noticed first.


Of course she did.


She always noticed.


"Andrew."


He didn't look up.


"Andrew."


Nothing.


He continued staring through the glass at his brother.


As though watching him closely enough would somehow keep him safe.


Kelly stepped directly in front of him.


"You're done."


Andrew blinked.


"What?"


"You're done."


Another.


"You haven't slept."


Another.


"You haven't eaten."


Another.


"And if I have to physically drag you to the lounge, I will."


Despite everything, Jane smiled.


Even Scott looked supportive of the idea.


"Kelly."


Andrew rubbed his eyes.


"I'm fine."


"No."


Her voice softened.


"You aren't."


Another.


"Your brother is alive."


Another.


"The doctors are doing everything they can."


Another.


"You collapsing isn't helping him."


The fight slowly left him.


Because deep down he knew she was right.


Jonathan stood from his chair.


Slowly.


Looking exhausted.


Older.


More fragile than Jane had ever seen him.


"I should probably go home too."


Nobody argued.


The last week had aged him years.


Scott appeared a few minutes later.


Looked around the room.


Then pointed.


"Everybody goes home."


The room immediately protested.


Scott sighed.


"The patient is stable."


Another.


"The family is not."


Even Kelly laughed.


Which meant it was probably true.


Eventually people began gathering their things.


Jonathan hugged Andrew.


Longer than usual.


Neither man said much.


There weren't enough words.


Kelly slipped her hand into Andrew's.


Steady.


Grounding him.


The way she always did.


Then Adam appeared beside Jane.


The last person still standing.


Of course.


He looked through the glass toward Kirby.


Then back at Jane.


"Jane."


She turned toward him.


Adam's expression softened immediately.


"What do you want to do?"


Jane frowned slightly.


"What do you mean?"


"One of us needs to go relieve your parents and be with Lizzy."


Another.


"She's going to wake up looking for us."


The words hit Jane immediately.


Their daughter.


Their little girl.


The life waiting for them outside these hospital walls.


Jane looked toward Kirby's room.


Then toward the hallway where Kelly was still trying to get Andrew to rest.


She sighed.


"I think I need to stay."


The words hurt.


"I think it has to be me."


Adam nodded.


Listening.


Not interrupting.


Not trying to change her mind.


"Andrew looks like he's going to collapse at any minute."


Another.


"He's in no shape to be the one who stays."


Another.


"And if Kirby wakes up..."


She swallowed hard.


"I think somebody needs to be here."


Adam reached for her hand.


Immediately.


Jane squeezed it.


"I hope you understand."


Tears filled her eyes.


"Because I hate this."


Another.


"I hate having to stay."


Another.


"I hate being away from you."


Another.


"And I hate being away from Lizzy."


Adam's expression softened even more.


"Jane."


Another.


"It's okay."


Another.


"I understand."


His eyes held hers.


And she knew he meant it.


"I hate it too."


Another.


"I'll miss you."


A sad smile appeared.


"Honestly?"


Another.


"I hate spending the night without you."


That earned the smallest laugh through her tears.


"But I understand."


The relief on Jane's face was immediate.


Because of course he understood.


Somehow he always did.


"Thank you."


Another.


"I love you so much."


She looked at him the way she always did when she forgot anybody else was in the room.


Completely.


Hopelessly.


In love.


Adam smiled.


"I love you too."


Then he leaned down and kissed her.


Slowly.


Tenderly.


The kind of kiss that made the rest of the world disappear for a second.


When they finally pulled apart, he brushed his thumb across her cheek.


"I'll be back in the morning."


Another.


"With lots of coffee."


He winked.


And despite everything, Jane laughed.


"There better be."


Adam grinned.


Then squeezed her hand one last time.


Before heading toward the elevator.


Back to Lizzy.


Back to their family.


Jane watched him disappear around the corner.


Then took a deep breath.


And walked back into Kirby's room.


Kelly eventually managed to convince Andrew to leave the ICU.


Not home.


Just to the doctors' lounge.


To shower.


To sleep.


To be something other than Kirby's brother for a few hours.


"I'm staying."


Andrew immediately looked at Jane.


"No."


"I'm staying."


Another.


"If he wakes up again, somebody should be here."


Andrew hesitated.


Because he knew exactly who Kirby would want to see.


Because deep down, so did she.


"Okay."


His voice was tired.


"Call me."


"I will."


Another.


"Try to sleep."


That earned the smallest smile.


"You first."


For a brief moment they smiled at each other.


Then Kelly led him away.


Leaving Jane alone with Kirby.


Just as she'd intended.


Because despite everything...


Despite Adam waiting at home.


Despite Lizzy.


Despite how complicated her life had become.


Jane couldn't bring herself to leave.


Not tonight.


Not after watching him panic.


Not after watching Andrew break apart.


Not after watching Jonathan cry.


So she stayed.


The hours crept slowly by.


The hospital grew quieter.


The lights dimmed.


The monitors continued their steady rhythm.


Eventually Jane moved her chair closer to the bed.


She reached for Kirby's hand.


And held it.


Because she didn't know what else to do.


Because letting go somehow felt wrong.


Exhaustion settled over her.


Heavy.


Merciless.


At some point her head drifted sideways.


Resting against the edge of the mattress.


Still holding his hand.


Still keeping watch.


Sometime after three in the morning, Kirby woke up.


Slowly.


Carefully.


His body felt heavy.


Everything hurt.


Even breathing felt strange.


For several seconds he stared at the ceiling.


Trying to understand where he was.


The room was dark.


Quiet.


Different.


Then he felt it.


Warmth.


A hand in his.


His eyes followed it.


And found her.


Jane.


Asleep beside him.


Her cheek resting against the mattress.


Still holding on.


The sight hit him immediately.


Relief.


A relief so overwhelming it brought tears to his eyes.


She was here.


His wife was here.


Everything else could wait.


He tried to speak.


Nothing came out.


His throat burned.


His voice refused to cooperate.


Instead he squeezed her hand.


Weakly.


Once.


Nothing.


Then again.


A little stronger.


Jane stirred.


Slowly opening her eyes.


Disoriented.


Confused.


Then she looked up.


And froze.


Kirby was awake.


Really awake.


His eyes were open.


Watching her.


Tears already gathered in his.


The expression on his face made her heart stop.


Because she knew that look.


She had seen it countless times before.


The way he looked at her when they were together.


The way he looked at her on their wedding day.


The way he looked at her when he told her he loved her.


Every ounce of it was still there.


Jane felt tears immediately fill her eyes.


"Kirby..."


His own tears slipped down his cheeks.


Neither of them spoke.


Neither of them could.


Jane stood so quickly she nearly knocked over the chair.


Then she leaned over and wrapped her arms around him.


Careful of the wires.


Careful of the IVs.


Careful not to hurt him.


Kirby immediately closed his eyes.


A tear slid down his face.


And for the first time since waking up...


He relaxed.


She was here.


He wasn't alone.


The nightmare was over.


Jane held him tightly.


Relief washing through her.


Thank God.


Thank God.


He was alive.


He was okay.


Andrew hadn't lost his brother.


Jonathan hadn't lost his son.


The miracle had happened.


But underneath the relief came something else.


Guilt.


Crushing.


Because she knew something Kirby didn't.


As far as Kirby knew...


He had come home.


And Jane suddenly realized that by morning...


Everyone would be asking her to help decide how much of the truth he could handle.


And when.
