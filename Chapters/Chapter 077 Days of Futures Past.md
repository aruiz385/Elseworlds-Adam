"And ensure he isn't babysitting alone."


Adam sighed.


Slowly.


Deeply.


The sigh of a man who already knew exactly where this was going.


Somehow.


Some way.


Ally was going to end up involved.


He didn't know how.


He didn't know when.


But he knew his fiancée.


And he knew that look.


Jane smiled.


"You hate my idea."


Adam opened one eye.


"I didn't say that."


"You sighed."


"Because I know this isn't just an idea."


Another.


"It's already becoming a plan."


Jane looked entirely unapologetic.


Which told him everything he needed to know.


Adam shook his head.


Laughing quietly.


Because the truth was he would probably help her.


Not because Andrew needed it.


Not because Ally might be good for him.


Not even because Jane's matchmaking schemes occasionally worked.


But because he loved her.


And being her partner in crime had become one of his favorite things.


Jane nestled against his shoulder.


"So you'll help?"


Adam wrapped an arm around her.


Pulling her closer.


"Unfortunately."


Jane smiled.


"You adore me."


"A terrible character flaw."


Adam replied.


Another.


"I'm working on it."


Jane laughed.


And for the first time all day, neither of them thought about the futures they had lost.


Only the one they were building together.
