# Chapter 17: The Morning After

Around four in the morning, Adam opened his eyes.

For a moment he didn't move.

He simply stared at Jane.

She was asleep against him beneath a pile of blankets, one arm draped across his chest as though she'd fallen asleep afraid he might disappear if she let go.

The thought made his chest ache.

Not in a bad way.

In the best way.

He still couldn't believe any of this was real.

Just yesterday morning he had been terrified.

Terrified of what she felt.

Terrified of what she didn't.

Terrified that she would realize how complicated he was and walk away.

Instead, she had told him she loved him.

She had chosen him.

Not Adam.

Not because he reminded her of someone else.

Him.

"Jane..." he whispered.

She stirred.

A moment later sleepy eyes opened and found his.

"Hey..."

"Hey."

She smiled automatically.

Then squinted at the dark room.

"What time is it?"

"Too early."

Jane frowned.

Then her eyes widened.

"Oh my God."

"What?"

"You have a six o'clock shift."

She sat up slightly.

"The terrible shift Andrew was talking about."

Adam groaned.

"Thank you for reminding me."

"You should be sleeping."

"You should be sleeping."

Jane ignored that.

"How much sleep did you get?"

Adam smiled.

The concern in her voice still felt foreign.

"Enough."

"That sounds like a lie."

"It probably is."

"Adam."

He laughed.

"You worry about me a lot."

Jane rolled her eyes.

"Someone has to."

The answer came so naturally that it surprised both of them.

Adam's smile immediately softened.

He looked down at their intertwined hands.

"You know..."

"What?"

His voice became quieter.

"I don't think I've ever had somebody worry about me before."

Jane's expression changed instantly.

The honesty caught her off guard.

Adam tried to laugh it off.

Failed.

"I spent so much time watching Adam have relationships."

A pause.

"Friends."

Another.

"People."

His gaze drifted toward her.

"And now you."

Jane squeezed his hand.

Hard.

"Well."

A small smile appeared.

"You have me now."

The words nearly undid him.

He closed his eyes briefly.

"You have no idea what that means to me."

The room became quiet.

Jane reached up and touched his cheek.

The gesture was gentle.

Instinctive.

Like she couldn't help herself.

"You deserve people too."

Adam leaned into her touch.

"So do you."

Jane looked away immediately.

Still uncomfortable with being on the receiving end of affection.

Adam smiled.

"There it is."

"What?"

"The inability to accept positive reinforcement."

Jane laughed.

"I've been told that's one of my flaws."

"I can think of several worse ones."

"Are you calling me difficult?"

"I would never."

The grin on his face suggested otherwise.

Jane threw a pillow at him.

Adam caught it.

Still smiling.

Then his attention drifted toward the clock.

Immediately his expression fell.

"Damn it."

"What?"

"I actually have to go."

Jane groaned dramatically.

"That's unfortunate."

"It really is."

The answer came too quickly.

Too honestly.

Jane studied him carefully.

"You really don't want to leave."

It wasn't a question.

Adam looked at her for a long moment.

Then shook his head.

"No."

A pause.

"Not even a little."

He brushed his thumb across her hand.

"I know this sounds ridiculous."

"Probably."

"The best night I've ever had was spending six hours eating terrible pizza and arguing with you about movies."

Jane smiled.

Adam's expression softened.

"I've spent most of my life feeling like I was borrowing things."

He stared at their hands.

"Borrowing time."

A pause.

"Borrowing a body."

Another.

"Borrowing a life."

Jane's heart broke.

"But last night..."

His voice lowered.

"Last night was mine."

Silence filled the room.

"And I got to spend it with you."

Jane didn't trust herself to speak.

Instead she wrapped her arms around him.

Adam immediately held her back.

Tightly.

Almost desperately.

"I love you."

The words were muffled against his shoulder.

For a moment Adam simply closed his eyes.

Still amazed every time she said them.

"I love you too."

A pause.

Then he laughed softly.

"Still can't believe you do."

Jane pulled back enough to look at him.

"Get used to it."

"I'm trying."

He kissed her.

Slowly.

Without urgency.

Just because he wanted to remember.

When they finally pulled apart, neither moved very far.

"Come find me after Dr. Ryan."

"I will."

"Promise?"

Jane rolled her eyes.

"There it is."

"What?"

"My favorite thing."

She laughed.

Then pointed toward the door.

"Go save lives, Doctor."

Adam stood reluctantly.

Very reluctantly.

At the doorway he stopped.

Turned.

Jane was already pulling the blanket around herself again.

Watching him.

Still there.

Still real.

"Jane?"

"What?"

A smile spread across his face.

"If you go back to sleep..."

She narrowed her eyes suspiciously.

"...dream about me."

Jane immediately threw another pillow.

Adam dodged it.

Laughing.

"Goodnight, Jane."

"Goodnight, Adam."

A pause.

"Or good morning, I guess."

He smiled one last time.

Then left.


At 5:55 a.m., Adam rushed onto the Oncology floor.

Technically he wasn't late.

But for Adam Carter, arriving five minutes before shift started bordered on recklessness.

The first person he saw was Scott.

Of course it was Scott.

The older man took one look at him and immediately smiled.

"Good morning."

Adam looked down.

Trying unsuccessfully to hide the smile.

"Good morning, Scott."

Scott folded his arms.

Interesting.

Very interesting.

Today's Adam looked suspiciously happy.

"So."

Adam sighed.

Here we go.

"So?"

"You don't look nearly as broody as usual."

"I have no idea what you're talking about."

The smile escaped anyway.

Scott laughed.

"Uhm huh."

Adam groaned.

"It isn't that obvious."

"It is painfully obvious."

Scott shook his head.

"I take it you spoke with Jane."

Adam looked away.

Which was answer enough.

"Yeah."

A smile appeared immediately.

"I did."

"Well?"

Adam leaned against the nurses' station.

"You were right."

Scott looked entirely too pleased with himself.

"Well obviously."

Adam rolled his eyes.

Scott's expression softened.

"Just do me a favor."

The protective godfather had entered the conversation.

"Anything."

"Be careful with her heart."

Adam's smile faded immediately.

"Scott..."

"No."

Scott pointed at him.

"I mean it."

A pause.

"She has had enough heartbreak for several lifetimes."

Adam nodded.

"I know."

His voice was quiet.

"I would never hurt her intentionally."

Scott believed him.

That wasn't the issue.

Adam looked down.

"I'm just worried about..."

"Adam."

Scott completed the sentence.

Adam nodded.

"Yeah."

A silence settled between them.

Then Scott asked:

"Have you looked into the therapy I suggested?"

Adam sighed.

Integration.

Again.

"You know we've tried before."

"The videos."

Scott nodded.

"I know."

Adam rubbed the back of his neck.

"If I try again and it works..."

He paused.

"I'm afraid I'll disappear."

The words felt heavier than ever.

Scott remained quiet for a long moment.

Then:

"You could disappear anyway."

Adam winced.

The honesty hurt.

"But if it works?"

Scott continued.

"You could become whole."

Adam looked away.

"Jane loves you."

A pause.

"Not Adam."

Another.

"She cared about him."

A sad smile.

"But she loves you."

The statement landed hard.

"Maybe if this works..."

Scott shrugged.

"She gets both."

Adam didn't answer.

Because part of him desperately wanted that.

And another part was terrified of it.

After a long silence, Adam spoke again.

"I talked to Andrew."

Scott stopped walking.

That got his attention immediately.

"You did?"

Adam nodded.

"Yesterday."

"Told him?"

"Everything."

Scott raised an eyebrow.

"DID?"

Adam nodded.

"The original Adam."

Another pause.

"The patient."

"The night Adam disappeared."

Scott studied him carefully.

"So he knows."

"He knows."

Adam nodded.

"I explained everything."

A small laugh escaped him.

"He was confused."

"As any reasonable person would be."

"But after I explained it, he actually took it really well."

Scott looked relieved.

"That's good."

"It was."

Adam nodded.

"For a little while."

Scott narrowed his eyes.

"Oh no."

"Yeah."

"What happened?"

Adam sighed heavily.

"He found out about Jane and me."

Scott closed his eyes immediately.

"Oh boy."

"Yeah."

Adam rubbed his face.

"He showed up at her apartment."

"Of course he did."

"Kelly heard music and got worried."

Scott nodded.

"Also not surprising."

"He came down to check on her."

Scott already knew where this was headed.

"And?"

"He found us."

The older man pinched the bridge of his nose.

"Wonderful."

"He knows."

Scott sighed.

"How did he react?"

Adam thought about it.

"Honestly?"

Scott waited.

"Better than expected."

That surprised him.

"Protective."

A pause.

"But mostly concerned."

"For Jane."

Adam nodded immediately.

"Exactly."

Scott folded his arms.

"Of course he is."

Adam frowned.

"What does that mean?"

Scott looked at him carefully.

"It means before you talk to Andrew today, you need to understand who he is."

Adam listened.

"You see an ex-boyfriend."

A pause.

"I don't."

Scott smiled sadly.

"I see the little boy who met Jane when he was five."

The statement made Adam pause.

"Jane, Andrew, and Kirby grew up together."

Another pause.

"They were inseparable."

Adam nodded quietly.

He knew.

Jane had told him enough stories.

"Kirby probably knew Jane better than anybody who ever lived."

Scott smiled faintly.

"But after Kirby died..."

A pause.

"Andrew became the person who knew her best."

Adam was quiet.

"He knows her tells."

Scott continued.

"The way she gets quiet before she cries."

"The way she pretends she's okay when she's not."

"The way she takes care of everybody else while falling apart herself."

A pause.

"He knows her heart."

The statement hung between them.

"And she knows his."

Adam looked down.

Scott noticed immediately.

"Don't."

"What?"

"Turn this into a competition."

Adam shook his head.

"I'm not."

"Good."

Scott nodded.

"Because you'll lose."

Adam blinked.

"What?"

Scott laughed.

"Not because Andrew is better than you."

A pause.

"Because you're competing with twenty-five years of history."

That landed.

Hard.

"They were each other's first everything."

Scott's voice softened.

"Andrew wasn't just her boyfriend."

A pause.

"He was her best friend."

Another.

"He is her family."

Adam looked away.

"The reason they aren't together isn't because they stopped loving each other."

Scott chose his words carefully.

"The problem was that Andrew saw a future with Jane."

A pause.

"And Jane couldn't."

Adam immediately understood.

The baby.

Kirby.

The guilt.

The fear.

Scott nodded.

"She convinced herself she couldn't give him the life he deserved."

A silence followed.

"Andrew didn't want to leave."

The words landed heavily.

"But eventually he respected what she asked of him."

Adam closed his eyes.

Then Scott delivered the part that mattered.

"The DID changes things."

Adam looked up.

"How?"

Scott sighed.

"Put yourself in Andrew's shoes."

A pause.

"The woman he has spent most of his life loving finally lets somebody in."

Another.

"And that somebody openly admits he may not always be here."

Adam felt his stomach drop.

Because he had thought the exact same thing.

Scott nodded.

"Andrew isn't worried because you're dating Jane."

A pause.

"He's worried because of what happens to Jane if you're gone."

The words hit too close to home.

"He's asking the same questions you ask yourself."

Adam looked away.

Because it was true.

Every day.

"What happens if Adam comes back?"

"What happens if I disappear?"

"What happens to Jane?"

Scott's voice softened.

"When you talk to him today, don't treat him like the enemy."

Adam nodded slowly.

"Because he isn't."

Another pause.

"He loves her too."

Not the same way.

Not anymore.

But he did.

And always would.

Scott smiled.

"Tell him you love her."

A pause.

"Tell him you're going to take care of her."

Another.

"And then listen."

Adam took a deep breath.

Because suddenly he understood.

The conversation with Andrew wasn't about winning.

The conversation was about Jane.

It always had been.

Scott clapped him on the shoulder.

"One more thing."

Adam looked up.

"If Andrew gives you a hard time..."

A pause.

"It means he cares."

Adam laughed softly.

"That's supposed to make me feel better?"

"No."

Scott grinned.

"But it's true."

Despite himself, Adam smiled.

"Let's do rounds."

Scott pointed down the hallway.

"You ready?"

Adam thought about Jane.

About waking up beside her.

About her telling him she loved him.

About the future.

Complicated as it was.

Then he smiled.

A real smile.

"Oh yeah."

For the first time in his life, he meant it.

"I've been ready for today."
