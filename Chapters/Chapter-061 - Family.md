# Chapter 61: Family


The first week settled into a rhythm.


Not a normal rhythm.


Nothing about this was normal.


But a rhythm anyway.


Every morning Adam arrived at the hospital carrying coffee in one hand and Jane's overnight bag in the other.


Fresh clothes.


Toiletries.


Whatever she'd forgotten.


Whatever she needed.


The overnight bag had become a permanent fixture again.


A fact Adam found deeply amusing.


And mildly offensive.


Mostly because after years of watching that bag appear and disappear from his apartment, it somehow still existed.


"I swear this thing is immortal."


Jane had laughed.


Adam had not appeared to be joking.


Not entirely.


Every morning followed the same pattern.


Coffee.


Breakfast.


A shower.


A check-in.


Adam making sure she had eaten something resembling food.


Jane pretending she didn't need someone to remind her.


Adam reminding her anyway.


During the day he worked.


Rounds.


Patients.


Charts.


Meetings.


The endless demands of hospital life.


But somehow he always found time.


A lunch break.


Ten minutes between patients.


Fifteen minutes before a meeting.


Whatever he could get.


And whenever possible, Jane escaped for a little while.


Long enough to sit with Adam.


Long enough to hold Lizzy.


Long enough to remember there was a life outside of ICU Room 401.


At night Adam went home.


Back to Lizzy.


Back to their apartment.


Back to an empty side of the bed he hated sleeping in.


Jane stayed.


Usually with Kirby.


Sometimes with Jonathan.


Occasionally with Andrew and Kelly.


The week moved slowly.


Painfully slowly.


And yet somehow Day Seven arrived.


Kirby was doing better.


Much better.


Physical therapy had him standing.


Walking short distances.


Talking.


Eating.


Recovering.


But he was also noticing things.


Questions.


Patterns.


Missing pieces.


Most notably...


Jane's absences.


She had become the center of his world.


The person he looked for first every morning.


The person he wanted beside him during therapy.


The person he searched for whenever he got confused.


And the explanations were becoming harder.


Much harder.


On the afternoon of Day Seven, Jane sat curled up on a couch in the doctors' lounge.


Lizzy asleep against her chest.


Adam beside them.


One arm draped behind her.


Quiet.


Peaceful.


For once.


The door opened.


Andrew stepped inside.


The exhaustion on his face immediately caught Jane's attention.


"Hey."


Andrew gave them a small smile.


Then sighed.


"I'm sorry."


Jane already knew.


"He asked for me?"


Andrew nodded.


Frustration flashed across his face.


Again.


"Yeah."


Another.


"He keeps asking where you are."


Another.


"And honestly?"


Another.


"I'm running out of excuses."


The room fell silent.


Because they all knew what that meant.


Time was running out.


Andrew looked toward Lizzy.


Immediately softening.


"Can I?"


Adam smiled.


"Of course."


A second later Lizzy was in Andrew's arms.


The transformation was immediate.


The stress.


The frustration.


The guilt.


Gone.


At least for a moment.


Andrew looked down at his goddaughter and smiled.


A genuine smile.


One Jane hadn't seen in days.


"Hey sweetheart."


He kissed her head gently.


"I'm sorry I haven't seen you much lately."


Another.


"Been a little preoccupied."


Lizzy yawned.


Completely unconcerned.


Andrew laughed.


"When all this calms down..."


Another.


"You and I are going to have a day."


Another.


"If your parents let me."


He looked toward Adam.


"Kelly and I will steal her away and completely spoil her."


Adam smiled.


"That sounds about right."


Andrew looked back down at Lizzy.


His smile softened.


"She is beautiful."


Another.


"I know I say that all the time."


Another.


"But seriously."


His eyes flickered toward Adam.


"It must be amazing being a dad."


Adam looked at Lizzy.


Then at Jane.


Then back at Andrew.


"Yeah."


A quiet smile appeared.


"Most days I feel like the luckiest guy on the planet."


Jane felt tears sting her eyes.


Immediately.


Because she saw it.


The way Andrew held Lizzy.


The love.


The tenderness.


The ease.


And despite everything...


Despite the years.


Despite the healing.


A small piece of her still remembered the baby they'd lost.


Adam saw it too.


Of course he did.


He always did.


Without saying a word, he gently took Lizzy from Andrew.


Giving them space.


Giving them privacy.


Giving them the conversation they needed to have.


Once Adam and Lizzy moved to the other side of the lounge, Andrew sat beside Jane.


For a moment neither spoke.


Then:


"We have to tell him soon."


Jane nodded.


"I know."


Andrew stared at the floor.


"He keeps looking for you."


Another.


"Every room."


Another.


"Every conversation."


Another.


"If you're not there, he asks where you are."


His voice tightened.


"I don't know what else to tell him."


Jane looked down.


Neither did she.


Andrew rubbed his face.


"I feel terrible."


Another.


"We're lying to him."


Another.


"And I know why."


Another.


"But it still feels awful."


He looked toward Adam.


Toward Lizzy.


Toward their life.


"And honestly?"


Another.


"I feel worse because I see what this is doing to you."


Jane blinked.


"What are you talking about?"


Andrew laughed softly.


"No."


Another.


"Don't do that."


Another.


"I can see it."


His voice softened.


"You miss your family."


Another.


"You haven't been home in a week."


Another.


"And you're carrying everyone."


Jane looked away.


Because he wasn't wrong.


Andrew shook his head.


"I need you to know how grateful I am."


Another.


"For everything."


Another.


"For staying."


Another.


"For helping him."


Another.


"For helping me."


Jane's eyes filled.


"Andrew."


Another.


"You don't need to thank me."


Another.


"I love both of you."


Another.


"You and Kirby are my family."


Another.


"So is Jonathan."


Her voice caught.


"You always will be."


Andrew immediately pulled her into a hug.


The familiar kind.


The kind that had existed long before romance.


Long before heartbreak.


Long before grief.


"I love you so much."


Jane smiled through tears.


"I love you too."


Another.


"We'll get through this."


Andrew nodded.


Together.


Always.


Eventually he pulled back.


"Scott filled me in on the plan."


Jane groaned.


"The Adam part?"


"The Adam part."


Andrew shook his head.


His reaction mirroring hers exactly.


"You okay with that?"


Jane stared across the room.


At Adam.


Holding Lizzy.


Making faces that were somehow captivating a four-month-old.


"Honestly?"


Another.


"I'm hesitant."


Another.


"But Adam really wants to help."


Andrew followed her gaze.


And smiled.


"Yeah."


Another.


"That sounds like him."


Because it did.


And despite everything happening...


Neither of them could deny the truth.


If anyone could sit in a room with Kirby and help him feel less alone...


It was probably Adam.
