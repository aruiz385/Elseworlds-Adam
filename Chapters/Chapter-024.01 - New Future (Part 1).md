# Chapter 24: New Futures (Part 1)

The next few weeks passed in a blur.


One appointment became another.

Then another.

Then another.


Suddenly there were calendars.

Schedules.

Prenatal appointments.

Blood work.

Ultrasounds.

Information packets.

Books.

More books.


And somehow in the middle of all of it, life kept moving.


The wedding was approaching.

Work continued.

Therapy continued.

The integration discussions continued.


Everything kept happening.

At the same time.


By the time they learned she was eight weeks along, Jane felt like she'd lived six months in three weeks.


Adam went to every appointment.

Every single one.


Not because she asked him to.


Because nothing short of a natural disaster was keeping him away.


"You're ridiculous."


Adam looked offended.


"Our child is in there."


Jane laughed.


"You sound ninety."


"I'm embracing fatherhood."


"You've known for three weeks."


"Three very important weeks."


The ultrasound technician was trying very hard not to laugh.


Jane wasn't doing much better.


Then the image appeared.


And suddenly Adam stopped talking.


Completely.


For almost an entire minute.


Which honestly worried Jane.


"Adam?"


No response.


"Adam."


Still nothing.


"Are you okay?"


He blinked.


Then pointed.


"That's our baby."


The awe in his voice made her chest ache.


Because he sounded completely overwhelmed.


"Yeah."


A smile slowly appeared.


"That's our baby."


Adam looked like he'd just witnessed a miracle.


Maybe he had.


Jane smiled.

Tried to smile.


Because she did feel happy.


Sometimes.


But other times?


The fear still found her.


Late at night.


During quiet moments.


When she thought about the future.


When she thought about integration.


When she thought about everything she stood to lose.


Dr. Ryan helped.

A lot.


More than Jane thought possible.


Their sessions shifted.


Less focus on surviving.

More focus on preparing.


More conversations about her medication.

Monitoring symptoms.

Adjusting treatment plans safely.


More conversations about fear.


A lot of conversations about fear.


Eventually Jane brought it to group therapy.


Which somehow made everything easier.


Hard.

But easier.


The fear became smaller once she said it out loud.


Once other people knew.


Adam had stopped attending group sessions a while ago.


At first she'd hated it.


Then she'd understood.


"You need a space that's yours."


That had been his argument.


"What if I want you there?"


Adam had smiled.


"You already have me everywhere else."


That wasn't exactly wrong.


Still.

She missed him.


Every time she sat in that room she found herself looking toward the chair beside her.


The empty chair.


And thinking about Original Adam.


The one who had dragged her into group therapy kicking and screaming.


The one who had sat beside her during those first awful sessions.


The one who had refused to let her quit.


Every single week.


*"Stay."*


That was what he'd always said.


*"Just stay."*


The memory made her smile.


And hurt.


At the same time.


Life had become very strange.


Karen and James' wedding sat only two weeks away.


Which was exactly when Jane finally told them.


Karen screamed.


Immediately.


"No."


"Karen."


"No."


Karen pointed dramatically.


"NO."


"Karen."


"YOU'RE PREGNANT?"


The entire restaurant turned to look at her.


James looked horrified.


Karen looked delighted.


Jane buried her face in her hands.


"Oh my God."


Karen immediately jumped out of her chair and hugged her.


Hard.


Then pulled back.


Narrowed her eyes.


And pointed.


"Are you trying to steal my wedding thunder?"


Jane laughed.


"What?"


"The timing feels suspicious."


"Karen."


"I'm just saying."


James pinched the bridge of his nose.


"I'm marrying her."


"Yes, you are."


The realization seemed to hit Karen all over again.


Then:


"Wait."


A pause.


"You can still fit in the maid of honor dress, right?"


"Karen!"


"What?"


"I'm being practical."


James laughed into his coffee.


"You deserve each other."


The next ten minutes mostly consisted of Karen demanding godmother status.


Immediately.


Aggressively.


Repeatedly.


"I call godmother."


"Of course! Who else would ask to be godmother?"


Jane hugged Karen tight. At least that was one thing that never would change. Karen would always be her Ride or Die.


As wedding preparations intensified, Jane and Andrew found themselves spending more time together.


The wedding venue was a gorgeous lakeside inn.


Which meant the entire thing had turned into a weekend-long event.


Meaning endless planning.


Meaning endless coordination.


Meaning Andrew and Jane were constantly talking.


About schedules.

Wedding logistics.

Karen.

James.


And eventually—

The baby.


One afternoon they sat overlooking the lake reviewing wedding timelines.


Andrew glanced over.


"How are you doing?"


Jane smiled.


"The answer you want or the real answer?"


"The real one."


She appreciated that he always asked.


Every time.


Not because he needed information.


Because he cared.


"I'm scared."


Andrew nodded.


"Yeah."


No judgment.

No fixing.


Just understanding.


"Did you schedule all your appointments?"


Jane laughed.


"There he is."


"What?"


"Doctor Call."


"You didn't answer my question."


"Yes."


"Good."


A pause.


"Call me if you need anything."


Jane smiled softly.


"I know."


And she did.


Before she left she stopped.


"Oh."


"What?"


A smile appeared.


"Tell Kelly thank you for me."


Andrew frowned.


"For what?"


"The bathroom conversation."


The realization hit immediately.


"Oh."


Jane laughed.


"Yeah."


Andrew smiled.


"I'll tell her. It meant a lot to me that she was there" Jane says with a slight smile.


Meanwhile, Adam was suffering.


Immensely.


At least according to him.


Because somehow Andrew had dragged him into helping plan James' bachelor party.


"Why am I here?"


Chris looked delighted.


"Excellent question."


Adam regretted arriving immediately.


The regret only grew worse when James casually mentioned:


"Jane's doing well."


Chris froze.


Waited.


Then his eyes narrowed.


Dangerously.


"What was that?"


James immediately realized his mistake.


Too late.


Chris looked between them.


Then gasped dramatically.


"No."


Nobody spoke.


"Oh my God."


Adam closed his eyes.


"No."


"OH MY GOD."


The entire restaurant turned toward them.


Chris pointed dramatically at Adam.


"What am I missing here?" Chris looked at James and then at Andrew and then At Adam


Adam immediately considered leaving.


Possibly the country.


James was laughing too hard to be helpful.


Andrew looked like he deeply regretted sharing the information.


Chris stood up.


Adam looks at Andrew who signals that he might as well tell him. "Jane and I are having a baby”


Chris stands up


"Sit down."


"I refuse."


"Chris."


"This is incredible."
