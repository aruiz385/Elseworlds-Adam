# Chapter 22: Orbit (Part 1)

The restaurant was beautiful.

Which was unfortunate considering Jane would've preferred somewhere less romantic and significantly less memorable.

Instead, Andrew and Kelly had somehow managed to pick the exact restaurant overlooking the bay where she and Adam had once had a very unofficial first date.

At least according to Adam.


"It wasn't a date."


"It was absolutely a date."


"It was not."


Adam looked at Andrew.


"It was a date."


Andrew immediately nodded.


"That sounds like a date."


Jane pointed at both of them.


"You don't get a vote."


Kelly laughed.


"What happened?"


Adam looked entirely too pleased with himself.


"I invited her out."


"You tricked me."


"I did no such thing."


"You absolutely did."


Andrew leaned forward.

Interested.


"This should be good."


Adam ignored her.


"We walked around the bay."


"A fact-finding mission."


"A date."


"A fact-finding mission."


"A date."


Jane groaned.


"You're impossible."


Adam grinned.


"I literally told you it was a date."


"You absolutely did not."


"I absolutely did."


Andrew was laughing now.


"What happened next?"


Adam smiled.


"She interrogated me for several hours."


"I asked reasonable questions."


"You interviewed me."


Kelly laughed.


"That's honestly exactly how I imagined it."


Adam nodded.


"Then eventually I kissed her."


Jane immediately blushed.


Andrew nearly choked on his drink.


"What?"


Adam shrugged.


"I was reasonably certain she wasn't going to kill me."


"Reasonably?"


"A strong seventy percent."


Kelly laughed so hard she had to put down her glass.


"You kissed her on a seventy percent certainty?"


Adam nodded.


"I like to live dangerously."


Jane covered her face.


"I hate this conversation."


"No, you don't."


Unfortunately, Adam was right.


The evening settled into easier territory after that.

Stories.

Laughter.

Wedding planning.

Chris being ridiculous.


Eventually Kelly ended up sharing the story of her first date with Andrew.


"I honestly didn't think he was going to call."


Andrew looked offended.


"I called."


"Three days later."


"I was busy."


Kelly laughed.


"You were nervous."


Andrew pointed.


"That's slander."


Jane smiled.

Then before she could stop herself, the words escaped.


"Based on that text message, you seemed pretty confident."


The table immediately grew quiet.


Jane froze.


Immediately realizing what she'd said.


The message.

The one Kelly had sent Andrew years ago.

The one she'd accidentally seen.


*Call me if that complication ever disappears.*


The memory hit harder than she expected.


"Oh."


Jane looked down.


"I'm sorry."


Immediately.


"I'm sorry. That was unfair."


Kelly's expression softened.


"No."


But Jane already felt it.

The old grief.

The old hurt.

The old version of herself.


Beside her, Adam quietly took her hand beneath the table.


No words.


Just his hand.


Steady.

Present.


The gesture helped.

A little.


Unfortunately, not enough.


The nausea arrived suddenly.


Strong.

Immediate.


Jane's face paled.


Adam noticed instantly.


"Jane?"


She shook her head.


"I'll be right back."


Before anybody could ask another question, she stood.

Then quickly made her way toward the restroom.


Adam was halfway out of his chair before Kelly stopped him.


"Adam."


He looked up.


"I'll go."


He hesitated.


Not because he didn't trust Kelly.


Because every instinct he had wanted to follow Jane.


Kelly seemed to understand immediately.


"I'll get her if she needs you."


Adam glanced toward the hallway.

Then nodded.


"Okay."


Kelly disappeared a few moments later.


Inside the restroom, Jane was leaning against the counter.

Trying to regain control.


The worst of it had passed.

Barely.


A few seconds later the door opened.


Kelly stepped inside.


Immediately her expression softened.


"Hey."


Jane managed a weak smile.


"Hey."


"Are you okay?"


Jane nodded.


"I think so."


Kelly looked unconvinced.


"You don't sound convinced."


Jane laughed weakly.


"I'm not."


That earned a sympathetic smile.


For a few moments neither spoke.


Then Jane sighed.


"I've just been feeling off lately."


Kelly leaned against the counter beside her.


"Yeah?"


Jane nodded.


"I've been stressing about today."


The admission felt embarrassing.


"For a long time thinking about you really triggered my anxiety."


Jane immediately winced.


"That sounded terrible."


Kelly shook her head.


"No."


A pause.


"It sounded honest."


Jane looked down.


"I've been trying to work through it."


Kelly nodded slowly.


"Thank you."


The response surprised her.


Kelly smiled sadly.


"I know I apologized back then."


A pause.


"But I still think about it."


Jane looked up.


Kelly seemed thoughtful.


"I wish things had been different."


Another pause.


"I would've liked being friends with you."


The honesty caught Jane off guard.


For a moment she didn't know what to say.


Eventually:


"Thank you."


Kelly smiled softly.


A silence settled between them.


Then Jane added quietly:


"I'm grateful to you for taking care of Andrew."


Kelly's expression softened even more.


"I know how much he means to you."


A pause.


"And how much he meant to Kirby."


The mention of Kirby hurt.

Still.


But not the way it once had.


Kelly looked down for a moment.


"I still think about him."


Jane blinked.


"What?"


Kelly nodded.


"I miss him."


The confession surprised her.


"There was a time when I thought I might even be in love with him."


Jane stared.


Kelly laughed sadly.


"Life is weird."


A pause.


"I'm sorry I didn't fight harder for him."


Neither spoke for a moment.


"Kirby chose you."


Kelly smiled.


"And I always knew that."


Another pause.


"But I really did love him."


The sincerity in her voice made Jane's chest tighten.


Kelly looked away.


"If life had gone differently..."


A small smile appeared.


"I could've imagined a future where it was you and Andrew."


Another.


"And me and Kirby."


She laughed softly.


"Instead we somehow ended up here."


Jane laughed too.


"Orbiting the same people?"


"Pretty much."


Kelly smiled.


"It was never intentional."


Jane nodded.


"I know."


A silence passed.


Then Kelly smiled.


"You and Adam look really good together."


Jane smiled immediately.


"He really is the best."


The answer came without hesitation.


Kelly noticed.


"Yeah."


A pause.


"You look happy."


Jane looked down.

Smiling.


"I am."


"Good."


Kelly squeezed her hand.


"What about us?"


Jane looked up.


Kelly smiled.


"We can try being friends."


A small laugh escaped Jane.


"Yeah."


A pause.


"I'd like that."


As they moved toward the door, Kelly stopped.


"Hey."


Jane turned.


"Yeah?"


Kelly studied her carefully.


"You said you've been feeling off lately?"


Jane nodded.


"How off?"


The question caught her by surprise.


"What?"


"Have you been nauseous?"


Jane blinked.


Then slowly nodded.


"A little."


"Usually mornings?"


Jane stared at her.


Then nodded again.


Kelly was suddenly looking at her differently.


Very differently.


And a second later Jane understood why.


"Oh."


Kelly didn't say anything at first.


Then carefully:


"Jane..."


A pause.


"Maybe you should take a test."


Silence.


Jane exhaled slowly.


"It can't be."


But even as she said it, she wasn't entirely sure.


Kelly smiled gently.


"Maybe it's stress."


A pause.


"Maybe."


Another.


"But don't worry about it tonight."


Jane nodded.


"If you feel okay, we can go back."


The offer in Kelly's voice was genuine.


"I can stay a little longer if you need."


Jane smiled.


"I'm okay."


Whether that was true or not remained to be seen.


Together they walked back toward the table.


Adam was already standing before they reached it.


Concern written all over his face.


"Jane?"


His eyes immediately searched hers.


"I'm okay."


The answer came automatically.


Adam didn't look convinced.


Neither was she.


But for now, it was enough.


And as they sat back down, Jane found herself glancing briefly at Kelly.


Kelly offered a small reassuring smile.


For the first time in years, the sight of it didn't hurt quite as much.
