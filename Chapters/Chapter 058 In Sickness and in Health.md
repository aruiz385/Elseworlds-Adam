# Chapter 58: In Sickness and in Health

Three hours later, Kirby woke up again.


This time there was no panic.


No alarms.


No confusion.


Just silence.


Soft morning light filtered through the window.


The ICU was beginning to wake up around them.


But inside the room everything felt still.


Kirby's eyes opened slowly.


His body hurt.


Everything hurt.


His throat.


His chest.


His muscles.


Even breathing felt like work.


But none of that was what he noticed first.


Jane.


She was asleep.


Curled awkwardly in the recliner beside his bed.


Still holding his hand.


Even in her sleep.


A small smile appeared on Kirby's face.


Weak.


Exhausted.


But real.


For several minutes he simply watched her.


Trying to understand.


Trying to remember.


Trying to make sense of everything.


But whenever his thoughts began to race, his eyes drifted back to her.


And somehow that helped.


Eventually Jane stirred.


For four months her body had been trained to wake at the smallest change.


A baby crying.


A monitor shifting.


A hand moving against hers.


Her eyes fluttered open.


And immediately found him watching her.


For a second she froze.


Then smiled.


Then unexpectedly wanted to cry.


Because the look on his face reminded her of someone else.


Adam.


The memory hit immediately.


Dr. Ryan's assignment.


The journal.


The list of things Adam did that bothered her.


One item in particular.


He stares at me.


When she fed Lizzy.


When she slept.


When she got lost in thought.


Adam stared at her constantly.


And when Dr. Ryan asked why that bothered her so much, the answer had been painfully obvious.


Because he loved her.


Now Kirby was looking at her that exact same way.


And instead of making her happy, it made her heart ache.


Because she could only imagine what was going through his mind.


And because suddenly...


She missed Adam.


Terribly.


It had only been one night.


But she missed him.


She missed Lizzy.


She missed the warmth of their bed.


The tiny sounds Lizzy made while sleeping.


The life waiting outside these hospital walls.


For the first time in four months she had spent a night away from her daughter.


And she felt it.


Every second.


But Jane pushed those thoughts away.


Because right now Kirby needed her.


And regardless of everything that had happened...


He deserved that.


This was the man who had given her six years of happiness.


The man she'd married.


The man she'd once promised forever.


The vows echoed in her head.


For better or worse.


In sickness and in health.


Till death do us part.


And death had.


That was the crazy part.


Death had parted them.


For seven years.


Seven years where she'd had to pick up the pieces.


Seven years of grief.


Of rebuilding.


Of healing.


Seven painful, beautiful, terrifying, wonderful years.


Jane stood too quickly.


Immediately regretting it.


Pain shot through her abdomen.


Her C-section scar reminding her it existed.


She winced.


Kirby's expression changed immediately.


Concern.


Jane forced a smile.


"I'm fine."


Another.


"Just stood up too fast."


She moved to the side of his bed.


"Hey."


Another.


"How are you feeling?"


Kirby considered the question.


Then gave a small shrug.


Which honestly answered everything.


Jane laughed softly.


"Yeah."


Another.


"That's about what I expected."


His eyes stayed on her.


Always on her.


Jane looked toward the door.


"Are you ready to see your dad and Andrew?"


His expression immediately tightened.


Not fear.


Just uncertainty.


Overwhelm.


Jane noticed.


"Okay."


Another.


"I can give you a few more minutes."


He visibly relaxed.


Then nodded.


Grateful.


"Do you want some water?"


Another nod.


Jane grabbed the cup.


Then sat beside him.


Carefully helping him drink.


The simple act seemed exhausting.


He tried to smile afterward.


But she could see the discomfort.


"Are you sore?"


He nodded.


"In pain?"


Another nod.


Jane squeezed his hand.


"I know."


Another.


"Lying in bed this long can't be fun."


Another.


"Scott is going to get physical therapy involved."


Another.


"We're going to get you moving."


Another.


"We're getting you out of this bed."


His expression immediately brightened.


The determination was obvious.


Jane laughed.


"See?"


Another.


"That look tells me you're already planning it."


A real smile appeared.


Tiny.


But real.


Then his thumb brushed across her hand.


The familiar gesture surprised her.


For a second she froze.


Then smiled back.


Kirby studied her face.


Really studied it.


His brow furrowed.


Questioning.


Concerned.


Jane knew exactly what he was seeing.


The exhaustion.


The dark circles.


The stress.


"Just tired."


Another.


"I've been here for a while."


Another.


"Nothing for you to worry about."


She squeezed his hand.


"Okay?"


Another.


"I need you focused on getting better."


Another.


"The faster you recover, the better I'll be."


Not a lie.


Not even close.


Kirby didn't seem entirely convinced.


But he accepted it.


Mostly.


Then she saw it.


The impatience.


The curiosity.


The need to know something.


Anything.


Jane smiled.


"Okay."


Another.


"Scott told me not to overwhelm you."


Another.


"But I can fill you in on some things."


Kirby's attention immediately sharpened.


Jane laughed.


"There he is."


Another.


"Okay."


Another.


"Karen and James got married seven months ago."


Kirby's eyes widened.


Jane pointed.


"Exactly."


Another.


"Took them long enough."


The corner of his mouth twitched.


"Andrew is a full-fledged doctor now."


Another.


"But still works part-time at the precinct."


Another.


"Your overachieving brother has apparently decided to become Superman."


That earned an eye roll.


A genuine eye roll.


Jane laughed.


"There he is."


Another.


"I knew that would get a reaction."


Kirby looked lighter.


Less afraid.


More himself.


Then he tugged gently on her hand.


Jane froze.


Because she knew what he was asking.


What about you?


Her throat tightened.


"Me?"


Another.


"Well..."


Another.


"Lots of therapy."


That earned a puzzled look.


She laughed.


"Trust me."


Another.


"Necessary."


Another.


"I'm still working with Lara."


Another.


"You remember Lara."


Another.


"She owns her own company now."


Another.


"So somehow she's even scarier than she used to be."


That got another smile.


"Still live in the same place."


Another.


"Andrew still lives upstairs."


Another.


"My life isn't exactly anything out of this world."


The statement almost made her laugh.


Because it was the biggest understatement she'd ever spoken.


"And..."


Another.


"Your mom still hates me."


A genuine smile appeared.


There it was.


The smile she'd been hoping for.


Jane pointed at him.


"I knew that would work."


Another.


"Oh."


Another.


"And Karen and James are having a baby."


Kirby's eyebrows shot up.


"Exactly."


Another.


"That's why you haven't heard her yelling down the hallway."


Another.


"You know she has absolutely no respect for hospital rules."


Another.


"Otherwise she'd be here already."


Kirby laughed.


Not a real laugh.


Too painful.


But close.


And it was enough.


"Okay."


Another.


"That's enough information."


Another.


"Time to let our family in here."


He gave her a grateful look.


The kind that made her heart ache.


Jane leaned down.


And kissed his forehead.


The familiar gesture immediately calmed him.


His hand squeezed hers tightly.


Then she stood.


And headed for the door.


She stepped into the hallway.


And found Kelly first.


"How is he?"


Jane let out a long breath.


"Awake."


Another.


"Confused."


Another.


"But okay."


Kelly smiled.


Then looked closer.


"How are you?"


That was all it took.


Jane broke.


"I miss Adam."


Her voice cracked immediately.


Another.


"And Lizzy."


Another.


"Oh God, Kelly."


Another.


"I miss my baby."


The tears returned.


Kelly immediately wrapped an arm around her.


And then suddenly stopped.


Her attention shifting down the hallway.


Jane turned.


The elevator doors opened.


And there he was.


Adam.


Coffee trays in both hands.


Jane's overnight bag hanging from one shoulder.


The sight of him hit her so hard she forgot how to breathe.


Before Adam could even react...


She was already running toward him.
