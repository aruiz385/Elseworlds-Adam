# Chapter 74: One More Thing


Jane was halfway to Dr. Ryan's office when she heard a familiar voice behind her.


"Well."


Another.


"That's not an encouraging face."


Jane stopped walking.


Closed her eyes.


Then turned around.


Kirby was sitting outside Physical Therapy in a wheelchair waiting for one of the rehab aides.


Looking entirely too pleased with himself.


Jane rolled her eyes.


"You know, most people start with hello."


"Most people don't look like they're heading toward a firing squad."


Despite herself...


A laugh escaped.


Small.


Tired.


But real.


Kirby's smile softened immediately.


"Hey."


His voice gentler now.


"What's wrong?"


Jane looked away.


That was answer enough.


Kirby sighed.


"Oh."


Another.


"It's me."


"Partially."


Kirby groaned.


Immediately.


"That means the other part is Andrew."


Jane pointed at him.


"See?"


Another.


"Still a lawyer."


"Still annoying."


Kirby smiled.


"Fair."


Then the smile faded.


"What happened?"


Jane folded her arms.


"You hurt my feelings."


The honesty surprised both of them.


Kirby's expression fell instantly.


"Jane."


Another.


"No."


Another.


"I really didn't."


She looked at him.


"You said you wished you'd stayed with Kelly."


Another.


"You said you wished you'd let Andrew come after me."


Another.


"You said you should've picked her."


For a moment Kirby just stared.


Then his eyes widened.


And suddenly he understood.


Completely.


"Oh."


Another.


"Oh no."


Jane looked away.


Kirby shook his head immediately.


"No."


Another.


"No, Jane."


Another.


"That is absolutely not what I meant."


"It felt like it."


The words came out smaller than she intended.


More vulnerable.


Kirby nodded.


Because feelings didn't care about intent.


He knew that.


"I wasn't talking about us."


Another.


"I was talking about consequences."


Another.


"I was talking about fourteen years of everybody paying for one moment."


His voice softened.


"Jane."


Another.


"If I could take back anything..."


Another.


"It wouldn't be our six years."


Another.


"It wouldn't be our marriage."


Another.


"It wouldn't be us."


His eyes met hers.


"It would've been the accident."


Silence.


Jane felt tears rise immediately.


"I wish I could take it back too."


Kirby's expression softened.


"I know."


Jane swallowed hard.


"I wish you hadn't lost eight years."


Another.


"I wish Andrew hadn't lost his brother."


Another.


"I wish I hadn't lost you."


Her voice cracked.


"I missed you terribly, Kirby."


The words hit him harder than he expected.


Jane laughed sadly through tears.


"For eight years there was this huge hole in my life."


Another.


"This void."


Another.


"The person I used to call when something happened was just...gone."


Kirby looked down.


Unable to speak.


"The person who knew everything."


Another.


"The person I talked to before making huge decisions."


Another.


"The person who always picked up."


Another.


"The person who always understood."


She smiled sadly.


"There wasn't an important milestone in my life that I didn't tell you about."


Because it was true.


For thirty years.


Phone calls.


Emails.


Texts.


Late-night conversations.


No matter where life took them.


No matter that he lived in New York and she lived in California.


Kirby had always been there.


"I missed my best friend."


Kirby laughed softly.


His eyes wet.


"That was really rude of me."


Jane snorted.


"What?"


"Leaving."


That earned a genuine laugh from both of them.


The kind that only existed between people who had known each other almost their entire lives.


Then Kirby reached over and squeezed her hand.


"And for the record..."


Jane looked up.


"I don't want you disappearing either."


She blinked.


"What?"


Kirby smiled.


"Jane, I'm signing divorce papers."


Another.


"I'm not filing a restraining order."


The laugh burst out before she could stop it.


Kirby looked pleased with himself.


"There it is."


Jane shook her head.


Hopeless.


Absolutely hopeless.


"My point is..."


His smile softened.


"I didn't ask you to disappear."


Another.


"I didn't ask you to stop talking to me."


Another.


"I didn't ask you to stop being my friend."


Jane stared at him.


The emotion catching her completely off guard.


Kirby shrugged.


"You've been one of the most important people in my life for thirty years."


Another.


"Before we dated."


Another.


"Before we got married."


Another.


"Before any of that."


His voice softened.


"You were the person I called when something mattered."


Another.


"The person who knew what was happening in my life before anybody else."


Another.


"And somehow I always knew what was happening in yours."


Jane squeezed his hand.


Unable to speak.


"I'd really hate to lose that."


Then unexpectedly Kirby became serious.


"And Jane?"


She looked up.


"What?"


The humor disappeared completely.


"Thank you."


Jane blinked.


"For what?"


Kirby shook his head.


"No."


Another.


"I mean it."


Another.


"Thank you."


The emotion behind the words caught her off guard.


"I don't think I've said it enough."


His grip tightened slightly around her hand.


"I know what you gave up to be here."


Jane immediately looked away.


Because that was the one thing nobody had really talked about.


Kirby continued.


"I'm not stupid."


Another.


"I know you have a daughter."


Another.


"I know you have Adam."


Another.


"I know you have a life waiting for you outside this hospital."


His voice softened.


"And I know what an enormous sacrifice it must have been to leave all of that behind to sit in a hospital room with me for five weeks."


Jane's eyes immediately filled again.


"Kirby..."


"No."


He shook his head gently.


"Let me finish."


She nodded.


Quietly.


Kirby took a breath.


"I woke up after losing eight years of my life."


Another.


"I woke up scared."


Another.


"I woke up confused."


Another.


"I woke up in a world I didn't recognize."


His voice cracked.


"And somehow the first face I saw was my wife who also happened to be my best friend refusing to let me fall apart."


Jane looked down immediately.


Tears falling freely now.


"You were there."


Another.


"Every day."


Another.


"When I was angry."


Another.


"When I was confused."


Another.


"When I was scared."


He smiled.


Small.


Grateful.


"And I will never be able to thank you enough for that."


Jane laughed through tears.


"I'd do it all again."


Kirby blinked.


"What?"


"I mean it."


She shook her head.


"Every day."


Another.


"Every bad cup of coffee."


Another.


"Every night."


Another.


"Every awful chair."


Another.


"All of it."


Her eyes never left his.


"I don't regret any of it."


Another.


"Not one second."


Kirby's expression softened completely.


"You don't get to thank me like that and then have me pretend it was some burden."


Another.


"It wasn't."


Another.


"It was hard."


Another.


"It was exhausting."


Another.


"It broke my heart more than once."


She squeezed his hand.


"But it wasn't a burden."


Neither spoke for a moment.


Then Jane smiled sadly.


"And thank you."


Kirby frowned.


"For what?"


"The papers."


His expression softened immediately.


Jane shook her head.


"They hurt."


Another.


"A lot."


Another.


"But after I stopped crying..."


Another.


"I realized what you were trying to do."


Her voice trembled.


"You were trying to take care of me."


Kirby's eyes filled.


Because yes.


That was exactly what he'd been trying to do.


"I know it couldn't have been easy."


Jane smiled through tears.


"But thank you anyway."


Another.


"Because I know you did it out of love."


For a moment neither spoke.


Finally Kirby smiled.


"I do love you."


Not romantic.


Not possessive.


Simply true.


The love of thirty years.


Of friendship.


Of history.


Of shared lives.


"I know."


Jane whispered.


Then Kirby pointed down the hallway.


"Anyway, I expect you to keep showing up."


Jane laughed.


"What?"


"You come here for therapy."


Another.


"I'm trapped here for at least a few more weeks."


Another.


"I fully expect you to stop by and say hi."


"A guilt trip."


Kirby grinned.


"I was a lawyer."


Another.


"It's a professional skill."


Jane rolled her eyes.


Smiling now.


"And after that?"


Kirby shrugged.


"I'll probably be staying with Andrew until I get settled."


Another.


"So I'm pretty sure I'll still be seeing a lot of you."


"I'd like that."


Kirby's smile widened.


"Good."


Then his expression shifted.


Not serious.


Not sad.


Knowing.


"And cut Andrew some slack."


Jane groaned immediately.


"Oh my God."


Kirby laughed.


"No seriously."


Another.


"Cut him some slack."


Another.


"But don't let him get away with it."


Jane blinked.


"What?"


Kirby pointed at her.


"You know exactly what I mean."


Another.


"Andrew has a terrible habit."


Another.


"Always has."


Another.


"He decides what's best for everybody."


Another.


"Then goes off and does something incredibly stupid."


Jane laughed.


Immediately.


"That is disturbingly accurate."


"I know."


Kirby looked entirely too pleased with himself.


"I've known him for thirty years."


Another.


"It's one of his defining characteristics."


The rehab aide snorted.


Kirby ignored him.


Jane folded her arms.


"So your professional opinion is what exactly?"


"My professional opinion?"


Kirby smiled.


"My professional opinion is that his boundaries are ridiculous."


That earned another genuine laugh.


Kirby pointed triumphantly.


"See?"


Another.


"I knew you'd agree."


Jane shook her head.


"You are unbelievable."


"No."


Kirby corrected.


"He's unbelievable."


Another.


"You should know Andrew by now."


Another.


"The man has never met an emotional problem he couldn't make worse by trying to handle it alone."


Jane laughed again.


Because that was also painfully true.


Kirby's smile faded slightly.


Not sad.


Just honest.


"I think he's hurting."


Another.


"I think he's confused."


Another.


"And I think he has absolutely no idea what he's doing."


Jane nodded.


That sounded exactly right.


"But I also know you."


Kirby said.


That got her attention.


"What does that mean?"


Kirby smiled.


"It means I have complete faith that you won't let him get away with this."


Jane stared at him.


Then laughed.


"Kirby."


"I'm serious."


Another.


"You are stronger than you used to be."


Another.


"You don't let people make decisions for you anymore."


Another.


"And there is absolutely no universe where Jane McFly spends another year not talking to Andrew Call."


The certainty in his voice made her smile.


Because honestly?


He was right.


Kirby continued.


"I fully support you taking down every single one of those ridiculous boundaries."


The rehab aide laughed.


Jane buried her face in her hands.


"Oh my God."


Kirby nodded.


"Whatever it takes."


Another.


"Phone calls."


Another.


"Showing up."


Another.


"Weaponizing concern."


Another.


"Use every tool available."


Jane looked at him in complete disbelief.


"You are actively encouraging me."


"I am."


Another.


"Because somebody has to make sure he's okay."


Another.


"And if Andrew is left to his own devices, he'll spend three days hiding in Hill Dale pretending he's fine."


That earned another laugh.


Kirby smiled.


Then his expression softened.


"Seriously, Jane."


Another.


"Don't give up on him."


Another.


"He's going to make it difficult."


Another.


"Probably very difficult."


Another.


"But don't give up on him."


Jane felt a lump form in her throat.


Because beneath all the teasing...


All the jokes...


All the lawyer sarcasm...


Kirby was worried about his brother.


And he trusted her.


The way he always had.


"I wasn't planning to."


Kirby's smile widened.


"Good."


Another.


"Because I already bet on you."


Jane rolled her eyes.


"Of course you did."


"Smart money."


That finally broke her.


She laughed so hard she had to wipe tears from her eyes.


And in that moment she remembered exactly why the two of them had made such a great team.


Because whenever life became impossible...


Somehow Kirby always found a way to make her laugh first.


Then solve the problem afterward.


The rehab aide finally stepped forward.


"Ready?"


Kirby sighed dramatically.


"No."


That earned another laugh from both Jane and the aide.


Then before she could overthink it, Jane stepped forward and wrapped her arms around him.


Tight.


Much tighter than she'd intended.


Kirby hugged her back immediately.


The way he always had.


The way he always would.


Two old friends.


Thirty years of history.


A marriage.


A loss.


A miracle.


A future neither fully understood.


"Thank you."


Jane whispered.


Kirby smiled against her shoulder.


"No."


Another.


"Thank you."


When they finally pulled apart, Jane wiped her eyes.


Smiled.


And pointed at him.


"You'd better still be here when I get out of therapy."


Kirby groaned.


"That's a terrible threat."


"Good."


She smiled.


"See you in an hour."


Then she turned and headed toward Dr. Ryan's office.


The divorce papers were still in her purse.


Andrew was still being impossible.


Nothing was fixed.


Not really.


But for the first time since Kirby handed her those papers...


Jane didn't feel like she was losing him.


And somehow...


That made the walk to therapy a little easier.
