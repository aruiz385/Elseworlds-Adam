# Chapter 62: Lost Time


By week 2, Scott decided it was time.


Not for the whole truth.


Not yet.


But for the next step.


Which was how Adam found himself standing outside Kirby's room.


Trying to figure out how to introduce himself.


Jonathan had stepped out for lunch.


Andrew was catching up on charts.


Jane was finally taking a break.


Which left Scott with an opportunity.


And apparently a plan.


Adam stepped into the room.


Kirby looked up immediately.


Curious.


Still tired.


Still recovering.


But stronger than he'd been a week ago.


"Hey."


Adam offered a small smile.


"I'm Dr. Adam Carter."


Another.


"I work here."


Another.


"With Scott and Andrew."


Another.


"And I've known both of them for years."


Kirby nodded.


Listening.


Adam pulled up a chair.


Then sat down.


For a moment neither spoke.


Then Adam decided honesty was probably the best place to start.


"Scott asked me to talk to you."


Kirby looked mildly suspicious.


Reasonable.


Adam laughed.


"I made the same face."


That earned the smallest smile.


Good.


Progress.


Adam folded his hands.


"Scott thinks I might understand a piece of what you're dealing with."


Kirby's attention sharpened.


Adam nodded.


"I'm not going to pretend I know exactly what you're going through."


Another.


"I don't."


Another.


"But I do understand waking up and realizing that life has moved on without you."


Another.


"That you lost time."


Kirby's expression changed immediately.


The words landed.


Adam saw it.


Of course he did.


So he continued.


"I have Dissociative Identity Disorder."


Another.


"DID."


Another.


"I've lost years of my life."


Another.


"More than once."


Kirby's eyebrows lifted.


Clearly surprised.


Adam nodded.


"The last major episode cost me almost three years."


Another.


"But that wasn't the first time."


Another.


"There were periods during pre-med."


Another.


"Periods during medical school."


Another.


"Years where I wasn't fully present in my own life."


Kirby stared at him.


Trying to process that.


Finally he spoke.


His voice rough.


Still weak.


"But..."


He paused.


Trying again.


"How?"


Adam understood immediately.


How do you live with that?


How do you survive it?


How do you keep going?


Adam smiled sadly.


"Honestly?"


Another.


"Not very well at first."


That earned the smallest smile.


Adam shrugged.


"A lot of therapy."


Another.


"A lot of mistakes."


Another.


"A lot of very patient people."


Kirby lowered his eyes briefly.


Thinking.


Then:


"Angry?"


Adam nodded immediately.


"Furious."


Another.


"For years."


Another.


"At myself."


Another.


"At the world."


Another.


"At the fact everybody else's life kept moving while I was trying to figure out where mine went."


Something shifted in Kirby's expression.


Recognition.


Not of circumstances.


Of feelings.


Adam saw it.


And continued.


"That's normal."


Another.


"If you're angry."


Another.


"If you're scared."


Another.


"If you're frustrated."


Another.


"You're having a pretty reasonable reaction."


For a moment neither spoke.


Then Kirby asked another question.


"How..."


A breath.


"Manage?"


Adam smiled.


"The simple answer?"


Another.


"You don't do it all at once."


Another.


"One day."


Another.


"One conversation."


Another.


"One piece of information at a time."


Kirby nodded slowly.


Adam leaned back.


"You don't need all the answers today."


Another.


"You don't need to solve your entire life today."


Another.


"You just keep moving."


The room grew quiet.


Then Adam added:


"And therapy helped."


Kirby immediately looked skeptical.


Adam laughed.


"Oh, that's the exact look I had."


Another.


"I fought therapy hard."


Another.


"Lost that battle."


Kirby's smile widened.


Slightly.


Adam pointed at him.


"My therapist used to look at me exactly like that."


For the first time Kirby actually laughed.


Brief.


Painful.


But real.


Adam smiled.


There it was.


The first genuine laugh.


After a moment Kirby became serious again.


"Did..."


Another breath.


"Life..."


Another.


"Work out?"


The vulnerability in the question caught Adam off guard.


Because it wasn't really about Adam.


It was about Kirby.


Will I be okay?


Adam thought of Jane.


Of Lizzy.


Of the life he had somehow managed to build.


A small smile appeared.


"Yeah."


Another.


"It did."


Another.


"Not the way I expected."


Another.


"Not even close."


Another.


"But it worked out."


Kirby seemed to relax.


Just a little.


As if he needed to hear that.


From someone who understood.


And it was at that moment that his attention drifted toward the window.


His expression softened immediately.


"A baby?"


The words came out rough.


Still strained.


But understandable.


Adam froze.


Because he followed Kirby's gaze.


And immediately saw the problem.


Melissa.


Marty.


And Lizzy.


Walking down the hallway.


Visible through the ICU glass.


Oh no.


Kirby looked back toward him.


Curious.


"Whose?"


Adam's brain went into overdrive.


Before he could answer, the door opened.


Nurse Hayle stepped inside.


Thank God for Nurse Hayle.


One look.


That was all it took.


She immediately understood.


"Thank you so much, Mrs. McFly, for holding her for a second."


Hayle smiled warmly.


Then looked toward Adam.


"Dr. Carter, your daughter is here to see you."


Adam nearly laughed from relief.


Kirby looked surprised.


"Your daughter?"


Adam nodded.


"Yeah."


Another.


"She's mine."


Which was true.


Entirely true.


Kirby smiled immediately.


"She's beautiful."


Adam's expression softened.


"Yeah."


Another.


"She really is."


Kirby looked at Lizzy again.


Then back at Adam.


"Can I hold her?"


Adam froze.


Completely.


Before he could answer, the door opened.


Jane stepped inside.


Still talking with Andrew.


Then she saw Lizzy.


Saw Kirby.


Saw the entire situation.


And nearly stopped breathing.


Adam met her eyes.


Very subtly shook his head.


Don't.


Not here.


Adam stood.


Putting on his best physician smile.


"Hey, Jane."


Another.


"Introducing your husband to my daughter who is here visiting."


His eyes held hers.


Hoping she understood.


Jane did.


Immediately.


Which somehow made it worse.


Because now she had to pretend too.


Kirby never noticed.


Far too focused on Lizzy.


Adam carefully placed the baby in Kirby's arms.


And for a moment everything stopped.


Kirby smiled.


A real smile.


The biggest smile anyone had seen from him all week.


Jane felt something inside her break.


Completely.


Adam saw it immediately.


Before she could hide it.


Before she could force herself through another minute.


"Nurse Hayle?"


Another.


"Can you keep an eye on Lizzy for a few minutes?"


Hayle nodded.


"Of course."


Adam turned to Jane.


"Jane."


Another.


"Can you come with me for a minute?"


Another.


"Scott wanted me to show you some paperwork."


The lie was terrible.


Absolutely terrible.


But Kirby was too distracted to care.


Jane nodded immediately.


Because if she stayed another second she was going to shatter.


Adam guided her out.


Down the hallway.


Away from the room.


Away from the window.


The moment they rounded the corner Jane broke.


Completely.


"I can't do this anymore."


The words came out between sobs.


"I can't."


Adam wrapped his arms around her instantly.


Holding her tightly.


As though he could somehow carry the weight for both of them.


A moment later Andrew appeared.


Concern already written across his face.


"What happened?"


Adam silently pointed toward Kirby's room.


Andrew looked through the distant window.


And immediately froze.


Kirby.


Holding Lizzy.


His goddaughter.


The little girl who looked more and more like Jane every day.


"Oh God."


The words escaped before he could stop them.


Andrew looked at Adam.


"What happened?"


"Melissa and Marty were bringing Lizzy over."


Another.


"He saw them through the window."


Another.


"Hayle saved us."


Another.


"I told him she's my daughter."


Andrew nodded slowly.


"Which is true."


"Exactly."


Andrew looked at Jane.


Still crying.


Still trying to breathe.


And immediately understood.


Because if there was one thing Andrew Call could never stand...


It was Jane crying.


Never had been.


Not when they were kids.


Not now.


His expression hardened.


"We're done."


Another.


"I'm not asking you to sacrifice any more than you already have."


Another.


"This isn't fair to you."


Another.


"It isn't fair to Adam."


Another.


"And it isn't fair to Kirby."


His voice tightened.


"We have to tell him."


Another.


"We have to stop this."


Then he turned.


Determined.


"I'm getting Lizzy."


A few minutes later Andrew walked back into the room.


Smiling.


"There's my goddaughter."


Another.


"Do you mind?"


Kirby carefully handed Lizzy over.


Still smiling.


"You are Adam's daughter's godfather?"


Andrew nodded.


"Yeah."


Another.


"We've been friends a long time."


Another.


"Adam doesn't have much family."


Another.


"So when he asked, I was honored."


Kirby smiled.


Then looked at Lizzy one last time.


"She's beautiful."


Another.


"It's weird."


Another.


"She reminds me of an old baby picture I once saw of Jane."


Andrew's heart stopped.


For one horrible second.


Then he forced a smile.


"You know babies."


Another.


"They all kind of look alike."


The lie felt awful.


But it worked.


For now.


Andrew carried Lizzy back into the hallway.


The second he reached Jane, he handed Lizzy back gently.


Jane immediately pulled her daughter close.


Holding her tightly.


As if reassuring herself that Lizzy was still here.


Still hers.


Still real.


Andrew looked at both of them.


Then wrapped his arms around Jane and Lizzy together.


Holding them both.


Protectively.


The way he always had.


The way he always would.


Jane buried her face against his shoulder and cried.


Andrew kissed the top of her head.


"I've got you."


Another.


"Always."


He tightened his hold.


"I'm serious."


Another.


"I've got your back."


Another.


"And I'm going to make this okay again."


His voice broke slightly.


But he meant every word.


"I promise."


Jane looked up at him through her tears.


And for the first time since this nightmare started...


She believed him.


Because Andrew Call had been keeping his promises to her since they were kids.


And he wasn't about to stop now.
