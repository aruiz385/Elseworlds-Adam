# Chapter 54: Morning


Jane woke slowly.


For several seconds she didn't know why she felt different.


Then she felt Adam's arm around her waist.


The warmth of him.


The steady rhythm of his breathing.


And remembered.


A small smile appeared immediately.


The previous night felt almost unreal.


The park.


The ring.


The promises.


Coming home.


Choosing each other.


For a few precious hours they'd stopped thinking about hospitals and impossible decisions.


Just long enough to remember who they were.


Jane carefully turned toward him.


Adam was still asleep.


The morning light spilling across his face.


For once the tension was gone.


No furrowed brow.


No restless sleep.


No nightmares.


Just Adam.


Peaceful.


She looked down at her hand.


The ring was still there.


The heart-shaped ring.


Back where it belonged.


A ridiculous ring.


A stubborn ring.


A ring that somehow always found its way back to her.


Jane smiled.


Then gently traced her thumb across it.


Almost immediately Adam stirred.


One eye opening.


Then the other.


The very first thing he did was look at her.


The second thing was look at her hand.


His eyes landed on the ring.


A smile spread across his face.


A real one.


The kind she loved.


"Good morning."


Jane laughed softly.


"Good morning."


Adam reached for her hand.


Lifting it between them.


Studying the ring for a moment.


Then kissing her knuckles.


Slowly.


Tenderly.


As though the ring itself meant something sacred.


Because to him it did.


"You kept it."


Jane rolled her eyes.


"Oh my God."


"What?"


"You sound surprised."


"I am."


Another.


"Statistically speaking, you have a history."


Jane groaned.


Adam laughed.


Then pulled her closer.


Resting his forehead against hers.


For several moments neither spoke.


Neither seemed to need to.


Eventually Jane whispered:


"I love you."


Adam closed his eyes.


The words still affected him.


Every single time.


"I love you too."


Another.


"Always."


His hand moved gently to her cheek.


"I slept."


Jane blinked.


"What?"


"I actually slept."


Another.


"Do you know how long it's been since I slept without thinking about any of this?"


Jane smiled sadly.


"Probably the same amount of time as me."


Adam nodded.


Then kissed her again.


Softly.


"No matter what happens today..."


He paused.


Then corrected himself.


"When things get hard today..."


Another.


"Remember last night."


Jane looked at him.


"What do you mean?"


"The part where I told you I'm not going anywhere."


His eyes held hers.


"Because I meant it."


The seriousness in his voice made her chest tighten.


"Adam..."


"No."


He shook his head gently.


"I know you're scared."


Another.


"I know today is going to be awful."


Another.


"I know you're carrying things nobody should have to carry."


His hand squeezed hers.


"But you're not carrying them alone."


Jane felt tears gathering immediately.


Adam smiled softly.


"Besides."


A faint smile appeared.


"You're stuck with me now."


Jane laughed.


Despite herself.


Then kissed him.


Because there really wasn't another response for that.


A few minutes later the sound of Lizzy announcing she was awake echoed through the baby monitor.


Both of them smiled immediately.


And just like that...


Their daughter reclaimed first place.


Breakfast smelled incredible.


Melissa was responsible.


Which meant there was far too much food.


Jane walked into the kitchen carrying Lizzy.


Adam immediately behind her.


Melissa looked up.


Then froze.


Just for a moment.


Marty noticed too.


Not because of the ring.


Not at first.


Because Jane smiled.


Really smiled.


For the first time in days.


She looked tired.


But not shattered.


And Adam looked different too.


Lighter.


Happier.


Like somebody had given him permission to hope again.


Then Melissa noticed the ring.


Her eyes immediately widened.


Only slightly.


Marty followed her gaze.


The ring.


Back on Jane's finger.


Melissa looked at Marty.


Marty looked at Melissa.


Neither said a word.


Neither needed to.


Because amidst all the chaos...


Amidst all the uncertainty...


Their daughter looked happy this morning.


And somehow that felt important.


Very important.


"Morning."


Melissa smiled.


"Good morning."


Jane sat down.


Adam took the chair beside her.


Instinctively close.


Melissa noticed that too.


The way they looked at each other.


The way they reached for each other.


The way they seemed more settled.


More certain.


She didn't say a word.


But her heart felt lighter.


A while later the doorbell rang.


Everyone knew who it was before Adam even stood up.


Reality had arrived.


Andrew.


Kelly.


Jonathan.


Adam opened the door.


Jonathan entered first.


Looking exhausted.


The last few days written across every feature.


Andrew wasn't much better.


Jane's heart sank the moment she saw him.


He looked drained.


Like he hadn't slept.


Like he hadn't eaten.


Like he was carrying the weight of the world alone.


Kelly looked tired too.


But when she spotted Jane her expression immediately brightened.


Then she noticed the ring.


A huge smile appeared.


The first genuinely happy expression Jane had seen from her in days.


Kelly waited until everyone settled into breakfast.


Then pulled Jane into the kitchen.


The second they were alone Kelly grabbed her hand.


"Oh my God."


Jane laughed.


"What?"


Kelly pointed directly at the ring.


"That."


Jane looked down.


The smile arrived automatically.


Kelly's eyes immediately softened.


"He put it back on."


Jane nodded.


Kelly smiled wider.


"And?"


Jane laughed.


"And I kept it on."


Kelly looked genuinely relieved.


"Good."


The answer surprised Jane.


Kelly squeezed her hand.


"Really good."


Jane looked down.


"It felt right."


Kelly nodded.


"It looks right."


The smile lingered.


Then Jane's expression softened.


"How's Andrew?"


Kelly's smile faded immediately.


Not dramatically.


Just enough.


And suddenly Jane knew.


"Not good?"


Kelly shook her head.


"Not good."


Jane looked toward the dining table.


Andrew was staring into his coffee cup.


Silent.


Somewhere far away.


Kelly followed her gaze.


"He isn't talking."


Another.


"He isn't sleeping."


Another.


"And he's trying very hard to make sure everybody else is okay."


Jane nodded slowly.


That sounded exactly like Andrew.


Which was the problem.


Kelly sighed.


"He's hanging on."


Another.


"But barely."


The words stayed with Jane.


Long after the conversation ended.


Because suddenly she realized something.


She wasn't the only person breaking under the weight of this.


Not even close.


A little while later everyone found themselves gathering near the front door.


Jonathan.


Andrew.


Kelly.


Adam.


Jane.


The hospital waiting.


The day waiting.


Kirby waiting.


Melissa lifted Lizzy into her arms.


Holding her close.


"We've got her."


Jane nodded.


Trying not to cry again.


Marty rested a hand on her shoulder.


"Go."


Another.


"We'll take care of everything here."


Jane kissed Lizzy's forehead.


Adam did the same.


Then together they headed toward the elevator.


Toward the hospital.


Toward Kirby.


And toward a day that was going to change all of their lives forever.
