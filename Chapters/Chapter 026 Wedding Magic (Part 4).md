# Chapter 26: Wedding Magic (Part 4)

Jane knew she was in trouble before she even reached the lodge.


Not because of the ring.


Because of Adam.


Adam was smiling.


That particular smile.


The one that usually meant he had already made a decision and there was absolutely nothing she could do about it.


"You look entirely too pleased with yourself."


Adam's smile widened.


"I have no idea what you're talking about."


"Liar."


"Probably."


Jane rolled her eyes.


Then instinctively turned the ring inward against her palm.


Adam immediately noticed.


"Don't."


"Don't what?"


"You know what."


Jane ignored him.


Mostly because she was already trying to figure out how to hide the very obvious heart-shaped diamond sitting on her finger.


Unfortunately, she had forgotten one key detail.


Her friends.


Her very observant friends.


The lodge fireplace was still burning when they walked inside.


Jane blinked.


"What are you all still doing up?"


Around the fire sat Karen, James, Kelly, Andrew, Chris, and Maria.


Apparently nobody had received the memo about sleep.


"Wedding weekend."

Karen replied.


"As if we're sleeping."


"Fair."


Andrew immediately stood.


Without a word, he picked up a mug from the table beside him and handed it to Jane.


Jane blinked.


"Hot chocolate?"


Andrew winked.


"Obviously."


Jane smiled.


Because of course he had somehow found her hot chocolate.


Because Andrew had always remembered things like that.


The little things.


The comfort things.


The Jane things.


"Thank you."


"You're welcome."


Jane took the mug.


Andrew looked down.


Then froze.


His eyes landed directly on her hand.


The ring.


For a second he said nothing.


Then he glanced toward Adam.


Adam looked back.


And that was all it took.


Andrew started laughing.


Not loudly.


Just enough.


Because he'd had the conversation with Adam earlier.


The one by the lake.


The one where Adam confessed he wanted to propose.


The one where Andrew had told him to stop overthinking it and do what he always did.


Apparently Adam had listened.


Of course he had.


Andrew shook his head.

Smiling into his beer.


Jane immediately narrowed her eyes.


"What?"


"Nothing."


"You saw something."


"I saw nothing."


"You absolutely saw something."


Andrew continued smiling.


Which was somehow worse.


Jane immediately tried to hide her hand.


Unfortunately, it was already too late.


"Oh my God."


Karen.


Every head snapped toward her.


Karen was staring directly at Jane's hand.


The room went silent.


For approximately half a second.


Then:


"OH MY GOD!"


The entire room exploded.


Kelly nearly spilled her drink.


Maria screamed.


Chris jumped to his feet.


James looked confused for three seconds before he finally spotted the ring.


Then even he started laughing.


Karen was already halfway across the room.


"LET ME SEE IT."


"Karen."


"LET ME SEE IT."


"Karen."


"JANE."


Reluctantly, Jane held out her hand.


Karen gasped dramatically.


"Oh my God."


Another gasp.


"Oh my God."


A third.


"It's beautiful."


Kelly immediately grabbed Jane's hand next.


"Wow."


Maria leaned in.


"Adam."


A pause.


"Wow."


Jane buried her face in her free hand.


"I hate all of you."


"No you don't."

Maria replied.


"Fair."


Karen was still staring at the ring.


Then suddenly:


"Would you consider a double wedding?"


Jane looked horrified.


"What?"


Karen nodded enthusiastically.


"I could share."


"Karen."


"It would be magical."


"Karen."


"A dream come true."


"Karen."


Karen finally laughed.


"I'm kidding."


A pause.


"Mostly."


Jane groaned.


"One thing at a time."


The women laughed.


Meanwhile, on the other side of the room:


Chris pointed directly at Adam.


"You actually did it."


Adam shrugged.


"I did."


James laughed.


"You really proposed during my wedding weekend."


"In my defense..."

Adam smiled.


"You took fifteen years."


The room erupted.


James pointed at him.


"That is not a defense."


"It felt relevant."


Chris slapped Adam's shoulder.


"Congratulations, brother."


A pause.


"Though I continue to maintain you skipped several important steps."


Adam laughed.


"Several?"


"Many."


James nodded.


"An alarming amount."


Chris pointed dramatically.


"You went from lunch to girlfriend."


Another point.


"From girlfriend to baby."


Another.


"And apparently decided the next logical step was fiancée."


The room erupted again.


Adam shrugged.


"Worked so far."


Even James laughed at that.


Andrew shook his head.


"I hate that he's right."


"So do I."

Chris agreed.


Then he raised his beer.


"Seriously though."


A smile appeared.


"I'm happy for you."


The room quieted slightly.


Because this time he meant it.


Adam smiled.


"Thanks."


Across the room, Jane looked up from the women surrounding her.


Her eyes found Adam immediately.


She gave him a look.


A very specific look.


The one that clearly said:


You did this to me.


I am not sure I like you right now.


Then came the eye roll.


The eye roll he loved more than almost anything.


Adam just smiled.


Because despite all her complaining...


Despite all the protesting...


Despite all the attention she hated...


She looked happy.


Really happy.


And he knew that look too.


Maybe they didn't get forever in the traditional way.


Maybe their future was uncertain.


Maybe he didn't know how much time he had.


But none of that mattered tonight.


Because he had learned something about Jane McFly.


She collected moments.


The important ones.


The ones that stayed.


The ones people carried long after everything else changed.


So if he couldn't promise forever...


Then he would make sure she had memories.


Beautiful memories.


The kind that lasted a lifetime.


The kind she would never forget.


And looking around the lodge at their friends laughing around the fire, at Karen glowing before her wedding day, at Andrew and Kelly sitting together, at James finally getting his happy ending...


And most importantly at Jane smiling with a ring on her finger...


Adam thought that maybe this was exactly the sort of memory worth keeping forever.
