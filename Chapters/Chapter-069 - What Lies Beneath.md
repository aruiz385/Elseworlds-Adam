# Chapter 69: What Lies Beneath


Jane barely remembered walking to Dr. Ryan's office.


One second she was standing in the hallway.


The next she was sitting on the familiar couch.


Staring at the floor.


Trying to breathe.


Trying and failing.


Dr. Ryan sat across from her.


Patiently waiting.


Finally:


"Kirby knows."


The words came out broken.


Small.


Fragile.


Dr. Ryan tilted her head.


"Tell me what happened."


Jane did.


Every word.


The daycare.


The encounter.


The comment.


The way Kirby had looked at her.


The certainty in his voice.


The devastation she felt afterward.


By the time she finished she was crying.


Again.


Somehow.


She'd started the morning glowing.


Happy.


Light.


After the best night she'd had in months.


And now she felt like she couldn't breathe.


Dr. Ryan listened.


Then leaned forward.


"Jane."


Another.


"Maybe it's time to tell him the truth."


Jane immediately shook her head.


"No."


Another.


"He's not ready."


Dr. Ryan was quiet.


Then:


"Are you sure?"


Jane blinked.


"What?"


"Are you sure Kirby isn't ready?"


Another.


"Because from everything you've told me..."


Another.


"It sounds like he already figured it out."


The words landed hard.


Jane looked away.


Dr. Ryan continued.


"I'm a mother."


Another.


"And one thing I've learned is that motherhood is hard to hide."


Another.


"Particularly from someone who knows you."


Jane closed her eyes.


Because that part felt true.


Painfully true.


Dr. Ryan smiled gently.


"Let me ask you something."


Another.


"What scares you most?"


Jane immediately answered.


"Hurting him."


Dr. Ryan nodded.


"Okay."


Another.


"And what happens if you don't tell him?"


Jane froze.


Because she hadn't thought that far.


Dr. Ryan continued.


"He already knows about Lizzy."


Another.


"He already knows there is a life you aren't showing him."


Another.


"What exactly are you protecting him from now?"


Jane couldn't answer.


Because she suddenly wasn't sure.


Dr. Ryan waited.


Then asked:


"Is it possible that Kirby is more prepared for this conversation than you are?"


The question hurt.


Because it felt true.


And Jane hated it.


An hour later she left Dr. Ryan's office feeling worse.


And better.


At the same time.


Waiting


When Jane returned to Kirby's room...


He was waiting.


Sitting in a chair beside the window.


Another chair positioned across from him.


As though he'd been expecting her.


There was a folder sitting on the table beside him.


Jane immediately noticed it.


But said nothing.


"Hey."


Her voice cracked.


Kirby looked up.


Immediately concerned.


"Hey."


Another.


"Are you okay?"


Jane nodded.


Even though the answer was obviously no.


Kirby pointed to the empty chair.


"Sit."


Another.


"I think we need to talk."


Jane's stomach dropped.


Slowly she sat down.


Then pointed toward the folder.


"What's that?"


Kirby glanced at it.


Then back at her.


"First things first."


Another.


"Do you want to tell me what's happening?"


The room became silent.


Jane stared at him.


Unable to speak.


Kirby smiled sadly.


"I promise you."


Another.


"Nothing you can tell me is going to surprise me."


Still she couldn't do it.


Couldn't find the words.


Eventually Kirby nodded.


"I figured."


He took a breath.


Then started.


"Andrew and I have been talking a lot."


Another.


"At first I noticed something."


Another.


"Every time I asked you about your life..."


Another.


"You talked about everybody except yourself."


Jane looked down.


Kirby continued.


"That bothered me."


Another.


"So I asked Andrew."


Another.


"He hesitated."


Another.


"But eventually he told me what the first few years after the accident were like."


His voice softened.


"He told me about your grief."


Another.


"The Jane Watch."


Jane immediately started crying.


Because she'd forgotten that name.


Or tried to.


Kirby reached for her hand.


"He told me how bad it got."


Another.


"I'm sorry."


Another.


"So incredibly sorry."


Jane shook her head.


"I wanted to die with you."


The confession hung in the room.


Heavy.


Kirby's eyes filled.


Immediately.


"Well."


Another.


"I'm glad you didn't."


Another.


"That would've been awkward."


Jane laughed through her tears.


"What?"


Kirby shrugged.


"If you'd done that..."


Another.


"You would've been alone."


Another.


"Because I wasn't actually dead."


A small laugh escaped her.


The joke working exactly as intended.


Then Kirby continued.


"Andrew told me about the baby."


Jane closed her eyes.


Immediately.


Pain.


Still there.


After all these years.


"No parent should lose a child."


Kirby said softly.


Another.


"No mother should lose a baby."


Another.


"I'm sorry."


He squeezed her hand.


"He told me about you and Andrew."


Jane looked up.


Kirby shrugged.


"It made sense."


Another.


"Honestly?"


Another.


"If I imagined you with anybody after me..."


Another.


"It would've been Andrew."


Another.


"I always thought the two of you were endgame."


Jane laughed weakly.


Kirby smiled.


"I was rooting for you long before you and I got together."


The room fell quiet.


"What Andrew won't tell me..."


Kirby continued.


"...is what happened the last few years."


Another.


"How your life turned out."


Another.


"Who you became."


Another.


"Who you loved."


He looked directly at her.


"Jane."


Another.


"I never would've wanted you to stop living."


Another.


"I hoped you found happiness."


Another.


"I hoped you found someone."


Another.


"I hoped you built a life."


Another.


"I would've hated knowing you spent eight years just surviving."


Jane was openly sobbing now.


Kirby moved his chair closer.


"Thank you."


She looked up.


Confused.


"For what?"


"For taking care of me."


Another.


"For being here."


Another.


"For sitting beside me every night."


Another.


"For loving me enough to do that."


His voice broke.


"You really lived up to your vows."


Another.


"You've been my wife where it mattered."


Jane covered her face.


Crying harder.


"But..."


Kirby continued gently.


"I'm observant."


Another.


"I became a lawyer for a reason."


Another.


"When I met Lizzy..."


Another.


"It clicked."


Jane froze.


"I saw your parents with her."


Another.


"I saw Hayle trying very hard to distract me."


Another.


"I saw how protective Andrew was."


Another.


"I saw the way you looked at her."


Another.


"That's your daughter."


Jane let out a shaky breath.


"I wish you hadn't felt like you had to hide her from me."


"I'm sorry."


The words came immediately.


"I just wanted to protect your recovery."


Another.


"I wanted you to be okay first."


Kirby nodded.


"I know."


Another.


"And I am okay."


Then he smiled.


"And then there was Lizzy's dad."


Jane immediately looked away.


Kirby laughed.


"Do you want to tell me?"


Another.


"Or should I?"


When she said nothing he continued.


"I thought it was Andrew."


Jane blinked.


"What?"


Kirby nodded.


"At first."


Another.


"Made perfect sense to me."


Another.


"Then I got to know Adam."


Another.


"And there's one thing I've learned about Adam Carter."


Another.


"The man doesn't lie."


Another.


"He redirects."


Another.


"He omits."


Another.


"But he doesn't lie."


Kirby smiled.


"I listened carefully."


Another.


"Every story."


Another.


"Every detail."


Another.


"And when he described Lizzy's mom..."


Another.


"I knew."


Jane stared at him.


"He loves you."


Kirby said simply.


Another.


"That part was obvious."


He laughed softly.


"And this morning?"


Another.


"The ring story confirmed it."


Another.


"When you threw that tissue box at Andrew..."


Another.


"I could suddenly picture you throwing the ring at Adam."


Despite everything...


Jane laughed.


Just a little.


Then Kirby reached toward the chain around her neck.


The one holding the engagement ring.


"I saw that too."


Jane stared at him.


"You knew?"


"I saw you take it off the chain before you went home."


Another.


"And put it back on your hand."


She felt her heart stop.


"Why didn't you say anything?"


Kirby looked at the folder.


Then picked it up.


"Because I needed to be sure."


Another.


"I was sure a week ago."


He handed it to her.


Jane opened it.


And froze.


Divorce papers.


Signed.


Already signed.


Tears immediately filled her eyes.


"Kirby..."


He shook his head.


"Jane."


Another.


"You deserve to go home."


Another.


"To your family."


Another.


"To the man you built a life with."


Another.


"To your daughter."


Another.


"To your future."


He smiled.


"I saw how happy you were this morning."


Another.


"That told me everything."


Jane couldn't breathe.


Kirby laughed softly.


Honestly.


"It's an insane love story."


Another.


"But a pretty epic one."


Then he pulled her into a hug.


"I don't want you to feel guilty."


Another.


"I don't."


Another.


"We had our story."


Another.


"It was a good one."


Another.


"But it wasn't supposed to be forever."


His own tears were falling now.


"I'm happy you lived your life."


Another.


"I'm happy you found your family."


Jane clung to him.


Terrified of letting go.


Terrified of losing this version of her life forever.


"Thank you."


She whispered.


Another.


"I love you."


Another.


"I always will."


Kirby smiled.


"I know."


Then he hesitated.


"There's one more thing."


Jane looked up.


"What?"


Kirby's smile disappeared.


"Kelly left Andrew."


Jane blinked.


"What?"


Kirby looked down.


The pain obvious.


And suddenly Jane understood.


A memory.


A courtyard.


Four people.


A choice.


Kirby's voice cracked.


"That day."


Another.


"I should've stayed with Kelly."


Another.


"I should've let Andrew go after you."


Jane's eyes widened.


Understanding flooding in.


All at once.


"Oh."


Kirby nodded.


Tears in his eyes.


"He needs you."


Another.


"I know it's not fair to ask."


Another.


"But please."


Another.


"Don't leave him alone."


Jane squeezed his hands.


Immediately.


"I won't."


Another.


"Ever."


Kirby smiled.


Relieved.


And Jane stood.


Still crying.


Still shaking.


But moving.


For the first time since she walked into the room.


Purposefully.


Toward the door.


Toward Andrew.


Running before she even realized she'd started.
