# Chapter 75: Full Circle (Part 2)


When Jane and Andrew entered Kirby's room, they found exactly what they'd expected.


Kirby.


Adam.


Lizzy.


And absolutely no peace.


Kirby was sitting up in bed with Lizzy happily perched in his lap.


Somewhere over the last five weeks he'd fallen completely in love with his niece.


The feeling appeared mutual.


Lizzy had one tiny hand wrapped around his hospital bracelet and seemed determined to keep it forever.


The second she saw Jane, her entire face lit up.


She kicked her legs excitedly and reached toward her mother.


Jane immediately melted.


"Hi sweetheart."


She leaned down and kissed the top of Lizzy's head.


"I missed you too."


Across the room, Kirby pointed dramatically at a pudding cup sitting on his tray.


"Tell your mother hospital pudding is underrated."


Jane rolled her eyes.


"No."


Lizzy immediately reached for the spoon.


Adam laughed.


"Looks like she disagrees too."


Kirby looked down at the baby.


"Really?"


Another.


"I thought we were on the same team."


The room laughed.


Before he could continue defending hospital pudding, the door opened.


Scott walked in.


Looking entirely too pleased with himself.


Which immediately made Jane suspicious.


And beside him stood a woman.


The room went quiet.


Blonde hair brushed just past her shoulders.


Bright blue eyes.


An easy smile.


Beautiful without seeming particularly aware of it.


The kind of person who put everyone at ease the moment she walked into a room.


She looked around.


Then froze.


Her eyes landed on Andrew.


"Oh my God."


Before anyone could react, she crossed the room.


And threw her arms around him.


"Andrew Call."


Andrew laughed immediately.


A genuine laugh.


The kind Jane hadn't heard in days.


"Ally."


Dr. Ally Miller pulled back and looked him over.


"You got old."


Andrew looked horrified.


"I'm thirty-three."


Ally nodded.


"Exactly."


Another.


"My point stands."


The room laughed.


Then Kirby's eyes widened.


"I know you!"


Everyone looked at him.


Kirby pointed dramatically.


"Ally Miller."


Ally immediately laughed.


"Kirby Keyger."


Kirby grinned.


"Wow."


Another.


"It's really good to see you."


He pointed toward himself.


"What?"


Another.


"I suddenly don't feel so alone."


The room laughed.


Ally smiled.


"I'm not sure that's a good thing."


"Maybe not."


Kirby admitted.


Then he pointed at her.


"Happy to have you back, Ally."


Another.


"Let's just hope your return isn't half as traumatic as mine."


Jane laughed.


Scott sighed.


Andrew rolled his eyes.


Adam smiled into his coffee.


Ally laughed.


"That's a pretty high bar."


"You'd be surprised."


Kirby replied.


Adam looked between Kirby, Andrew and Ally.


Then raised an eyebrow.


"I sense history here."


Andrew groaned immediately.


"No."


Adam pointed at him.


"Oh, definitely."


Kirby grinned.


"You're absolutely getting the story later."


Andrew closed his eyes.


"Kirby."


"What?"


Kirby looked completely innocent.


Another.


"The man deserves context."


Adam nodded.


"I appreciate that."


"You would."


Jane laughed.


Then she looked at Ally and waved a hand toward Kirby.


"Don't pay him any mind."


Another.


"Kirby's certifiable."


Kirby looked offended.


Jane winked at him.


"And somehow becoming more so every day."


Ally laughed.


"Good to know."


"It's really not."


Andrew muttered.


Which made the room laugh again.


Finally Ally turned toward Jane.


"And you must be Jane."


Jane blinked.


"You know who I am?"


Ally laughed.


"Oh, absolutely."


Jane groaned.


"Oh no."


Ally smiled.


"When Andrew and I first met, all I heard about was Jane."


Andrew buried his face in his hands.


Again.


Ally ignored him.


"'Jane, stop writing. I'm mad at her.'"


Jane immediately laughed.


Ally continued.


"'Jane was my best friend. I miss her.'"


Andrew looked like he wanted the floor to swallow him whole.


Ally wasn't done.


"'Jane would've thought that was funny.'"


Another.


"'Jane would've loved this.'"


Jane looked over at Andrew.


His face had gone completely red.


Ally smiled.


"You came up a lot."


"Apparently."


Jane said softly.


"Constantly."


Ally corrected.


And despite herself, Jane felt her heart squeeze.


Ally held out her hand.


"I'm Ally."


"Jane."


They shook.


Then Ally smiled.


"So how exactly are you connected to all of this?"


The room immediately got quiet.


Jane looked at Kirby.


Then Adam.


Then Andrew.


Then laughed.


"Well..."


Another laugh escaped.


"I'm married to Kirby, who came back to life after eight years."


Ally blinked.


"Okay..."


"I'm engaged to Adam."


Another blink.


"Okay..."


"And I dated Andrew for eight years before that."


Silence.


Ally stared.


Then looked around the room.


Then at Scott.


Then back at Jane.


Jane pointed helpfully.


"My entire romantic history happens to be standing in this room."


The room exploded.


Ally nearly doubled over laughing.


"That is the wildest introduction I've ever heard."


Adam grinned.


"You get used to it."


Then Ally noticed Lizzy.


Immediately.


Her entire expression softened.


"Oh my goodness."


She looked at the little girl.


Then back at Jane.


"Who is this beautiful gorgeous girl?"


Jane smiled.


"This is Lizzy."


Ally looked at Kirby.


Then Andrew.


Then Adam.


Then back at Lizzy.


Clearly trying to do the math.


Jane immediately started laughing.


Adam came to the rescue.


"Lizzy would be Jane's and my daughter."


Another beat.


"We may have skipped a step or two but we're getting there."


Then he winked at Jane.


Which only made her laugh harder.


Ally pointed.


"No."


Another.


"I refuse."


Another.


"This cannot be a real conversation."


Kirby nodded.


"It is."


Jane smiled at Lizzy.


"Say hi to Ally, sweetheart."


Lizzy studied Ally carefully for a moment.


Then rewarded her with a huge gummy smile.


Ally immediately melted.


"Oh, that isn't fair."


Adam laughed.


"She weaponizes the smile."


Andrew smiled proudly.


"My goddaughter."


Scott finally stepped forward.


"Now that everyone has finished turning this into a social event."


The room laughed.


Scott pointed toward Ally.


"For those of you who haven't figured it out yet, Dr. Ally Miller is the attending neurologist who'll be overseeing Kirby's neurological care."


Ally smiled.


"Please call me Ally."


Scott ignored that entirely.


"She's one of the reasons we're optimistic about getting him home sooner rather than later."


Kirby immediately pointed.


"See?"


Another.


"I told you I liked her."


Scott then gestured toward Andrew.


"And you've already reacquainted yourself with Dr. Andrew Call."


Another.


"One of our attending cardiologists."


Ally looked at Andrew.


"That sounds considerably more impressive than Andrew."


"It isn't."


Andrew replied.


The room laughed.


Scott pointed toward Adam.


"And this is Dr. Adam Carter."


Another.


"One of our attending oncologists."


Ally shook Adam's hand.


"It's nice to meet you."


"You too."


Adam smiled.


Scott nodded.


"You'll all be working together from time to time."


Another.


"Complex patients don't always fit neatly into one specialty."


Another.


"A case like Kirby's is a perfect example."


Another.


"Neurology."


Another.


"Cardiology."


Another.


"And sometimes oncology."


Adam groaned.


Scott smiled.


"My point is you'll be consulting with each other."


Another.


"In the ICU."


Another.


"In the ER."


Another.


"And on patients who need more than one specialty involved in their care."


Ally nodded.


"So I should probably start collecting phone numbers."


Andrew laughed.


"That's usually how it starts."


Scott looked back at her.


"We're very happy to have you here."


Another.


"Welcome to the team."


Ally smiled warmly.


"Thank you."


Then she glanced around the room.


"If this group is any indication of what I'm getting myself into..."


Another.


"I probably should've negotiated hazard pay."


The room erupted.


Kirby immediately pointed at her.


"See?"


Another.


"I definitely like her."


A few minutes later, Ally looked back toward Jane.


"You know, I just moved here."


Jane perked up immediately.


"Really?"


Ally nodded.


"I know practically nobody."


Then she smiled.


"And I was hoping we could be friends."


Jane's face lit up.


"I'd love to get together sometime."


Another.


"Show you around. Go to brunch."


She pointed at Lizzy.


"She'd love that too."


Ally smiled.


"I'd love that."


Then Jane's grin turned mischievous.


"Also, I can't wait to get some dirt on the mysterious three or four years of Andrew's life that I missed."


Andrew groaned immediately.


"Jane."


"I need stories."


Another.


"Good stories."


Another.


"Embarrassing stories."


Ally laughed.


"I think I can help with that."


Andrew pointed at her.


"You are absolutely not helping."


"Too late."


Jane pulled out her phone.


"Perfect."


Another.


"Let's exchange numbers."


Ally immediately pulled out hers.


Andrew looked at Kirby.


"This feels dangerous."


Kirby nodded solemnly.


"Extremely."


Another.


"We're outnumbered now."


Scott smiled.


"Good."


Adam just laughed as Jane and Ally exchanged numbers and started talking like they'd known each other for years.


Then Jane's phone rang.


Everyone stopped.


She looked down.


James.


Immediately she answered.


"James?"


A pause.


Then her eyes widened.


"Right now?"


Across the room Andrew immediately straightened.


Jane lowered the phone.


"Karen's in labor."


The room instantly erupted.


Adam laughed.


Kirby cheered.


Scott smiled.


Ally blinked.


"What?"


Jane pointed toward the door.


"Our best friends are having their baby."


Then she pointed at Andrew.


"And we're the godparents."


Ally's eyes widened.


"Seriously?"


"Seriously."


Andrew was already moving toward the door.


Jane laughed.


"You know James."


Ally nodded.


"Of course I know James."


"Karen's his wife."


Jane explained.


"And apparently Henry has decided today is the day."


Ally laughed.


"Oh my God."


"We have to go."


Before following Andrew, Jane crossed to Kirby's bedside.


She squeezed his hand.


"I'll come tell you everything later."


Kirby smiled.


"You'd better."


Another.


"And if that kid isn't cute, I'm suing."


Jane laughed and kissed his forehead.


Then she crossed to Adam.


He stood just in time for her to pull him into a quick kiss.


"See you soon."


"You definitely will."


He smiled.


Jane kissed the top of Lizzy's head.


"See you soon, sweetheart."


Lizzy immediately grabbed a fistful of her hair.


Jane laughed.


"Yep."


Another.


"Definitely my kid."


Then she turned toward Scott.


Her godfather opened his arms without a word.


Jane stepped into the hug immediately.


"Love you."


Scott kissed the top of her head.


"Love you too, kid."


Another.


"Go meet your godson."


Jane smiled.


Then looked back toward Ally.


"We're definitely finishing this conversation later."


Ally laughed.


"Absolutely."


Before leaving, Andrew stopped in front of Ally.


For a moment neither spoke.


Then he smiled.


Really smiled.


"It's good to see you."


Ally's smile softened.


"You too."


Andrew pulled her into a quick hug.


"I'll definitely see you tomorrow."


Ally nodded.


"You'd better."


Another.


"I have fifteen years of updates to collect."


Andrew laughed.


To Jane's enormous amusement, he actually blushed.


She immediately filed that away for future teasing.


Then she grabbed his arm.


"Come on."


And together they hurried out of the room.


Toward Labor and Delivery.


Toward James.


Toward Karen.


Toward Henry.


Just like they always had.


Running toward the people they loved.
