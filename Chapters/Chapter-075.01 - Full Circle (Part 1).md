# Chapter 75: Full Circle (Part 1)

Jane stepped out of Dr. Ryan's office feeling lighter.

Not better.

Not healed.

Just lighter.

The last two days had felt like someone had ripped open every wound she'd spent years stitching together.

At least now she had somewhere to put all of it.

Dr. Ryan had helped.

Not enough.

But enough.

Jane checked the time and headed toward Kirby's room.

She had promised she'd stop by.

She almost made it there before she spotted someone standing near the windows overlooking the courtyard.

Andrew.

He looked like he'd been waiting.

When he saw her, he straightened.

"Hey."

Jane stopped.

"Hey."

The silence wasn't uncomfortable.

Just careful.

Finally Andrew took a breath.

"I owe you an apology."

Jane folded her arms.

"Andrew..."

"No."

He shook his head.

"Please."

Something in his voice made her stop.

So she nodded.

Andrew stared out the window for a second.

Then looked back at her.

"I talked to Adam this morning."

A small smile appeared.

"Of course you did."

Andrew laughed.

Then the humor faded.

"I think he helped me understand why what I said hurt you."

Jane stayed quiet.

Listening.

"I wasn't trying to push you out of my life."

His voice was honest.

Raw.

"There isn't a version of my life where you're not in it."

The tightness in Jane's chest eased slightly.

Andrew continued.

"I think I've spent the last week trying to understand why losing Kelly hit me so hard."

A beat.

"When we broke up..."

Jane looked down immediately.

"You told me Kelly could give me things you couldn't."

He laughed softly.

Without humor.

"And for years I convinced myself that was what I was grieving."

Jane didn't interrupt.

"I thought I wanted marriage."

Another.

"I thought I wanted children."

Another.

"I thought I wanted a family."

His jaw tightened.

"But eventually I realized that wasn't true."

Jane looked up.

Andrew met her eyes.

"I think I only ever wanted those things with you."

The words landed heavily between them.

Heartbreaking.

Not romantic.

Just sad.

Andrew immediately continued.

"It doesn't mean I want your life to be different."

Another.

"It doesn't mean I wish things were different with Adam."

Another.

"Because I don't."

His voice softened.

"I love Adam."

Another.

"I love Lizzy."

Another.

"I love You.”

Another.

"I love what you've built."

Another.

"I'm genuinely happy you found that life."

Jane felt tears gathering.

"Kelly was supposed to be proof that you were right."

Andrew smiled sadly.

"She was supposed to be proof that there was somebody else I could build that life with."

Another.

"And we never got there because I didn't really want it."

Another.

"At least not with anyone else but you."

Silence.

"I care about Kelly."

He said quietly.

"I really did."

Another.

"But I don't think I loved her the way she deserved."

Jane nodded slowly.

Then stepped a little closer.

"Andrew, I understand this more than you think I do"

Andrew frowned.

"You do?"

Jane looked out the window.

Toward memories.

Toward another version of her life.

"There was a time when I thought all of those things belonged to us too."

Andrew's eyes softened.

Jane continued.

"The whole thing. Us together. Married with a family. Kids and even a beagle"

That earned a laugh.

A real one.

Jane smiled.

"See?"

Another.

"I had the whole thing planned."

The smile faded.

"When that changed..."

Another.

"After Kirby died and we lost our baby, I stop wanting those things..."

Her voice softened.

"I actually grieved it when we broke up freshmen year of college when you went to UMASS but I still hoped that somehow, some day we would find our way back to each other. When that didn’t happen, I grieved that future over again."

Andrew stared at her.

Listening.

Really listening.

Jane took a slow breath.

"You know, for a long time I honestly believed you didn't choose me."

Andrew immediately looked down.

Not because she was wrong.

Because she'd found the exact wound.

Jane continued.

"Not because I thought you didn't love me."

Another.

"I knew you loved me."

Another.

"I've always known that."

Another.

"But loving someone and choosing them aren't always the same thing."

Andrew swallowed hard.

"When I came back to Hill Dale..."

Another.

"When we found our way back to each other..."

Another.

"I kept thinking maybe someday you'd choose me."

Her voice was gentle.

Not angry.

Just honest.

"And forty-eight hours before my wedding..."

She laughed softly.

Without humor.

"I was terrified."

Andrew looked at her.

Jane met his eyes.

"I was afraid of marrying Kirby not because I didn’t love him but because I knew I still loved you. I suppose I always have and maybe always will to some extent. The beauty of first love is that they never quite go away, especially if that love is with someone who has been in your life since you were kids. I loved Kirby so much. We had an amazing life together. I wouldn’t ever trade the 6 years we had together."

Another.

"But I knew I’ve always loved you. And even then, I still saw that possibility of a future with you.”

Another.

"I was terrified of losing you. Losing that future."

The admission hung between them.

Raw.

Truthful.

"I think part of me still believed that if you asked..."

Another.

"If you told me not to do it..."

Another.

"If you told me to leave it all behind and go with you..."

She smiled sadly.

"I would've."

The silence that followed felt enormous.

Andrew closed his eyes briefly.

Then shook his head.

"Jane."

Another.

"I think about that night sometimes too."

She looked up.

Andrew met her eyes.

"And I need you to know something."

Jane waited.

"It wasn't that I didn't choose you."

Her breath caught.

Andrew continued.

"There were moments I wanted to."

Another.

"God, there was a moment that night that I wanted to steal you."

Jane laughed through tears.

Despite herself.

Andrew smiled sadly.

"I'm serious."

Another.

"I thought about it."

Another.

"Taking you somewhere."

Another.

"Marrying you myself."

Another.

"Running away with you."

Jane stared at him.

Because she believed every word.

Andrew sighed.

"But I couldn't."

Another.

"Not because I didn't love you."

Another.

"Not because I didn't want you."

His voice softened.

"But because I genuinely believed Kirby could make you happier than I could."

Tears immediately filled Jane's eyes.

Andrew continued.

"I was a cop."

Another.

"Trying to get into medical school."

Another.

"Then trying to survive medical school."

He laughed.

Without humor.

"I had no idea what my future looked like."

Another.

"No stability."

Another.

"No certainty."

He looked at her.

"And Kirby was Kirby."

Another.

"Successful."

Another.

"Stable."

Another.

"Ready."

Another.

"He could give you things I didn't think I could."

The hallway fell quiet.

"I was terrified I wasn't enough."

Jane covered her mouth.

Andrew shook his head.

"So I stepped away."

Another.

"Because I loved you."

Another.

"And because I loved him."

Another.

"And because I genuinely thought the two of you would be happier together. You had been until I came back."

For a moment neither spoke.

Then Jane nodded.

"I know that now."

Andrew looked relieved.

Just a little.

Jane smiled sadly.

"But back then?"

Another.

"I wouldn't have understood."

Another.

"Even if you'd said every one of those words."

Andrew laughed softly.

"Probably not."

"Definitely not."

That earned another smile.

Jane squeezed his hand.

"The irony of all of it is that you still spent the next three years taking care of me anyway."

Andrew laughed.

Because she wasn't wrong.

Kirby died.

Everything fell apart.

And somehow Andrew was still there.

Every day.

Every crisis.

Every terrible anniversary.

Every panic attack.

Every setback.

Jane shook her head.

"You never really stepped away."

His smile softened.

"No."

Another.

"I guess I didn't."

Silence settled between them.

Comfortable.

Familiar.

Then Jane looked down.

And her voice became quieter.

More fragile.

"Honestly..."

Another.

"There's still a part of me that doesn't know what would've happened."

Andrew frowned.

"What do you mean?"

Jane's eyes filled.

"Our baby."

The words hung heavily between them.

Another life.

Another future.

Another impossible question.

Jane shook her head.

"I honestly don't know what would've happened if we'd had the chance to meet him. Losing him changed everything because that future that we both could see really disappeared the day we lost our son."

Andrew's eyes immediately filled.

He stepped forward, understanding the grief not only for their son but for the future they should have had.

Andrew wrapped his arms around her.

And kissed the top of her head.

Gently.

The way he always had.


The way he always would.

After a moment he spoke.

Softly.

"Maybe we will leave that one for another day. Please forgive me, I am still here. I am not pushing you away. I need you in my life. I always have. I always will."

Jane nodded against his shoulder.

"I think that's probably wise. I forgive you. Of course I do. I need you in my life too, Andrew. I couldn’t be without you in my life in some way shape or form. Don’t forget how important you are. To me, to Adam, to Lizzy, to Kirby, to Jame and Karen and God help us to Chris! You are not alone. I’ll never let you be alone. You are stuck with us whether you like it or not!"

Andrew smiles slightly.

“I know, I know. Adam made sure I knew that”

Andrew laughs again because Jane and Adam were more in sync than ever.

Eventually he stepped back and offered her his arm.

"Come on."

Jane slipped her arm through his.

"Let's go see your brother."

And together they started down the hallway.

Toward Kirby.
