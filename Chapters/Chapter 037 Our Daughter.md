# Chapter 37: Our Daughter

Jane woke slowly.


Everything hurt.


Her head.


Her stomach.


Her entire body.


The medications made everything feel distant.


Fuzzy.


Like she was floating somewhere between sleep and reality.


For a moment she couldn't remember where she was.


Then she turned her head.


And saw the bassinet.


Lizzy.


Sleeping peacefully beside her.


Jane immediately smiled.


The sight stole her breath.


Tiny.


Perfect.


Real.


Her daughter.


Their daughter.


Jane felt tears fill her eyes.


Then she noticed something else.


Adam.


His head rested beside her bed.


One hand still holding hers.


Fast asleep.


She stared at him quietly.


Remembering.


The operating room.


The pain.


The fear.


The last moments before she passed out.


For a second she'd thought she'd seen her Adam standing beside her.


She'd heard his voice.


Felt his hand.


Seen that look.


But now she knew better.


That was impossible.


Adam stirred.


Slowly waking.


He blinked.


Saw her looking at him.


Immediately sat up.


"Hey."


His voice was sleepy.


Relieved.


"You're awake."


Jane smiled weakly.


"Looks like it."


Adam glanced toward the bassinet.


Then back at her.


"Would you like to hold your daughter?"


The question nearly made her cry.


"I would."


A shaky laugh escaped her.


"I'm just not sure I can."


"That's okay."


Adam stood immediately.


One of the nurses noticed and walked over.


Together they carefully lifted Lizzy from the bassinet.


Then gently placed her against Jane's chest.


The moment Lizzy settled against her...


Jane broke.


Tears poured down her face.


"Oh my God."


Her voice cracked.


"Oh my God."


She couldn't stop staring.


Tiny fingers.


Tiny cheeks.


Tiny breaths.


Real.


She was real.


The medications.


The pain.


The fear.


Everything disappeared.


Because for one perfect moment...


The only thing that existed was Lizzy.


Adam stood quietly nearby.


Watching.


Watching the woman he loved.


Watching his daughter.


And for the first time since waking up three years too late...


Everything felt right.


Eventually the nurse returned.


"We need to take her for some routine monitoring."


Jane nodded reluctantly.


And kissed Lizzy's forehead.


Before the nurse takes Lizzy away:


Jane couldn't stop crying.


Neither could Adam.


Lizzy shifted slightly against her chest.


Making a small sound.


Jane laughed through her tears.


"She's real."


Adam smiled.


"She is."


Another tear slid down Jane's face.


"I spent nine months imagining this."


Another.


"And somehow she's even better."


Adam looked down at Lizzy.


Then back at Jane.


"Yeah."


Another.


"She is."


For a moment neither spoke.


Just watching her.


Their daughter.


Finally Jane looked up.


And caught him staring.


"What?"


Adam shook his head.


"Nothing."


Jane immediately narrowed her eyes.


"That's a lie."


A small laugh escaped him.


"Maybe."


A pause.


"You were incredible."


Jane rolled her eyes immediately.


"I screamed at a resident."


"He deserved it."


"I threatened a nurse."


"He survived."


That earned a laugh.


Then Adam grew serious.


"No."


Another.


"I mean it."


His eyes drifted to Lizzy.


Then back to Jane.


"You were incredible."


Jane suddenly found it difficult to look at him.


Because she wasn't used to being seen that way by him.


Not this Adam.


Or maybe...


That wasn't entirely true anymore.


The nurse appeared.


"Time for some monitoring."


Jane immediately frowned.


"I just got her."


"I know."


The nurse smiled.


"You'll get her right back."


Reluctantly Jane let Lizzy go.


And watched her disappear from the room.


As if part of her heart had been carried away too.


"Hey."


Scott crossed the room immediately.


"Where's my god-granddaughter?"


He hugged Jane carefully.


Then smiled.


"Honey, I called your parents."


Another.


"They're on their way."


Jane nodded.


Then glanced toward Adam.


Suddenly realizing.


He'd never met them.


Not really.


Andrew immediately launched himself toward her.


Much more carefully than usual.


But still very Andrew.


"Hey, Mom."


He squeezed her hand.


"I am so proud of you."


Another.


"Congratulations."


Jane laughed through fresh tears.


"Thank you."


Adam sat quietly watching.


Looking at the two men who loved Jane most.


Each differently.


Each unconditionally.


Andrew suddenly stood.


Walked over to Adam.


And pulled him into a hug.


"Hey."


A grin appeared.


"You did good."


Another.


"You made it."


Adam laughed.


Relieved.


"Thanks."


Their eyes met briefly.


And Adam knew exactly which conversation Andrew was referring to.


The one in the physician lounge.


The one where Andrew chose to step aside.


The one where Andrew made sure Adam knew he had a place in his daughter's life.


"Thanks for the talk."


Adam said quietly.


Andrew nodded.


Any other day they would've argued about feelings.


Today wasn't that day.


Scott was next.


He pulled Adam into a hug.


No hesitation.


No uncertainty.


"Congratulations, kid."


Another.


"You're going to be a great dad."


The words hit harder than Adam expected.


He could only nod.


Andrew sat back down beside Jane.


Smiling mischievously.


"I hope you're ready."


Jane immediately groaned.


"For what?"


"The cavalry."


Another.


"They've been texting me every fifteen minutes."


Another.


"They're coming."


Jane laughed.


"Oh no."


Two minutes later the nurses returned with Lizzy.


Perfect timing.


Scott immediately held out his arms.


"I was here first."


Andrew rolled his eyes.


Scott ignored him.


"You get here first."


Another.


"You get exclusive baby access."


The room laughed.


The tension disappeared.


For a little while.


Then the door opened again.


And Karen practically charged into the room.


"WHERE IS MY GODDAUGHTER?"


James followed behind her.


Looking resigned.


And carrying more balloons.


Jane immediately started laughing.


Karen hugged her.


Then immediately stole Lizzy from Andrew.


"I found her."


Another.


"Perfect."


Another.


"I'm taking her home."


"No."


Five people answered simultaneously.


Karen ignored all of them.


Then Jane noticed something.


The look.


The look on James's face.


The one he couldn't quite hide.


She exchanged a glance with Andrew.


Who had noticed it too.


"Karen."


Jane's voice was suspicious.


Karen immediately became fascinated by Lizzy.


"Karen."


Another.


"Honey."


Karen sighed.


Slowly looked up.


James rubbed the back of his neck.


And suddenly Jane understood.


"Oh my God."


Karen smiled sheepishly.


Then nodded.


Jane immediately burst into happy tears.


"Congratulations!"


Karen laughed.


"Well."


She glanced down at Lizzy.


"I figured I should get some practice."


James finally smiled.


And suddenly everyone was hugging everyone.


Adam included.


The room became louder.


Happier.


Warmer.


Not long after, Chris, Maria, and Kelly arrived.


The fight over who got to hold Lizzy became immediate.


And intense.


Andrew attempted crowd control.


Failed.


Spectacularly.


Several hours passed like that.


Laughter.


Stories.


Pictures.


Visitors.


Love.


Eventually people began leaving.


One by one.


Promising to return tomorrow.


Until finally the room was quiet again.


Just Adam.


Jane.


And Lizzy.


The silence felt different now.


Not awkward.


Just tired.


Jane attempted to feed Lizzy.


And immediately struggled.


The frustration hit harder than she expected.


Everything hurt.


Nothing seemed natural.


Nothing seemed easy.


Eventually she looked ready to cry.


The nurse gently suggested supplementing with formula.


Jane looked devastated.


Before she could say anything Adam spoke.


"It's okay."


Jane looked at him.


"It's really okay."


Another.


"She's going to be okay."


The reassurance helped.


A little.


After that Jane grew quiet.


Very quiet.


Adam stayed beside her.


Helping when she needed it.


Backing off when she didn't.


Never pushing.


Never leaving.


Several times she tried to tell him he didn't need to stay.


Every time he simply shook his head.


And she stopped arguing.


Because she was too tired.


Because Lizzy was finally here.


And because part of her wasn't entirely sure she wanted him to leave.


As the room darkened and Lizzy slept peacefully beside them...


Jane found herself wondering about everything he'd said before her water broke.


The integration.


The memories.


The future.


The things he promised he would explain.


One day.


Not tonight.


Tonight was for healing.


For resting.


For Lizzy.


And for the first time in a very long time...


Jane closed her eyes.


Knowing she wasn't carrying everything alone anymore.


A few hours later the room was finally quiet.


The friends had gone home.


The balloons remained.


The flowers remained.


Lizzy slept peacefully in her bassinet.


And for the first time all day...


It was just the three of them.


Jane watched Lizzy sleep for a while.


Then looked over at Adam.


He sat in the chair beside her bed.


Exhausted.


His hair was a mess.


Dark circles sat beneath his eyes.


He looked like someone who hadn't slept in days.


Which honestly wasn't very far from the truth.


"Adam."


He looked up immediately.


"Yeah?"


Jane hesitated.


Then sighed.


"We should probably talk about my parents."


Adam immediately understood.


His expression shifted.


Not dramatically.


Just enough.


Enough for her to notice.


"You've never met them."


Adam nodded.


"No."


Another.


"I haven't."


Jane looked down at her hands.


"This whole situation is confusing enough."


Another.


"I never told them about..."


She stopped.


The word caught in her throat.


She still hated saying it.


Adam understood anyway.


"The DID."


Jane nodded.


Silence settled between them.


Adam leaned back slightly.


Thinking.


Then:


"What do you want to do, Jane?"


The question surprised her.


"What do you mean?"


"I mean..."


He shrugged.


"I can step out."


Jane immediately stared at him.


Adam looked away.


Toward the bassinet.


Toward Lizzy.


Anywhere but at her.


"In your original plan..."


His voice was quiet.


"I wasn't supposed to be here anyway."


There was no accusation.


No anger.


Just hurt.


And somehow that felt worse.


Jane felt tears immediately fill her eyes.


Because she could see it.


Could see exactly how much that statement had cost him.


Adam stood.


Slowly.


Carefully.


As though already preparing himself.


"I'll step away."


Another.


"Just let me know when they get here."


Another.


"It'll make things easier."


Jane didn't answer.


Couldn't.


Because suddenly he looked defeated.


Not devastated.


Not angry.


Just...


Tired.


So incredibly tired.


Like someone who had spent months fighting for a place in his own life.


Only to assume he would lose it anyway.


Her Adam would never have done this.


The realization came immediately.


Her Adam would've sat beside her.


Taken her hand.


Smiled at her parents.


Introduced himself.


Made everyone feel comfortable before anyone else had the chance to worry.


She wouldn't have had to think about it.


Wouldn't have had to choose.


Wouldn't have had to carry it.


But this Adam was different.


This Adam worried.


This Adam questioned whether he belonged.


This Adam would quietly step aside if he thought it made things easier for her.


Even if it broke his heart.


Jane watched him move toward the door.


And suddenly felt something twist painfully inside her chest.


Because after everything he'd done.


After driving her to the hospital.


After staying beside her through labor.


After holding her hand during the surgery.


After being the first person to meet Lizzy.


After refusing to leave her side.


He still somehow believed he was the complication.


"Adam."


He stopped.


Turned.


"What?"


Jane opened her mouth.


Then closed it again.


Because for once she genuinely didn't know what to say.


She looked at him.


At the exhausted man standing in the doorway.


At the father of her daughter.


And realized she wasn't sure what she wanted anymore.


Only that watching him walk away felt wrong.


Very wrong.


So instead of answering...


Jane simply looked away.


And Adam nodded.


As though her silence had given him all the answer he needed.


Which somehow made her feel even worse.
