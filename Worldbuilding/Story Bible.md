Elseworlds: Adam — Story Bible

A comprehensive reference document capturing the core worldbuilding, characters, timelines, rules, and thematic architecture of your novel. This bible is designed to live inside your GitHub /Worldbuilding/ folder and serve as the authoritative guide for continuity across all 80+ chapters.

1. Core Premise

A trauma‑intimate, character‑driven contemporary drama exploring identity, grief, memory, and chosen family. The story follows Jane McFly, a woman rebuilding her life after catastrophic loss; Dr. Adam Carter, a physician living with dissociative identity disorder; and the impossible return of Kirby Keyger, Jane’s husband believed dead for years. The narrative blends psychological realism, emotional intimacy, and slow‑burn relational healing.

2. Themes

Survival vs. Living — Jane’s arc from numb endurance to active healing.

Identity & Integration — Adam’s DID and the merging of memories, selves, and past lives.

Grief & Rebirth — miscarriage, Kirby’s death, Lizzy’s birth, and the return of the past.

Chosen Family — the community that sustains Jane and Adam.

Forgiveness & Accountability — confronting past choices, secrets, and emotional debts.

Love After Loss — rebuilding intimacy without erasing history.

3. Character Profiles

Jane McFly

Role: Protagonist

Core Traits: resilient, emotionally guarded, guilt‑ridden, fiercely loyal

Backstory Highlights: childhood intertwined with Andrew & Kirby; slept with Andrew two days before marrying Kirby; Kirby murdered 48 hours after wedding; miscarriage two months later.

Arc: From survival to healing; from guilt to acceptance; from fear of love to choosing Adam.

Key Relationships: Adam (partner), Lizzy (daughter), Andrew (childhood friend/ex), Kirby (husband), Scott (friend), Dr. Ryan (therapist).

Dr. Adam Carter

Role: Co‑protagonist

Core Traits: empathetic, steady, self‑sacrificing, quietly intense

Backstory Highlights: Lives with DID; alternate identity “Other Adam” lived three years Jane doesn’t remember; integration therapy returns memories.

Arc: From fractured identity to integrated self; from fear of abandonment to chosen family.

Key Relationships: Jane, Lizzy, Other Adam (internal), Scott, Dr. Cohen, Dr. Ryan.

The Other Adam

Role: Integrated identity / past self

Core Traits: protective, blunt, deeply loving

Backstory Highlights: Recorded videos documenting three years of life with Jane; built a nursery; planned a future.

Arc: Memories merge into Adam; influence persists in mannerisms and emotional instincts.

Kirby Keyger

Role: Husband believed dead; returned from coma

Core Traits: perceptive, gentle, emotionally intelligent

Backstory Highlights: Murdered (believed); discovered alive as a coma patient; DNA confirmed.

Arc: Reclaiming identity; confronting Jane’s new life; navigating grief and gratitude.

Andrew Call

Role: Childhood friend; emotional anchor

Core Traits: loyal, conflicted, protective

Backstory Highlights: slept with Jane before her wedding; lost a baby with Jane; breakup with Kelly.

Arc: Healing old wounds; redefining his place in Jane’s life; potential romance with Dr. Ally Miller.

Elizabeth “Lizzy” Carter

Role: Jane & Adam’s newborn daughter

Symbolism: rebirth, hope, continuity

Supporting Cast

Time & Memory
