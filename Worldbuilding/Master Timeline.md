Elseworlds: Adam — Master Timeline
I. Before the Story (Childhood → Early Adulthood)
Jane, Andrew, and Kirby grow up together — inseparable trio.

Jane & Andrew sleep together two days before Jane’s wedding.

Jane marries Kirby.

Kirby is murdered 48 hours after the wedding.

Jane miscarries two months later.

Jane enters a years‑long survival mode: couch sleeping, rotating friends, emotional shutdown.

II. The Numb Years (Pre‑Chapter 1)
Jane’s life becomes mechanical: work → home → collapse.

Friends (Scott, Andrew, Melissa, Karen, Marty) rotate to keep her functioning.

Jane begins therapy with Dr. Ryan.

Jane joins group therapy, reluctantly.

Jane meets Dr. Adam Carter, who openly shares he has DID.

III. Jane & Adam’s Early Connection
Weekly therapy → coffee → long conversations.

Adam becomes Jane’s emotional anchor.

Jane begins to reclaim small pieces of herself.

Adam’s DID becomes a lived reality in the narrative.

Adam suffers a traumatic patient loss → relapse.

The Other Adam appears, in person.

Jane meets the alternate identity for the first time.

IV. The Revelation of the Lost Years
Other Adam reveals videos documenting three years of life Jane doesn’t remember.

Videos show:

A nursery

Domestic routines

A planned future

A deep relationship between Jane & Other Adam

Adam chooses not to take a job in Oakland.

Adam begins integration therapy to reclaim memories.

V. The Pregnancy Arc
Jane discovers she is pregnant.

Adam commits to staying.

They choose the name Elizabeth “Lizzy” Carter.

Community rallies around them.

Jane and Adam’s relationship deepens into an untitled but functional partnership.

VI. Labor, Birth, and the New Family
Jane’s water breaks → Adam drives her to the hospital.

Emergency surgery.

Lizzy is born.

A nurse calls Adam “Dad” — a defining moment.

Adam refuses to leave Jane’s side.

Postpartum blur: monitoring, visitors, exhaustion.

Adam takes over night shifts, bottles, diapers, logistics.

VII. Integration & Identity Slippage
Integration therapy returns full memories of Other Adam’s life.

Adam begins slipping into mannerisms and emotional instincts of Other Adam.

Jane struggles to tell which version of Adam she’s with.

Adam puts Jane’s heart‑shaped ring back on her finger — symbolically loaded.

Domestic routines stabilize their bond.

VIII. The New Normal (Early Postpartum)
Jane and Adam function as a couple without naming it.

Therapy continues:

Dr. Ryan (individual)

Dr. Cohen (couples)

Jane admits she loves Adam but fears losing the life she rebuilt.

Adam promises: “One day at a time.”

IX. The Impossible Return — Kirby Lives
A transferred coma patient arrives.

Nurse Hayle recognizes him.

Andrew confirms it: Kirby Keyger.

DNA matches.

Jane is legally responsible for medical decisions.

Kirby’s first awakening is brief and disoriented.

Second awakening: he recognizes Jane and Scott.

Kirby begins noticing:

Jane’s absences

Lizzy

Adam

The life she built without him

X. The Emotional Fallout
Jane is terrified of betraying her past or losing her present.

Adam fears abandonment but stays steady.

Andrew is pulled into the crisis.

Andrew & Jane confront their shared history (lost baby, breakup).

Andrew breaks up with Kelly.

Kirby had signed divorce papers (with Kelly’s help).

Jane has not filed them.

Jane’s guilt intensifies.

XI. Community Response
Scott, Melissa, Marty, Karen, James, Jonathan rally around the crisis.

Karen & James have baby Henry — symbolic counterpoint to Lizzy.

Dr. Ally Miller arrives as Kirby’s neurologist.

Ally reconnects with Andrew → new romantic subplot begins.

XII. The Present Status (Where the Story Currently Stands)
Jane & Adam are functioning as a committed couple.

Lizzy is healthy and thriving.

Adam is progressing through integration therapy.

Kirby is awake, recovering, and slowly learning the truth.

Andrew is stabilizing and exploring new emotional territory with Ally.

Jane is preparing to sign the divorce papers.

Jane & Adam plan to visit Adam’s family.

The family chooses to focus on small certainties while navigating the impossible situation.

XIII. Future Arcs (Foreshadowed by Current Events)
Kirby’s full emotional reckoning.

Jane’s final decision about the divorce.

Adam’s complete integration.

Andrew & Ally’s relationship.

Lizzy’s developmental milestones as emotional anchors.

Jane confronting the videos and memories directly.

The community adapting to a blended, complicated family structure.
